**Short answer: about 3,000 things, if you count everything the game tracks.** That figure is PowerPyx's count of the in-game Progress database, and it includes vehicles, weapons and animals. You don't need all of them for 100%.

## Where to track them

Open the map and go to **Progress**. Some categories stay hidden until you find the first item in them. Playtested says the full 100% checklist shows up under Progress after you finish the story.

## The main collectibles

| Collectible | Total | Needed for 100% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collectible cards | 480 in 18 sets | 15 sets |
| Cocaine flamingos | 125 | 100 |
| Underwater discoveries (Kenton's shipwrecks) | 33 | All 33 |
| Magazines, books and comics | 27, plus 7 book swaps | 30 of the 34 |
| Meditation spots | 18 | 15 |
| WAiNK scans (landmarks, curiosities, sculptures, No Flusher tags) | 112 | 100 |
| Styles (outfits, tattoos, watches) | 79 | 70 |
| Unique radio playlists | 2 | 2 |

You scan the WAiNK spots with the WAiNK app on your phone. Push Square's guide hub also lists all 18 card sets by name, from the Vice City Flamingos to Backyard Wrestling.

## Activities that count too

PowerPyx's list also includes 397 stunts (300 needed), 100 basketball hoops (90 needed), 42 drone time trials, 42 shooting range gold medals, 15 of Bret's hunting target decks and 80 challenges (70 needed). It also has discovery lists: 277 base-model vehicles (200 needed), 66 standard weapons (50 needed) and 255 animals (100 needed).

## Can you miss any?

Playtested says nothing you need for 100% is permanently missable, citing PowerPyx and GTABase. You can finish the rest in free roam after the story; see [playing on after the ending](/posts/2026-11-26-gta-6-keep-playing-after-ending). A few single items are in places that close, like two cards at the Starlet Motel after Chapter 2. Since 100% only needs part of each type, missing those doesn't matter.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar hasn't published collectible counts. The in-game Progress menu is the only official tracker.
- **Reported:** every number here is a guide writer's count from playing the game, mostly from PowerPyx, with Push Square and GTABase agreeing on most of them. Push Square says its guide is still a work in progress, and Playtested marks a few figures, such as the 15 meditation spots needed, as unconfirmed. Lists from other sites with very different totals haven't been confirmed.
