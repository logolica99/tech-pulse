**Short answer: you have two options. Pay the Scooter Bros phone app to retrieve the car, or go to the police impound lot, take it, and lose the cops. Either way, a registered car that gets impounded is not gone for good.**

## Where did my car go?

Guides say the police can take your personal car if you get busted with it or leave it behind near a crime. It goes to a police impound lot, and Gameta says the map shows which one: the car is marked on the map, and the map index's **Personal** tab lists it under Impounded Vehicles.

Guides have found lots in several places, including Key Lento, Hamlet, Port Gellhorn and, according to Game8, beside Vice Beach Police in Vice City.

## Option 1: pay Scooter Bros

1. Press Up on the D-pad and open the Scooter Bros app.
2. Your impounded cars are in their own list. Pick the car and choose to retrieve it.
3. Pay from your BuckMe bank account.

The price is a $100 base rate plus a retrieval charge that changes. How To Video Game was quoted $604 and later $550 for the same car. Tech4Gamers warns that bags stashed in the trunk can go missing when you pay for delivery. Guides disagree on where the car ends up: How To Video Game says it was parked near them, while Gameta says it goes to one of your garages.

Scooter Bros unlocks in the Chapter 2 mission Moses (see [how to call your car with Scooter Bros](/posts/2026-11-22-gta-6-summon-car-scooter-bros)).

## Option 2: take it back yourself

This costs nothing but a wanted level.

1. Set the impounded car as your destination on the map.
2. At the lot, break the gate open (How To Video Game used a Square prompt on PS5) or climb over the fence.
3. Get in and drive out. Expect the police: guides report one or two wanted stars.
4. Lose them (see [how to lose the cops](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-wanted-level-lose-cops)). Gameta suggests a respray afterwards so the car isn't recognized.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar has not published a guide to impounds. The lot signs, app menus and prices come from the game itself, as reported by people who played it.
- **Agreed across guides:** impounded cars are marked on the map, Scooter Bros can retrieve them for a fee, and you can drive them out of the lot yourself at the cost of a wanted level.
- **Where guides disagree or vary:** retrieval prices, where a retrieved car is left, and how many stars a break-out gives you.
