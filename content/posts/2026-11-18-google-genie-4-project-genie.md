Google DeepMind and Google Labs are giving eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers their first hands-on access to Genie 4, the newest version of the world model behind Project Genie, the experimental prototype for generating and exploring interactive worlds in a browser.

## What Genie 4 adds

Google calls Genie 4 its most realistic and interactive world model so far. The headline change is that users can type new prompts while moving through a world and edit it in real time, such as changing the weather, adding a character, or redirecting the action. It is also the first Genie model to generate real-time native audio alongside the visuals, from ambient sound to character dialogue.

## Genie 3 gets upgrades too

Genie 3 stays in the model picker for longer runs. According to Google, sessions now last up to three minutes per generation, up from 60 seconds, worlds can be chained together, new worlds can be seeded from 360-degree panorama images, and in-world events can be triggered with natural-language prompts.

## Agent and gallery

A new Project Genie agent turns a conversational description into a world, orchestrating Google's multimodal models to create the starting visuals and a matching soundtrack. Gemini can also join a session to suggest where to go next or trigger events. A community gallery lets people explore, remix, and publish worlds.

Google says all generated video and audio carries SynthID watermarks and that inputs and outputs are safety-screened. It describes both Genie models as frontier research with open problems in camera latency, complex physics, audio precision, and long-term scene consistency. Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist.
