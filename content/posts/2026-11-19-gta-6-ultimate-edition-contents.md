**Short answer: in-game extras, not more story.** The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition costs $99.99, $20 more than the $79.99 Standard Edition. It adds vehicles, weapons, outfits, two side activities and five shops you can only use in this edition. The items are spread across Jason and Lucia's story, so they unlock as you play rather than all at once.

## What you get

This is the list on Sony's and Microsoft's store pages, in short:

- **Vehicles:** a '95 Grotti Cheetah sports car, a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest Kayak at Jason's safehouse, a Shitzu Squalo boat with a crate of explosives, and a '67 Vapid Dominator off-road buggy with its own garage (Paradise Garage in Watson Bay).
- **Custom build:** the Ganado Retro Build, exclusive mods for Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup.
- **Weapons:** the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, a matching pair for Jason and Lucia, plus engraved versions of Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17 pistols.
- **Looks:** Vice City Style outfits, hairstyles and tattoos, and Goodtime Gear clothing themed on the in-game TV character Macca the Gator.
- **Activities:** the PTT Youngin$ Compound, a raid on a Southside Vice City gang's base for special items and contraband, and the Classic Car Collection, a side quest for the fixer Wyman to find and restore abandoned classic cars.
- **Exclusive shops:** Rideout Customs (car mods), Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305 (streetwear), Electric Fang Tattoo (over 50 tattoos designed by the art collective FAILE) and One-Eyed Willie's (off-road mods).

## When does it unlock?

Sony's store page says new items are "uncovered behind each chapter." GamesRadar+ played through and mapped it out: most shops, the gang compound raid and Jason's pistol arrive in Chapter 1; the revolvers, Lucia's pistol, the Dominator buggy and the classic car quest in Chapter 2; the outfits and the Squalo boat in Chapter 4; and the Cheetah in Chapter 5. It also reports that the Ganado Retro Build can only be used during Chapter 1.

## Can you upgrade from the Standard Edition?

Yes. Both stores sell an Ultimate Edition Upgrade for Standard owners. Microsoft's Xbox store lists it at $20.00. The PlayStation Store has an upgrade page too, and Sony's support pages say upgrades for digital and boxed Standard copies are sold only through PlayStation Store.

## Is the Vintage Vice City Pack part of it?

No, that is a separate bonus for every edition. Take-Two says all purchases made before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier and a Shore Court garage, Vice City outfits and hairstyles, and a tropical weapon pattern. Digital pre-orders also came with a free month of GTA+.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Take-Two, Sony and Microsoft stores):** the $79.99 and $99.99 prices, the full item list, the $20 Xbox upgrade price and the Vintage Vice City Pack deadline.
- **Reported:** the chapter in which each item unlocks comes from GamesRadar+'s playthrough, not from Rockstar.
