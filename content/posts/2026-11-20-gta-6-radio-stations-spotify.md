**Short answer: yes. Since November 20, 2026, Spotify has a dedicated Grand Theft Auto VI section with playlists for more than 20 of the game's radio stations and mixtapes.** Spotify says it opened at 9 a.m. ET (6 a.m. PT) in 33 markets.

## What's in it

Spotify's announcement lists:

- **The Grand Theft Auto VI Official Playlist**, with songs from the game's soundtrack, score, trailers and other key moments. It includes new music from artists such as Dua Lipa, Playboi Carti and Lana Del Rey, plus earlier singles from Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen and Tove Lo.
- **More than 20 radio station and mixtape playlists.** Spotify names Cocoteo FM (hosted by Bad Bunny and RaiNao), Flash FM (Robyn and Alex Consani, back from Vice City), Back Country Radio (Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey), Stockyard FM (PinkPantheress and The Dare) and Vice City Rap Radio (JT).
- **A look back at the series.** A "GTA Series Museum" picks one well-known song from each game, and "Iconic Radios of Past GTAs" brings back eight classic stations, including Emotion 98.3, K-DST, Vladivostok FM and FlyLo FM.
- **Personal mixes.** There are GTA Mixes for GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV and GTA V. The Grand Theft Auto Radio station, built from songs across the series, now includes GTA VI music. There is also a shelf of GTA podcasts.

## Is every in-game song there?

Not necessarily, at least not yet. Spotify says the station playlists "will fill out as fans play through the game" and that the section will keep growing over the coming months as Rockstar releases more music. The hub is at spotify.link/grandtheftauto.

If you want the 34 original songs made for the game, see our post on [GTA VI: The Album](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-soundtrack-album).

## Official or not?

This is official. It comes from Spotify's own newsroom announcement of November 20, 2026, covering a hub built around Rockstar's game. The announcement does not list which countries make up the 33 markets.
