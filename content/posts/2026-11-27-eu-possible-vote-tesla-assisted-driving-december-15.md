Tesla's driver-assistance software could get a formal up-or-down decision across the European Union next month. A draft agenda published by the European Commission for the December 15 meeting of its Technical Committee on Motor Vehicles lists further examination of the Netherlands' request to grant EU type-approval for "Tesla Assisted Driving", "with possible vote".

## Why it matters

Reuters reports this is the first time the Commission has explicitly raised the possibility of a vote. The meeting had earlier been pushed back to December 15.

The Netherlands approved the system first, and Reuters says nearly a dozen countries have followed the Dutch lead. An EU-wide approval would let Tesla offer the system across the bloc rather than country by country.

## The bar

EU-wide approval needs support from at least 15 of the 27 member states, representing 65% of the bloc's population. The agenda says a vote is possible, not guaranteed, so the discussion could still end without a decision.
