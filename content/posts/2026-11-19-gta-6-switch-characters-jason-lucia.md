**Short answer: hold right on the D-pad, then press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. That only works after free switching unlocks, partway through Chapter 2.** Until then, the game picks whether you play as Jason or Lucia.

## When does switching unlock?

Not right away. Guides from Polygon, TheGamer and VGC agree that the early story hands you one character at a time. Lucia is playable in some early missions, but you can't pick her yourself in free roam.

Polygon and TheGamer both say free switching opens after the main mission **Breakfast Run**, about halfway through Chapter 2. TheGamer says the short step right after it, which it calls Coffee Break, introduces switching. Polygon notes it can take 10 hours or more to get there.

## How to switch

- **Switch:** hold right on the D-pad. Character portraits appear in the bottom-right corner. TheGamer says you then press R1 (PlayStation) or RB (Xbox) to swap.
- **Call your partner instead:** the same menu can bring the other character to you. Polygon says they usually turn up within a few seconds.
- **Split up:** hold right again and choose to go your separate ways.

TheGamer says the swap is instant when the two are close. When they are far apart, the camera zooms out over the map, like in GTA 5, and takes a few seconds.

## Why is a character greyed out?

VGC says a greyed-out portrait means that character can't be used right now for a story reason, for example because they are busy elsewhere. It also says that right after a swap, the character you left sometimes calls a RydeMe and drives off, which can briefly grey out the menu.

## Tips

- **Some missions are for one character only.** Polygon says Jason's missions are marked "J" and Lucia's "L". "J/L" means a solo mission you can take with either.
- **The other character keeps living their life.** Leave Lucia at home and you may find her somewhere else hours later.
- **Stats are separate.** Polygon says Jason and Lucia share weapons and vehicles, but each has their own health, stamina and Focus. Pick the stronger one before a big fight.
- **Switching works as a shortcut.** Swap to whoever is already near where you want to go.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** none of this comes from a Rockstar controls guide; we found no official write-up of switching.
- **From the game, as tested by outlets:** when switching unlocks, the buttons, the greyed-out cases and the tips all come from Polygon, TheGamer and VGC, which played the game. They agree on the main points. They word the button press a little differently, so if R1 or RB doesn't work, try picking the portrait while you hold right.
