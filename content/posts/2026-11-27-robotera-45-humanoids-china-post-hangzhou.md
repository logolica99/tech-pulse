Chinese robot maker ROBOTERA says it now has 45 humanoid robots working at China Post's Hangzhou Postal District Center, which it calls the largest humanoid testing deployment in Chinese postal operations so far.

## What the robots do

The humanoids take parcels off conveyor lines, turn them so the barcode faces the right way, and place them on induction platforms that feed China Post's automated sorting system. They are also trained to spot and reject oddly shaped or packed items that the automated sorters can't handle.

## The numbers

According to ROBOTERA's announcement, the robots have been on the floor since April 2026. Throughput started at about 500 to 600 parcels an hour and has climbed to roughly 1,200 parcels an hour during peak testing, with accuracy above 90%. These are company figures from a testing deployment, not independently audited results.

China Post's Hangzhou branch plans to add more automation, including intelligent sorting and security inspection systems.
