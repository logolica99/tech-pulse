**Short answer: no.** GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on every console it is on: PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. There is no 40fps or 60fps performance mode on any of them.

## What Rockstar said

In its November 10 pre-load announcement, Rockstar said the game was built "from the ground up" for current consoles, "targeting 30 frames per second" so it can keep the density and visual detail of Leonida. The extra power of the PS5 Pro goes into image quality instead of frame rate: sharper images through Sony's PSSR upscaler, plus ray-traced shadows that only the Pro version gets.

Rockstar also says the PS5 versions add DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D audio and improved load times. Xbox One owners can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming, which Microsoft says uses Xbox Series S graphics settings.

## How well each console holds 30fps

Digital Foundry tested all four versions after launch:

- **PS5 Pro:** the smoothest. A consistent 30fps through around 75 hours of normal play, with short dips to about 24fps only when blowing up lots of vehicles. Renders at 1440p and upscales to a 4K-like image with PSSR.
- **PS5 and Xbox Series X:** also 1440p, but hold 30fps "a little more loosely", often flicking between 30 and the high 20s in driving and combat. Worst cases dip into the low 20s.
- **Xbox Series S:** the weakest. Renders at 720p, upscaled to about 1080p, and large parts of the game run in the mid to high 20s, with drops to around 20fps in heavy destruction.

Digital Foundry calls PS5 Pro the best version, says base PS5 and Series X still look and play well, and calls Series S the hardest to recommend. It also notes Xbox consoles can use a 120Hz VRR mode at the system level for slightly smoother motion, which PS5 can't do for a 30fps game.

## Will a 60fps patch come?

Nobody knows. Rockstar has not announced any performance mode or frame-rate patch. Any claim that one is coming is a guess until Rockstar says so.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** the 30fps target on all consoles, PSSR and ray-traced shadows on PS5 Pro only, and the PS5 haptics, audio and load-time features.
- **Official (Microsoft):** Xbox Cloud Gaming streams the game with Xbox Series S settings.
- **Independent testing:** the resolutions and real frame rates come from Digital Foundry's analysis, not from Rockstar.
- **Not announced:** a 60fps or 40fps mode, or any patch adding one.
