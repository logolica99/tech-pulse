**Short answer: no. Rockstar hasn't apologized for GTA 6's performance and hasn't announced an update for November 25. The "statement" going around on X and Facebook doesn't appear on any official Rockstar channel, and fact-checkers say the image looks made with a fake-post generator.**

## What the viral post claims

On November 18, hours before launch, the X account @GoofysExe posted that Rockstar had "reportedly apologized" for lag, stuttering and performance problems in GTA 6, and that "a major update is planned for November 25". It came with a screenshot styled like an Instagram post from Rockstar, thanking players for their patience.

Portuguese newspaper Público said the post had passed 600,000 views when it checked on November 19, and that X had added community notes saying it was false. The same screenshot later spread on Facebook, where Portuguese broadcaster SIC found it shared with the caption "They took 13 years to release broken game".

## What fact-checkers found

Público, SIC and Spanish site Escuadrón Geek each checked separately and reached the same verdict: false.

- **Nothing from Rockstar.** No apology or November 25 date on Rockstar's website, Newswire, support site or official social accounts.
- **The image looks fake.** Público and SIC say it copies the look of an Instagram post and appears to come from an online tool that lets anyone make fake social media posts.
- **No coverage.** No major or games outlet reported the supposed statement, which both Público and SIC say would be very unlikely during GTA 6's launch week if it were real.

## Is there any GTA 6 patch at all?

Yes, but only from before launch. GTA 6 got two updates: 01.002.000 on November 13 and 01.002.001 on November 16. Rockstar didn't publish patch notes for either one. Escuadrón Geek notes that patches are normal for a game this size, but none had been announced or dated when it checked.

If a real update comes, it will show up on the Rockstar Newswire and Rockstar Support first.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** No apology, no performance statement and no November 25 update. According to the fact-checks, Rockstar's recent posts were about the launch and its Miami festival, not fixes.
- **Fact-checked as false:** The @GoofysExe post and its screenshot, per Público, SIC Notícias and Escuadrón Geek.
- **Reported, not official:** The two pre-launch update version numbers and dates come from outlets tracking console downloads. Rockstar hasn't published notes for them.
