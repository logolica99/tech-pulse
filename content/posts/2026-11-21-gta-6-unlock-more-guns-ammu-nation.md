**Short answer: keep buying guns. Ammu-Nation has a loyalty system, so every purchase opens up more stock. For heavy weapons, go to one of the bigger stores.** Some guns also need a bank account.

## Buying adds more stock

GamesRadar+ says most Ammu-Nation stores carry only a few guns at the start. The more weapons you buy, the more stock appears. Insider Gaming calls it a loyalty scheme that also unlocks attachments.

Tech4Gamers says progress is shared between stores, so it doesn't matter which one you shop at. Your first purchase comes in the Chapter 1 mission "Gear Up".

## Store size matters

Not every store sells everything. Tech4Gamers says hovering over a gun store on the map shows its stock type: Limited, Standard, Hunting or Expanded. It lists six Expanded stores as the only places that sell sniper rifles, LMGs, grenade launchers and rocket launchers:

- Big Shots (Hamlet, Vice Dale County)
- Woodhatch (Capri, Mariana County)
- Phil's Ammu-Nation (Vice City)
- Black Eagle (northwest of Vice City)
- Iron & Oak (Vice City)
- Prestige Firearms (Ocean Beach, Vice City)

GamesRadar+ also mentions premium stores with a wider range and their own cosmetics.

## Get a bank account

Tech4Gamers says pocket cash only covers purchases up to $999. Anything pricier is paid from your BuckMe account, which you get in the Chapter 1 mission "Buck Me". Until then, those guns are out of reach however much cash you carry.

## Other ways to get guns

- **Side content.** Tech4Gamers says the Shrewsbury SMS-01 sniper rifle only goes on sale after Bret Vogel's ninth hunting job. Those jobs start in Chapter 2.
- **Unique variants.** GamesRadar+ says unique weapon variants turn up around the map, marked by a gold gun icon on the minimap. Once you find one, Jason and Lucia both keep it, but you can't modify it.
- **Shared guns.** A gun bought by either character goes to both, says GamesRadar+, but each one modifies and equips it separately.

## Official or not?

Rockstar hasn't published details of Ammu-Nation's unlock system. This all comes from guides written after playing the game. Tech4Gamers says nobody has confirmed how much you need to buy for each unlock, or which guns are held back by loyalty and which by store size.
