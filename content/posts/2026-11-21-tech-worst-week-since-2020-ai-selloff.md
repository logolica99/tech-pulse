Anthropic's record IPO landed in the middle of a rough week for AI stocks. The tech-heavy Nasdaq fell 10% over five trading days, its worst week since the start of the Covid lockdowns in March 2020, according to CNBC and NPR.

## The numbers

- Nvidia fell 23% for the week, its sharpest weekly drop in 18 years, CNBC reports.
- Anthropic opened at $111.50 in its Thursday debut but closed Friday at $88.91, slipping more than 8% on the day to end near its IPO price.
- NPR reports that the S&P 500 and the Dow both entered a correction by Friday's close, meaning they were 10% below recent highs.

## What investors are worried about

NPR lists several pressures that have been building for weeks: the Federal Reserve's rate increase in September, which makes it costlier for AI companies to borrow for data centers; Democrats taking both chambers of Congress after promising tougher AI rules and a slower data center build-out; and leadership turmoil at Nvidia after CEO Jensen Huang resigned this month.

The open question is whether this is a bubble deflating or a correction. One strategist quoted by NPR expects a slow recovery but notes that the AI trade is led by large, profitable companies, unlike the dot-com era. CNBC notes Anthropic still ended the week with a fully diluted market value of about $1.6 trillion, behind only six US companies.
