**Short answer: not announced.** As of launch day, November 19, 2026, Rockstar Games and Take-Two have not announced any DLC, story expansion or season pass for GTA 6. They have not ruled one out either.

## What Rockstar has said

The closest thing to a hint came from Rockstar's VP of design, Stephen Donkin, in an interview Kotaku published on November 18. Kotaku asked why the map has at least two large casinos that players cannot enter. Donkin said gambling did not feel right for Jason and Lucia's story, given the balance Rockstar wanted in the game's economy.

He then added that "the map has been built for the long term," and that "a few areas on the map" give Rockstar "options in the future should we have a gameplay or narrative need for them." Kotaku says Rockstar gave no more detail. Its reviewer also mentions a zoo, a large graveyard and a small northern town he never visited during the story.

That is a comment about how the map was designed. It does not name an expansion, a date or a price.

## What Take-Two has said

Take-Two's pre-order announcement in June described GTA 6 as "a single-player experience" and listed a Standard and an Ultimate Edition. It said nothing about later story content.

On its November 9 earnings call, CEO Strauss Zelnick said Rockstar had finished developing GTA 6 and would "focus on what comes next," adding: "we'll announce that in the fullness of time." Asked how Take-Two would keep players around after the story, he said only that "you can always keep going" in Grand Theft Auto games. In its prepared remarks, the company also said Rockstar will keep supporting the existing GTA Online with new content and events.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** no GTA 6 DLC or expansion has been announced. Donkin's quote (given to Kotaku) and Zelnick's remarks on the earnings call are on the record, but neither promises add-on content.
- **Speculation:** Kotaku and Engadget read Donkin's comment as a sign that expansions, updates or an online mode could use those areas. That is their interpretation, not a Rockstar plan. Treat any "confirmed GTA 6 DLC" claim you see elsewhere as a rumor until Rockstar announces it.
