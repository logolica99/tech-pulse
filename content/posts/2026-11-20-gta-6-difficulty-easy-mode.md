**Short answer: no. GTA 6 has no Easy, Normal or Hard choice. But two settings, Accessible Combat and Reduced Vehicle Damage, do most of what an easy mode would, and you can turn them on or off at any time.**

## Where to find them

VGC gives the path: open the menu with the start button, go to Settings, then use the bumpers to move to the Gameplay tab. Both toggles are in that list. Push Square files them under the game's accessibility options.

## What they do

- **Accessible Combat:** enemies have less health and do less damage to Jason and Lucia. Push Square calls it the kind of rebalancing you'd expect from a normal difficulty slider.
- **Reduced Vehicle Damage:** your car holds up much better. VGC says several high-speed crashes can still wreck it.

When you switch them on, the game warns that the change can have a big effect on your experience. VGC says this is useful in later missions, which throw more enemies at you.

## Will it make the game too easy?

Push Square tested both and says they do **not** make you invincible. You still need to take cover in gunfights, but the overall challenge is noticeably lower. The idea, it says, is to help newer, tired or disabled players get through without too much friction.

## Do they affect trophies?

Push Square says turning them on does **not** disable trophies. That is different from [cheats](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-cheat-codes), which only unlock after the story. For the full list, see [how many trophies GTA 6 has](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-trophies-achievements).

## Other settings that help

Push Square and VGC list more options in the same menus: aim assist and lock-on settings, button taps you can change to holds, "simplified" minigame controls, several controller layouts, low-vision options, a brightness setting and a high-contrast mode.

## Official or not?

- **In the game, from Rockstar:** the two toggles, their names, where they sit and the warning message are in the game itself, as described by Push Square and VGC. Rockstar has not published how much exactly they change enemy health or damage.
- **Reviewers' testing, not Rockstar:** that the toggles don't make you invincible and don't disable trophies comes from Push Square's own testing. VGC says they can be switched on and off at any time.
