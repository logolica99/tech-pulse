**Short answer: no. GTA 6 is a single-player game with no online mode, so you don't need PlayStation Plus on PS5 or Game Pass on Xbox Series X|S to play it. Once it's downloaded and updated you can play offline. It also isn't included in either subscription, so you have to buy it ($79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate on the PlayStation Store).**

## Why no subscription is needed

PS Plus and Xbox's paid online plans are needed for online multiplayer, and GTA 6 doesn't have any. Sony's listing says outright that "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience" and lists it as "1 player". Meristation makes the same point: with no multiplayer component, there's nothing to pay extra for.

## Can you play GTA 6 offline?

Yes. The PlayStation Store and PlayStation.com both show **"Offline play enabled"** for GTA 6. Sony's standard licence note applies too: you can play it offline on the PS5 that's set as your main console (the **Console Sharing and Offline Play** setting), and on any other PS5 when you sign in with the same account.

You still need internet for a few things:

- downloading the game, which needs up to 212GB of space,
- redeeming a code from a physical copy, since the boxed version is a code, not a disc, and
- installing updates.

## Is GTA 6 on Game Pass or PS Plus?

No. It isn't part of the PS Plus game catalog or Game Pass. Xbox Wire's announcement about streaming GTA 6 says plainly that "Grand Theft Auto VI must be purchased separately."

## The one case where Game Pass matters: Xbox One

GTA 6 doesn't run natively on Xbox One, but you can stream it through **Xbox Cloud Gaming**, using Series S graphics settings. You still need to own the game, plus cloud playtime. Game Pass is one way to get that playtime. Xbox also says that from November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime without a Game Pass subscription. PS4 can't play GTA 6 at all.

## What if GTA 6 gets an online mode later?

Nothing is announced. Meristation and GuideDistrict both expect that if a GTA 6 online mode arrives, it would probably need PS Plus or a paid Xbox plan, as GTA Online does on consoles. That's an expectation, not a Rockstar announcement.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Sony):** the PlayStation Store and PlayStation.com list GTA 6 as a single-player, 1-player game with offline play enabled, at $79.99 (Standard) and $99.99 (Ultimate).
- **Official (Microsoft):** Xbox Wire confirms Xbox One cloud streaming, cloud playtime sold without Game Pass in supported markets, and that the game must be bought separately.
- **Reported, not official:** that GTA 6 isn't in the Game Pass catalog at all comes from media reports summarized by GuideDistrict. That a future online mode would need PS Plus or Game Pass is outlets' expectation, not anything Rockstar has said.
