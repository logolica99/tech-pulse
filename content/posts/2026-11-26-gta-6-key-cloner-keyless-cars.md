**Short answer: keep playing the story until Chapter 3, when Randy gives you the key cloner.** GamesRadar+ and Polygon say it's handed to you during the story mission "Steal the Zorrusso for Randy". There's no way to get it any earlier.

## Why some cars won't start

GTA 6 has keyless cars. GameSpot says it's the first GTA to have them. If you break into one before you have the key cloner, Polygon says you still can't drive it away. So check a car before you smash a window and set off its alarm.

The tools come in this order, according to Polygon and GameSpot:

- **Chapter 1:** you can only smash a window, which may trigger an alarm.
- **Chapter 2:** Moses gives you a **slim jim**. It gets you into cars that only have an alarm without setting it off. You still have to hotwire them.
- **Chapter 3:** Randy gives you the **key cloner** for keyless cars. GameSpot says you don't have to hotwire those once you're in.

## How to unlock it

Polygon lays out the steps:

1. Do the Chapter 3 (Vice City) main missions until you get the "steal the car for Raul" objective. You meet Randy at the end of it. His garage then takes stolen cars, but you don't have the key cloner yet.
2. Keep going with the main missions until you've finished "Crosstown Traffic".
3. Shortly after that, Randy calls and starts "Steal the Zorrusso for Randy". He gives you the key cloner in this mission. GamesRadar+ says he installs it as an app on your phone.

## How to use it

- **Check the car first.** Hold Up on the D-pad to open the WAiNK scanner, or open WAiNK on your phone. Polygon says keyless cars list "key cloner" as their access type.
- **Clone the key.** Go up to a front door and pick "clone key". Rotate the right stick until you find the sweet spot, then hold it there to unlock the car. Polygon says the center circle turns pink. GamesRadar+ says you'll also hear a whir and feel the controller rumble.

Many keyless cars have trackers too. Polygon says a standard tracker only stops you modifying the car until it's registered. Police actively follow an advanced tracker, so you have to keep driving fast.

## Official or not?

Rockstar hasn't published a guide to the key cloner. Everything here comes from Polygon, GamesRadar+ and GameSpot, which describe what happens in the game. Some fan guides name the unlocking mission differently, so go by the Randy phone call and the Zorrusso job in your mission list.
