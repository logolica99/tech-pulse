**Short answer: the Criminal Profile is GTA 6's version of Red Dead Redemption 2's honor system. It tracks how violent you are, mostly how often you kill, and puts Jason and Lucia on one of five levels. You can move up and down between the first four, but the fifth, Irredeemable, is permanent.**

## The five levels

PC Gamer, Polygon and GamesRadar+ list the same five, from cleanest to worst. Each shows as a small devil-face icon:

- **Professional (yellow):** you avoid casualties, knock enemies down instead of killing them, and stay unseen when you can.
- **Hardened (orange):** you kill enemies and don't worry much about being seen, but don't harm people for no reason.
- **Violent (purple):** you regularly kill police and civilians when you don't need to.
- **Psycho (gray):** sustained extreme violence, including killing pets and defenseless animals. PC Gamer says normal hunting doesn't count; GamesRadar+ says it can in some cases.
- **Irredeemable (dark gray or black):** you stayed a Psycho for too long. Polygon says one rampage isn't enough; you have to keep it up for hours.

## Jason and Lucia are tracked separately

Polygon and GamesRadar+ say each character has their own profile, so Jason can be a Psycho while Lucia is still Professional. To check, hold right on the D-pad or open the Jason & Lucia menu.

## What changes with each level

- **Focus:** on Professional or Hardened, hitting non-lethal spots such as hands and knees refills your Focus meter. On Violent or Psycho, headshots do. GamesRadar+ adds that petting dogs and cats also helps while you're Professional.
- **Surrender:** PC Gamer and GamesRadar+ say Psycho and Irredeemable characters can't surrender to the police.
- **The world:** GamesRadar+ says an Irredeemable profile changes how civilians react, makes cops open fire faster, and drives a wedge between Jason and Lucia.

## How to get back to Professional

Polygon says the profile moves slowly in both directions, so one good or bad hour won't flip it. To improve it:

- Don't hit or shoot civilians, and don't run people over on purpose.
- In gunfights, aim for the yellow (non-lethal) weak spots in Focus mode, not the red ones.
- Do clean robberies: come in through a back or side door and avoid being seen.
- If a witness sees you, let them go instead of hurting them.

GamesRadar+ says threatening people doesn't hurt a Professional profile. It also says a Psycho icon with a scratch through it means you're close to Irredeemable, so pull back right away.

## Official or not?

- **Rockstar, in the game:** the five levels and the warning that being Irredeemable "will have a lasting impact on Jason and Lucia" come from the game itself, as quoted by PC Gamer and Polygon.
- **Rockstar's director:** Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told IGN Spain that each character's Criminal Profile matters a lot for which [ending](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-how-many-endings) you get. Exactly how it does is not yet known.
- **Guides, not Rockstar:** the details on what moves you between levels, the Focus bonuses and the tips for recovering come from PC Gamer, Polygon and GamesRadar+ playing the launch version. They don't all agree on small points, such as whether hunting counts.
