Hyundai Motor Group now owns 100% of Boston Dynamics. A third-quarter regulatory filing from Hyundai Glovis, reported by The Korea Herald, shows that all put and call options on Boston Dynamics' common shares were exercised and expired during the quarter.

That closes out SoftBank's remaining 9.65% stake. SoftBank triggered its put option in July under an earlier shareholder agreement, and existing holders, including Executive Chair Chung Euisun, HMG Global and Hyundai Glovis, bought the shares. Hyundai Glovis put in 46.5 billion won (about $34.8 million) to raise its own stake to 12.47%.

## What Hyundai wants to do with it

With no outside shareholders left to answer to, Hyundai can move faster on spending, research and work across its companies. The center of the plan is the Atlas humanoid robot. Hyundai aims to be able to build 30,000 Atlas units a year by 2028 and use them in its own operations.

The first rollout is planned at Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America in Georgia. Atlas will start with simpler jobs like sorting and moving auto parts, then take on more complex assembly once the technology proves itself.

## The money problem

Boston Dynamics is growing but still losing a lot of money. Its revenue for the first nine months of the year rose 29% to 136 billion won, while its net loss widened to 626.6 billion won because of heavy research and production costs. The Korea Herald cites sources saying an IPO could come around 2029 or 2030 once production ramps up, though no official timeline has been set.
