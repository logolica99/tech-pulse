**Short answer: you get rid of a tracker by registering the car, but only once you've unlocked that tracker type.** Pay 'n' Sprays deal with **standard** trackers early in Chapter 3. **Advanced** trackers only come off after you finish Randy's car jobs, which runs into Chapter 4.

## The two trackers

GameSpot says high-end cars can carry one of two trackers. The car icons above the minimap show which one you have:

- **Standard (yellow):** police can trace the car only after you stop and wait a few seconds. Registering the car is the only way to get rid of it.
- **Advanced (red):** the car pings your location to the police if you drive slowly for about five seconds, so you have to keep moving fast. For most of the game, Pay 'n' Sprays won't take these cars. You also can't store one in your garages until it's registered.

The [stolen car guide](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-keep-register-stolen-car) explains how registering works in general.

## Removing a standard tracker

Game Rant says that shortly after Chapter 3 starts, you call Moses about selling cars. He says he has contacts. From then on, Pay 'n' Sprays sell and register cars with standard trackers. Registering one removes its tracker.

## Removing an advanced tracker

This takes a lot longer. Game Rant and Destructoid describe the same order:

1. In Chapter 3, keep playing the story until you meet Randy. He gives you a Wish List of 20 cars to steal. The [key cloner guide](/posts/2026-11-26-gta-6-key-cloner-keyless-cars) covers the tool you need for keyless cars.
2. Deliver cars from the list. Game Rant says to deliver all 20. Destructoid says Randy asks for the Mamba GT after the first 10.
3. In Chapter 4, a few in-game days after a promo-shoot story mission, Randy asks you to steal a Declasse Mamba GT from a set spot. Game Rant calls the mission "Photo/Shoot" and Destructoid calls it "Shoot a Promo".
4. Some time after you deliver it, Randy calls again. Destructoid says it takes 24 in-game hours. He tells you Pay 'n' Sprays can now deal with advanced trackers. From then on, you can sell and register those cars at any Pay 'n' Spray.

Destructoid says you can check this by hovering over a Pay 'n' Spray on the map. The description says the shop handles tracked vehicles. You may need to unregister an old car to free a slot.

## Official or not?

Rockstar hasn't published notes on how trackers work. This guide uses what GameSpot, Game Rant and Destructoid found while playing. Their accounts differ on how many Wish List cars you need and on the mission's name, so those details aren't confirmed.
