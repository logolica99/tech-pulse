Xiaomi is moving its humanoid robots onto real production floors. Speaking at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) CEO Summit in Shenzhen on Nov. 17, founder and CEO Lei Jun said the company's embodied AI robots have begun factory trials, according to Lianhe Zaobao, citing Reuters and Chinese outlets.

## A full shift, with recharging

Lei said the robots can work a full eight-hour shift and can recharge themselves, possibly twice during that time. He framed the trials as part of Xiaomi's broader push into AI-driven smart manufacturing, saying the company has used AI to optimize its factory operations and has built an unmanned factory.

The claim follows an earlier video, reported by STAR Market Daily, in which Lei showed Xiaomi's general-purpose humanoid attempting an eight-hour shift at a car factory for the first time. He said the robot met that workstation's production needs for the whole day, which he described as initial validation that it can handle production-line tasks.

## Waiting for a breakout product

Lei also said he has not yet seen a revolutionary, benchmark-setting product emerge from this wave of AI in the past four years, but expects great products to appear in the next few years as AI moves into the physical world and into devices.

These are Xiaomi's own claims; the company has not published independent performance data from the trials.
