Chinese carmaker GAC launched the third generation of its Magazine battery, sold in China as Danxia Battery 3.0, at the 2026 Guangzhou Auto Show. The first version came out in 2021 and the second in 2023. This one is mostly about physical safety.

## What GAC claims

- **Underbody protection**: seven layers that resist impacts of up to 2,000 joules. China's current standard asks for 150 joules.
- **Tests with CAERI**: GAC ran underbody impacts at 150, 300, 500, 1,000 and 2,000 joules plus a scraping test with the China Automotive Engineering Research Institute.
- **Grenade video**: GAC showed a Type 82-2 hand grenade (62 g of TNT) detonated directly under a pack fitted to a car. The pack did not catch fire, and the car still worked afterwards.
- **Side impacts**: no cell intrusion in a 32 km/h side-pole crash test.
- **Sensors**: elastic-wave sensors under the pack raise an alert after a hard hit, and the system can spot a 2 mm leak within three seconds.

The grenade demonstration is GAC's own video, not a published independent test report. GAC has not said which models will get the pack first.

GAC also said its own charging network now covers 240 cities with 2,564 stations and 33,000 chargers, all 1,000 V DC units.
