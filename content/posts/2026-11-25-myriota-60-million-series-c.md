Adelaide-based Myriota, which runs a satellite network for industrial internet-of-things devices, said on November 24 that it closed the first tranche of its Series C at A$60 million (US$40 million), ahead of schedule.

## Who invested

Existing investors Main Sequence Ventures and Hostplus led the first close together with Australia's National Reconstruction Fund Corporation. Funds SA joined as a new investor. Myriota says this brings its total funding past A$160 million (US$107.5 million), and that the full round should close in early 2027.

## What the money is for

The company plans to grow its satellite constellation and push into new industrial sectors and international markets. Its platform combines low-power devices, satellite connectivity and fleet software, and is used to track mining equipment and trailers and to monitor fuel tanks, livestock water and off-grid solar. CEO Ben Cade also pointed to Myriota's role in Australia's defence industrial base as a provider of secure narrowband communications from space.
