MegaRobo Technologies, based in Suzhou, China, announced on November 18 that it has raised nearly $200 million in a new round. The Oman Investment Authority, Oman's sovereign wealth fund, joined the round, existing backers Asia Investment Capital, Granite Asia and Sinovation Ventures put in more, and CMB International came in as a new investor.

## What MegaRobo builds

The company pitches itself as infrastructure for AI that works in the physical world. Its "One Intelligence Stack" ties together models and agents, data, lab workflows, scientific instruments, robotics and automation, so an AI system can design an experiment, run it in a real lab, observe the result and use it for the next step.

Science, and especially pharmaceutical R&D, is where it is starting. MegaRobo says it has delivered products to more than 500 customers, including drug makers, biotech firms and research institutions. Its Megalaxy Lab service lets researchers and AI agents configure experiments, send them to automated lab systems and get machine-readable results back.

Alongside the raise, the company and partners including Tencent Cloud and Twist Bioscience launched Poreva Open, a global AI protein design challenge in which Megalaxy Lab will wet-lab test more than 10,000 submitted protein sequences.

## The money

MegaRobo says the new funding will go into core technology, global expansion of its AI for Science business, and infrastructure for AI operating in more complex physical settings. By the company's count, it has now raised about $600 million across all its rounds.
