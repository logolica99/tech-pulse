**Short answer: No. In a ballot that closed on November 26, 2026, Rockstar Games workers voted 75.3% against a formal union recognition agreement with the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB). The vote was 1,163 against and 381 in favor, on a 93.2% turnout.**

## What the vote was about

Voting ran from November 12 to November 26, so it covered GTA 6's November 19 launch, The Verge reports. A yes result would have set up a formal framework for collective negotiation with the studio over pay, overtime, bonuses, leave and pensions, plus a right to be consulted on future changes such as AI use and layoffs.

Without it, Rockstar has no obligation to meet union representatives. Workers can still join the IWGB and push for changes informally.

## What Rockstar said

Rockstar head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe said in a statement (reported by The Verge via Bloomberg News, and sent to GamesRadar+): "We are extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we've built together and the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI." She added that the result "reflects our team's strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong."

## What the union said

The IWGB Rockstar Games Workers Union said the result is "not a sign to retreat, it's a sign to double down on what we stand for," and that it will keep pushing for pay transparency, contractual overtime pay, and protections against AI and layoffs. It acknowledged it has "more work to do" to win the trust of more colleagues.

## Background

According to GamesRadar+, a group of Rockstar workers began seeking union recognition in June 2026, and in July Rockstar said it would meet union representatives to discuss voluntary recognition. The Verge notes that Rockstar's firing of 31 IWGB members last year is the subject of a UK employment tribunal case, with a judgment still pending.

## Does this affect the game?

Nothing in the reporting suggests any effect on GTA 6 itself, its patches or its future plans. It is a workplace matter at the studio.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** the vote result and figures come from the ballot as reported by the IWGB and outlets; Rockstar's position comes from Jennifer Kolbe's on-the-record statement.
- **Union's view:** the "keep fighting" statements are the IWGB's own position, not Rockstar's.
- **Pending:** the tribunal case over last year's dismissals has no judgment yet.
