**Short answer: no.** GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Take-Two's launch-day announcement names just those two consoles, and there is no announced PC version or PC release date.

## What Take-Two has said

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has been asked about PC more than once and has not given a date. On the company's November 9 earnings call, when asked about PC and Nintendo Switch 2 versions, he said that "information always comes from our labels", meaning Rockstar, Insider Gaming reports, citing Variety.

On a call the next day, Zelnick said that "generally speaking, the experiences on console and PC tend to be roughly the same," PC Gamer reports. That was a general comment about platforms, not a GTA 6 PC announcement. PC Gamer notes that Take-Two has consistently declined to share any plans for GTA 6 on PC.

## When might it come?

This part is guesswork. Raymond James analyst Andrew Marok told CBS News that Rockstar's PC versions have historically arrived about 12 to 18 months after the console release, though he said it could come sooner this time. Reuters reports that some industry analysts expect the PC launch, and the online mode, in 2027.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC version has been announced.
- **Analyst expectation, not confirmed:** a PC release around 2027, 12 to 18 months after the console launch.
