**Short answer: plan on about 50 to 65 hours just for the story.** That is the range from outlets that played GTA 6 to the credits before launch. Take your time with side content and it is more like 70 to 100 hours. Doing everything is about 200 hours. Rockstar has not published an official length.

## What reviewers clocked

| Playstyle | Hours | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story, as fast as possible | about 50 (40 to 60) | SVG |
| Story-focused run with some extras | about 53 | Polygon |
| Main campaign | 65 or more | GameSpot |
| Story with side content | 70 to 100 | Polygon, SVG, GameSpot |
| 100% completion | about 200 | SVG, GameSpot estimate |

CBS News, citing Insider Gaming, also puts the average time to finish all the missions at roughly 50 hours.

## Why you can't rush it

Both Polygon and SVG say the story can't simply be played back to back. At points you have to earn a certain amount of money or wait for a call before the next story mission opens, so even a story-only run picks up some side activities along the way.

Polygon also reports that Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson said his last full-story playthrough before launch took just over 100 hours, in a run he called "rushed." That comes from a Rockstar developer, but it is one person's playthrough, not an official estimate.

Polygon calls GTA 6 the longest game in the series by a wide margin.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar has not stated a play time.
- **Reported:** every figure above comes from reviewers' own playthroughs, plus one developer anecdote reported by Polygon.
