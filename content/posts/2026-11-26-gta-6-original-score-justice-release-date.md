**Short answer: On December 11, 2026. Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score is composed by the Grammy-winning French duo Justice, and Rockstar says it will be on all major streaming services that day, plus double CD and vinyl.**

If you've finished a few missions and want the music that plays under them, not the radio, this is the album you're waiting for. It isn't out yet.

## What's on it

Rockstar's official music page says the album has **30 tracks**, plus "a number of digital bonus tracks" included across all formats. Justice's label, Because Music, describes the full package as about three hours of music from the game.

Three singles are already out, and Rockstar lists them on the page:

- "Starting from Zero" (feat. Michael McDonald)
- "Flower in a Hurricane" (feat. Tame Impala, SZA, King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard), the song from the launch trailer
- "Pusher" (feat. Gesaffelstein), released October 29 with the date announcement. Rolling Stone says it runs about seven minutes.

## Formats

- Streaming on all major services
- Double CD
- Triple vinyl, including a limited crystal-clear edition (Because Music says there is also a standard black version)

Rockstar's music page links to the CD and vinyl orders.

## How is this different from The Album?

There are two separate GTA 6 records:

- **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album** is the soundtrack of songs by artists like Dua Lipa, Travis Scott and Playboi Carti. It's already out, and we covered it in [What songs are on the GTA 6 soundtrack?](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-soundtrack-album). Rockstar has since added a **Deluxe Edition** with 23 bonus tracks, from artists including The Dare, Slayyyter, Denzel Curry, HEALTH and Overmono, for 57 in total.
- **Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score** is Justice's in-game score. Rockstar calls it an "adaptive score" that is tuned to how you play as you move through Vice City and Leonida. This is the one coming December 11.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** the December 11 date, Justice as the composers, the 30-track count with digital bonus tracks, the streaming, CD and vinyl formats, the three singles, and the 23-track Deluxe Edition of The Album. All of these are on Rockstar's GTA VI music page.
- **From the label and press:** the roughly three-hour length and the standard black vinyl come from Because Music's announcement. The "Pusher" running time is from Rolling Stone.
- Beyond the singles, the full score tracklist hasn't been published yet.
