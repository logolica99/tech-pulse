SatSure, an Earth observation company based in Bengaluru, has raised a $21 million Series B, Via Satellite reports. India's sovereign space-tech fund, the Antariksh Venture Capital Fund (AVCF), led the round.

## The round

According to the company, the $21 million is a mix of $17.6 million in equity, $2.7 million in venture debt and $1.1 million in secondary transactions. AVCF put in about $6.4 million. The fund is anchored by India's space regulator IN-SPACe and managed by SIDBI Venture Capital.

## What the money is for

SatSure plans to use the funds to advance its optical payloads before their first orbital flight in 2027 and for its role as a partner in India's national Earth observation constellation. Its subsidiary KaleidEO builds optical, multispectral satellite payloads; they have been tested from aircraft but have not yet flown in space. SatSure also builds AI and foundation models that turn imagery into insights, so it aims to cover the whole chain from payload to data product.
