**Short answer: no. GTA 6 has no option to replay a story mission once you've finished it. If you want to play one again, you have to load a manual save from before that mission.**

## No replay menu

GTA 5 let you replay missions from the pause menu. GTA 6 doesn't. GamesRadar+, whose reviewer played for more than 100 hours, says there is simply no option for it, and Dexerto's guide says the same.

GamesRadar+ thinks the reason is that GTA 5 used replays for mission skill challenges. In GTA 6, challenges have moved to their own place in the progress menu, are broader, and are mostly used to unlock [cheats](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-cheat-codes) after the story.

## The workaround: manual saves

- **15 manual slots.** Pause the game, open the Game tab and choose Save Game. You can only save in free roam; the option is greyed out during a mission.
- **One autosave.** It saves after each mission and after some open-world events, such as getting killed or arrested, and each new autosave replaces the last one.
- **Save at chapter breaks.** Dexerto suggests starting a new manual slot after each chapter, so you can jump back to different points in the story later.
- **Back up first.** Loading an old manual save can overwrite your autosave, so make a manual save of your current progress before you go back.

For the full how-to, see [how to save in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-how-to-save-game). Without an earlier save, the only other way to replay a mission is to start a new game.

## Official or not?

- **In the game:** the lack of a replay option, the 15 manual slots, the single autosave and the no-saving-during-missions rule are how the game works, as described by GamesRadar+ and Dexerto. Rockstar has not said anything about why replay was left out.
- **Not from Rockstar:** the reason about challenges is GamesRadar+'s own view. GamesRadar+ also thinks replay is unlikely to be added later, but that is a guess, not an announcement.
