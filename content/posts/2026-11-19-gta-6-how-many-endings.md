**Short answer: more than 10, if you count small variations.** GTA 6 has multiple endings. Players and reviewers have confirmed three or four so far, and IGN Spain says Rockstar's Rob Nelson told it the game has more than 10. This post explains what decides your ending without saying what happens in any of them.

## What Rockstar has said

IGN Spain's reviewer says he asked Rob Nelson, whom the outlet describes as the game's director, how many endings there are during the review process. According to IGN Spain, Nelson said there are more than 10. Some differ only in subtle ways, and others are radically different from each other.

Nelson also told IGN Spain that there are no good or bad endings in his view, only different ones. He said each protagonist's Criminal Profile matters a lot, and that the state of Jason and Lucia's relationship has the biggest potential to change the story. If you neglect the relationship, he said, you can reach endings that are very different from the "love" path.

VICE separately reports that Rockstar said there are "a lot more than two" endings, in a Q&A that VICE says was reported by Game8.

## What decides your ending

VICE says it beat the game, unlocked three endings, and ran the finale four times to test it. It lists four main factors:

- **Family Savings.** How much money you put into Jason and Lucia's shared savings account.
- **The relationship.** Whether Jason and Lucia become a couple. The romance is optional.
- **Criminal Profile.** The game tracks what kind of criminal each of them becomes, careful or chaotic.
- **Story choices.** Some missions let you decide what happens to other characters, such as whether someone lives.

GameSpot says it has accounted for at least four endings and has seen two firsthand. In its playthroughs, the best ending came when the couple was deeply in love and the savings account was at its maximum of $2 million. Keeping the relationship at its fullest without maxing out savings gave a good, but not best, ending. GameSpot says it does not yet know how Criminal Profiles feed into the ending.

## Tip if you want to see more than one

IGN Spain's reviewer suggests saving before key story moments so you can go back and choose differently. He says the game makes it fairly clear which missions are important.

## Official or not?

- **Rockstar, as reported:** more than 10 endings, some subtly different and some radically different, with no "good" or "bad" ones (Rob Nelson to IGN Spain). "A lot more than two" (Rockstar Q&A, as reported by VICE via Game8). Rockstar has not published an official count on its own site.
- **Reviewers' findings:** three endings unlocked by VICE, at least four counted by GameSpot, and the four deciding factors listed above. GameSpot's "best ending" conditions are its own finding from playing, not a Rockstar statement.
- **Unknown:** the exact number of endings and exactly how each factor is weighed.
