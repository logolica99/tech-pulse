Lute, a physical AI company started in January 2026, says it has raised $20 million in seed and pre-seed funding. The seed round was co-led by OTB Ventures and FORWARD.one. Credo Ventures and Seedcamp led the earlier pre-seed, and Inovo VC, Borusan Ventures, ff Venture Capital and Naval Ravikant also took part.

## The bet on touch

Most robot models rely mainly on vision. Lute argues that vision tells a robot where things are but not how firmly it's holding something, whether an item is starting to slip, or how fragile it is. So the company is building a family of foundation models trained on tactile input. Working with partners, it designed small elastomer-based touch sensors and built them into a portable rig for collecting data.

## Where it's deployed

The founders, Paweł Budzianowski and Krzysztof Walas, say Lute's models are already running at Europe's largest automotive parts distributor, ten months after the company started. They also say it works with some of the world's largest retailers and is partnering with integrators, starting in health care and manufacturing. On site, the robots work on their own where they can, and remote humans step in when needed. Those interventions go back into training.

Lute says it will spend the money on collecting more data, training its models and deploying with more customers.
