Spotify founder Daniel Ek is backing Europa Fusion, a quiet UK fusion power startup, TechCrunch reports, following an earlier Bloomberg report. The size of his investment has not been disclosed.

## Who's behind it

Europa Fusion was founded in February by Nantas Nardelli, an AI researcher who has worked at Google DeepMind, Facebook AI Research and Microsoft Research Cambridge. Most recently he was head of machine learning at Pacific Fusion, the California company building a pulsed-power fusion demonstration facility in New Mexico.

## A different bet

Like Pacific Fusion, Europa Fusion plans a pulsed-power reactor, which uses huge, precisely timed bursts of electric current to squeeze fuel targets until atoms fuse. The difference is in the method. According to a source cited by TechCrunch, Nardelli thinks fusion startups lean too heavily on building physical machines to test ideas, and wants to put more weight on simulation and data instead.

No company has yet built a fusion plant that sends power to the grid. Nardelli, who says he used to build robots for a living, has written that he left AI work to help fix climate change.
