Voltus announced the Voltus Battery Network on November 16, 2026. The consortium is meant to take a commercial or industrial battery from site selection through daily dispatch.

The San Francisco company aggregates distributed energy resources into wholesale markets. It says the network gives a host site one path for assessment, funding, engineering, permitting, construction, maintenance, optimization, market registration, and operation. Voltus pre-vets the members. The announcement names launch partners including JLL, Scale Microgrids, PowerFlex, and Redaptive, along with dozens of other developers, financiers, and operators.

## Why Voltus says the timing changed

Voltus is explicit about the market it wants. It says commercial and industrial customers use 63 percent of U.S. electricity, that battery prices are down more than 50 percent since 2023, and that flexible capacity is increasingly valuable. In PJM, the grid region serving 13 states and Washington, D.C., the company says capacity prices rose from $29 to $325 per megawatt-day in under four years.

With its members, Voltus says it aims to add 1,000 megawatts of new battery capacity at commercial and industrial sites in PJM by 2030. The network is also open to hosts and members elsewhere in the United States and Canada.

The offer leans on a product Voltus calls Bring Your Own Capacity, a bilateral arrangement it says can lock in long-term revenue for distributed batteries and make projects that previously did not pencil out financeable. Earlier in 2026, Voltus acquired Brightfield AI, which it describes as software for picking and developing commercial battery sites.

Tim Hade, senior vice president of battery energy storage at Voltus, said the network is meant to put that stack of specialists behind one process so a business can add a battery and see the financial case quickly.

The percentages, the capacity-price range, and the 2030 target are Voltus's. The announcement does not list projects already financed, or megawatts already under construction, through the new network.
