**Short answer: this is a Chapter 3 secret, not a story mission. After you move into the Vice City apartment, unlock the bedroom laptop with the password Muffins, Queen and 15, then read the WhatUp message from Unknown. It gives you the code 92665. Enter that on the keypad of a roll-up garage near Auntie's Place on Del Rio Boulevard in La Perle, and you get Vanessa's Growler, a pink Pfister sports car, plus the garage itself.**

## Who is Vanessa?

Vanessa is the previous occupant of the Chapter 3 apartment in Vice City. Guides call that place Boobie's apartment or Bayside Apartments. The car the game hands you is labelled Vanessa's Growler. It isn't a main mission or a map collectible you stumble on in free roam. It's a hidden lockup you unlock with a laptop puzzle and a keypad code.

## How to get the garage code

Do this once you have access to the Chapter 3 apartment, and check the laptop before you move on:

1. In the bedroom, look at the laptop. It's password-protected and asks for three parts.
2. Find the clues around the apartment. Guides describe a framed photo of a dog named **Muffins**, and a notepad or drawing of a dog on a throne or wearing a crown, for **Queen**. Flip that pad over for **15** (a "Class of '15" note, according to GTA BOOM). Guides disagree a little on exactly where the photo sits: GTA BOOM puts it on a small table opposite the kitchen, Game Rant puts it to the right of the kitchen, and PlayStationTrophies mentions a Muffins photo toward the front door.
3. At the laptop, enter **Muffins**, **Queen** and **15**. GTA BOOM says to pick the plural Muffins, not Muffin.
4. Open the WhatUp messages (R1 on PlayStation, RB on Xbox, according to GTA BOOM) and read the conversation from **Unknown**.

PlayStationTrophies quotes the message as telling you to go to La Perle, right next to Auntie's Place, and use **92665**. Once you have the code, Leonipedia says it can also show up in a Notes banner on screen.

Guides don't all agree on whose laptop this is. Game Rant calls the messages Boobie's. GTA BOOM, PlayStationTrophies and Insider Gaming treat the laptop as Vanessa's, left behind by the former tenant.

## Where is the garage?

It's on **Del Rio Boulevard** in **La Perle**, a Vice City district west of Bayside, according to PlayStationTrophies. Look for a small concrete garage with a grey roll-up door. Guides say to find it by the **Auntie's Place** landmark. GTA Xtreme describes Auntie's Place Take Out and a courtyard beside the shop; PlayStationTrophies points to Aunt Zola's Soul Food on the same block and a lockup just north-northwest of it. Game Rant says it's south of La Perle, just to the right of a tattoo parlor. Leonipedia adds Daniel's Auto Parts as another nearby sign.

The keypad is on the wall to the right of the shutter, under bow graffiti and, Leonipedia says, a red "WARNING DO NOT PARK HERE" sign. Type **92665**. The shutter opens, the place is labelled **Del Rio Boulevard Garage**, and stepping inside triggers a notification that Vanessa's Growler is now in your possession.

GTA Xtreme says the door opens with that code once you have the Chapter 3 clue, so get the laptop message first rather than trying the keypad cold.

## What do you get, and is it free?

You get two things: the pink (Leonipedia also says lavender) Pfister Growler, which is a different car from Jason's Growler, and the Del Rio Boulevard Garage as a place to store vehicles. Leonipedia, playing a French-language copy, also found a weapon locker in the back office.

You don't pay to take the car. Whether it's free to keep as a registered personal vehicle is less settled:

- GTA BOOM and Leonipedia both looked at the vehicle sheet and saw no tracker, a **$20,600** sell value and a **$29,450** registration fee. Game8 lists the same registration cost and a **$20,500** sell price. GTA BOOM says opening the garage does not register the car, and suggests checking the offer at Moses.
- Game Rant says you should take it to a Pay 'n' Spray (the article writes "Pan 'n' Spray") to register it for free. Leonipedia and GTA BOOM say they have not confirmed that this fee is waived.

GTA Xtreme also notes that claiming the Growler can send your current personal vehicle to another garage, so check your vehicle list before you leave.

## Don't leave it until later

This can be missed. Insider Gaming warns that if you have already finished Chapter 3, you can no longer complete the laptop puzzle and claim the rewards. Game8 says that once you leave that apartment at the start of Chapter 4, you lose access to the laptop and can no longer unlock the garage if you haven't already. GTA Xtreme similarly says to collect it while you're still in Chapter 3.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar has not published a guide to Vanessa's car, this garage or the laptop password. The code, the car name and the garage label come from the game itself, as reported by people who played it.
- **Agreed across guides:** the Chapter 3 laptop password (Muffins, Queen, 15), the Unknown message, the 92665 keypad code, the La Perle / Del Rio Boulevard location near Auntie's Place, and the reward of Vanessa's Growler plus the garage.
- **Where guides disagree:** whose laptop it is (Boobie's or Vanessa's); whether registration is free at a Pay 'n' Spray or costs $29,450; the sell value ($20,600 vs $20,500); and whether the closest named landmark is Auntie's Place Take Out or Aunt Zola's Soul Food, which are described as being in the same area.
- **Missable warning:** that comes from Insider Gaming, Game8 and GTA Xtreme, not from a Rockstar statement.
