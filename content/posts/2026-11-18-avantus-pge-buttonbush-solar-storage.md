Solar and storage developer Avantus has signed 20-year power purchase agreements with Pacific Gas and Electric for Buttonbush 1A and 1B. These are the first two phases of the Buttonbush Solar Center in western Kern County, California, and the company announced the deals on Nov. 17.

## The numbers

Together, the two phases would add 800 MW of solar generation and 3,200 MWh of battery storage. Avantus says that is enough to power about 381,000 homes a year and calls the agreements among the largest utility-scale solar-and-storage offtake deals in California so far. The batteries are meant to keep solar power flowing into the evening, when demand peaks.

By the company's estimate, the projects are a roughly $3 billion investment and will create about 900 union construction jobs. Construction is expected to start in 2027, with commercial operation in 2029. Avantus plans to own and run both phases as an independent power producer.

## Not final yet

The contracts still need approval from the California Public Utilities Commission. Avantus says the projects will be built on water-stressed farmland. It already runs the nearby Aratina 1 project (200 MW of solar, 500 MWh of storage) and is building Aratina 2.
