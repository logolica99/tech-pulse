**Short answer: click both sticks at the same time. That's L3 + R3 on PS5 and LS + RS on Xbox Series X|S.** Focus is GTA 6's version of Red Dead Redemption 2's Dead Eye and Eagle Eye in one ability, and both Jason and Lucia can use it.

## When you unlock it

Game Rant says Focus isn't available right away. It unlocks in the Chapter 1 main mission **Down to Earth**, which includes a short tutorial. HowToVideoGame says L3 + R3 is only the default: in the PS5 version you can assign Focus to another input under Settings > Gameplay.

## In a gunfight

With a gun out, Focus slows time for a moment so you can line up shots. It runs on a Focus meter that drains while it's on. Game Rant says enemies show weak spots in two colors:

- **Red:** a fatal shot, such as the head or heart.
- **Yellow:** a non-lethal shot that takes someone out of the fight, such as hands or knees.

That matters because GTA 6 tracks how violent you are in your Criminal Profile. If you want a cleaner record, aim for yellow spots.

## Exploring and looting

Outside combat, Red Bull and GAMES.GG say you can keep Focus on as long as you like. It highlights:

- **Green:** cash.
- **Yellow:** things you can interact with, including notes that can hold safe codes.
- **Red on a person:** an enemy, even through walls.
- **Doors:** white means you can go in, red means the door is alarmed.

GAMES.GG and Red Bull say it can also show animal tracks and valuables hidden in a car's trunk. GAMES.GG says it can help you track down the source of nearby shouting, gunshots or sirens.

## Refilling and upgrading Focus

- **Refill:** HowToVideoGame and GAMES.GG say food, drinks and petting dogs and cats give Focus a boost. Game Rant adds certain foods and cigarettes.
- **Upgrade:** Game Rant says Shooting Range Challenges at Rodger's Ranch & Shooting Range in Kelly County, at the northeast edge of Port Gellhorn, raise your Focus. It also lists fishing, golf, pool and good Samaritan jobs. GAMES.GG says Focus gains are permanent.
- **After the story:** HowToVideoGame says an Unlimited Focus cheat becomes available once you finish the story.

## Official or not?

- **Rockstar:** HowToVideoGame quotes Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, comparing Focus to Red Dead Redemption 2's Eagle Eye during a demo. He said it helps you find things on the map, including cops.
- **Guides, not Rockstar:** the button mapping, unlock mission, color meanings, refill items and upgrade spots come from Game Rant, GAMES.GG, HowToVideoGame and Red Bull, based on playing the launch version. Rockstar hasn't published its own Focus guide.
