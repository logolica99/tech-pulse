**Short answer: drive into a mod shop.** The shops open after **Just a Scratch**, a Chapter 1 mission for Joey at Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento. Every shop specializes in one type of vehicle, so take your car to the one that matches it.

## Finding a mod shop

Open the map or the Index, go to **Vehicles** and pick **Mod Shops**. That list shows every shop you've found so far. GamesRadar+ says a blue wrench icon next to a shop's name means it specializes in the vehicle you're in, and that shop has extra parts the others don't stock.

GTA Playbook and GTA6 Land both count **14 mod shops**. Two of them, **Rideout Customs** (donks) and **One-Eyed Willie's** (off-road), come only with the Ultimate Edition, so the Standard Edition has 12. Some examples:

- **Coastal Auto Care**, Key Lento: retro cars (your first shop)
- **Capri Classic**: muscle cars
- **Euroform Automotive**, Little Cuba: luxury cars
- **Vice Auto Customs**, Balsero: tuners
- **Lowballers Customs**, Balsero: lowriders
- **Vice Vinyl**: wraps and liveries
- **Two Wheel Fury Motorsports**: sports bikes
- **Wasteland Off-Road** and **Dirt Merchants**: off-road cars and dirt bikes

Game Rant's list leaves out one shop, Ledford Motorcycle Workshop (choppers and classic motorcycles), which GTA6 Land includes.

## Using the menu

1. Lose the police first. GTA6 Land says a shop won't let you in while officers can see you.
2. Drive in. A damaged car gets repaired before you can add parts.
3. Switch tabs with **L1/R1** on PS5 or **LB/RB** on Xbox: **Pro Builds** (ready-made kits), **Cosmetics** (respray, liveries, plates), **Bodywork**, **Performance** (engine, transmission, nitrous), **Wheels** and **Interior**.
4. Check the stat preview before you buy. Game Rant notes that engine parts raise speed and acceleration, while wheels affect on-road or off-road handling.

## Money and stolen cars

Bigger purchases come out of your **BuckMe** bank account, not your pocket cash, so put money in at an ATM if a part won't go through. GTA6 Land lists nitrous at $5,000. Prices change from car to car. Our [banking guide](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-bank-save-money) explains how BuckMe works.

You can't mod a stolen car with a **standard tracker** until you register it as your own. Cars with an **advanced tracker** are meant to be sold, not modded. See [how to keep a stolen car](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-keep-register-stolen-car) and [how to remove trackers](/posts/2026-11-26-gta-6-remove-car-trackers).

If you only need a new paint job to lose vehicle heat, GamesRadar+ says any Pay 'n' Spray will do.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar hasn't published a car customization guide. The menus and shops described here come from the game itself.
- **Reported:** the unlock mission, the shop list, the count of 14 and the prices all come from guide writers playing the game (Game Rant, GamesRadar+, GTA Playbook, GTA6 Land). The guides don't fully agree on the list, and prices can change from car to car.
