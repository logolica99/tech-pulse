France's Ministry of Higher Education, Research and Space has published a ten-year national fusion energy strategy. Minister Philippe Baptiste presented it on Monday, November 16, during a visit to ITER and the CEA's WEST tokamak at Cadarache in southern France.

## The plan

The roadmap is built around three French facilities: the international ITER project, the WEST tokamak for magnetic confinement, and the Laser Mégajoule near Bordeaux for inertial confinement. It is meant to coordinate research, guide investment and line French work up with European plans.

- **2026–2030:** consolidate the scientific and technological foundations, strengthen skills and experimental infrastructure, and secure the necessary funding.
- **By 2035:** have the technologies needed for a European pilot plant.

The strategy also aims to double the number of students in fields such as plasma physics, nuclear engineering and cryogenics. According to AFP, the document says controlling fusion has become an objective "achievable within this century." It also estimates cumulative private investment in fusion at about €13 billion by the end of 2025, mostly in the United States and Asia.

## ITER progress

ITER says machine assembly is running six months ahead of its baseline plan and that the tokamak torus will be complete in April 2027.
