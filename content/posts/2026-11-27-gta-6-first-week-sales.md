**Short answer: about 53 million copies and about $4.7 billion in its first week, according to an estimate from the research firm Alinea Analytics. That is not an official number. Take-Two has not published one-week sales.**

## What's new

Until now, the numbers covered launch day only. Take-Two said GTA 6 made $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and sold 32.5 million units before release (see [our earlier post](/posts/2026-11-20-gta-6-official-sales-take-two)), and before that Sensor Tower had estimated about 24.6 million copies on day one (see [this post](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-day-one-sales)). On November 27, Alinea Analytics put out the first estimate for the full first week.

## What the estimate says

As reported by GamesBeat and Polygon, Alinea Analytics estimates that, in the week after the November 19 launch:

- GTA 6 sold about **53 million copies**.
- It brought in about **$4.7 billion**.
- **75.7% of copies** were sold on PlayStation 5, and PS5 made up 76.1% of revenue, which GamesBeat attributes to regional pricing.
- About **7 million players** have already played more than 50 hours, and more than **250,000** have passed 100 hours.
- The US accounts for 33.5% of PS5 copies.

Polygon adds that, by the same report, sales of several other big 2026 PlayStation games fell 34% during GTA 6's launch week, though Alinea notes normal sales decline was also a factor. For comparison, Polygon notes GTA 5 sold 29 million copies in its whole first month.

## Official or not?

- **Official, from Take-Two:** only the launch-day figures so far: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million units sold before release.
- **Not official:** the 53 million copies, $4.7 billion, the PS5 share and the playtime numbers are all Alinea Analytics estimates. GamesBeat points out that Take-Two has not disclosed one-week numbers, and that its day-one figures turned out quite different from earlier analyst reports. Treat the week-one figure as an educated guess until Take-Two reports its own numbers.
