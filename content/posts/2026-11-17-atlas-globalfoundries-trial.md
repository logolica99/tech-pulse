Semiconductor manufacturer GlobalFoundries will begin testing Boston Dynamics' Atlas humanoid in early 2027, making it the first publicly announced Atlas customer outside the automotive industry and outside parent company Hyundai Motor Group, The Robot Report reported.

The two companies plan to identify humanoid use cases in chip fabs, assess integration needs, and start training Atlas on specific tasks. They did not say how many robots are involved, which facilities will host them, or which tasks come first.

## Building on Spot

GlobalFoundries already uses Boston Dynamics' Spot quadruped for industrial inspections and is expanding those deployments across its global network. According to the companies, two Spot robots at its Singapore site have traveled nearly 900 km and completed more than 90,000 inspections in sub-fab areas since April 2026, with a 99% mission success rate.

## Why it matters

Car factories have been the early proving ground for humanoids, and Atlas's initial 2026 production run was committed to Hyundai and Google DeepMind. A chip fab brings different technical and operational demands, so the trial is a test of whether Atlas can generalize beyond automotive work. Hyundai has separately outlined plans to deploy 25,000 Atlas robots across Hyundai and Kia plants over the next several years.
