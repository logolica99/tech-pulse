**Short answer: take the loot bag to a fence, shown as a green icon on the map. Fences unlock with the story: Brian and Donnie in Key Lento (Chapter 1), Dunk in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2), then Tyrone and Yasmani in Vice City (Chapter 3). For the best prices, take drugs to Tyrone, and jewelry and marked bills to Yasmani.**

## Why your robbery didn't pay

GTA 6 splits stolen goods into three kinds: narcotics, jewelry and marked bills. Anything that isn't cash goes into a stash bag you carry. You can also leave the bag in your car's trunk. It only turns into money when you hand it to a fence, so robbing a jewelry counter and getting no money is normal. Guides also warn that a bag you're carrying, or one in your trunk, can be lost if you don't get away from the police after a job.

## Every fence and when it unlocks

According to PowerPyx, Power Up Gaming and The GTA 6 Map:

- **Brian**, south Key Lento: after the Chapter 1 mission Lab Fees. Pays very low, and goes away after Chapter 1.
- **Donnie**, his trailer in Key Lento: after Clean Up After Donnie in Chapter 1. Pays low, and PowerPyx says he goes away after the Chapter 1 mission Swamped.
- **Dunk**, Port Gellhorn: after the Chapter 2 mission Slam Dunk. You leave goods in the trunk of his car.
- **Tyrone**, La Perle, Vice City: Chapter 3, after Here, Kitty Kitty. PowerPyx lists his narcotics rate as High.
- **Yasmani**, Little Cuba, Vice City: early Chapter 3. Guides name either Boobie Time or the jewelry job right after it as the unlock. PowerPyx lists his jewelry and marked bills rates as High.

If you hover over a fence's map icon, it shows what that fence pays for each type of goods.

## How you get paid

The early fences use a drop box and pay you later. Before you open a bank account, the cash shows up in the mailbox outside Jason's house. After that it comes by bank transfer. Tyrone and Yasmani pay on the spot.

PowerPyx says that once Yasmani is unlocked, you can drop goods at one of your garages and choose which fence buys them. The GTA 6 Map credits PC Gamer for the detail that this pickup costs a fee and opens after two of Yasmani's Payday Loans jobs. PowerPyx also describes telling your partner to "Fence Goods" when the bag is in your car's trunk, though the option doesn't always appear.

## Stolen cars are different

Goods fences don't take cars. Moses in Port Gellhorn buys stolen cars without trackers from Chapter 2. Later, Randy in Vice City takes certain high-end cars. ## Official or not?

Rockstar hasn't published a fence guide. The names, locations, unlock missions and payout ratings above come from walkthrough sites (PowerPyx, Power Up Gaming and The GTA 6 Map) based on their own playthroughs. They mostly agree, but they differ slightly on which mission unlocks Yasmani.
