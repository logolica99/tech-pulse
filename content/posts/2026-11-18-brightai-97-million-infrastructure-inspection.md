BrightAI, a physical AI company focused on infrastructure like power lines, pipelines and water systems, said Wednesday it has raised $97 million. Smith Point Capital, the growth firm started by former Salesforce co-CEO Keith Block, led the round.

GE Vernova, already a customer, also invested, along with Cobalt Capital, Marlinspike Partners and Zero Infinity Partners and existing backers Khosla Ventures, Inspired Capital and Upfront Ventures. According to Fortune, citing a person familiar with the matter, the round brings total funding to $184 million and values the company at $750 million.

## What it does

Most utilities and pipeline operators still send crews out on a fixed schedule to inspect equipment, and BrightAI says more than half of those trips find nothing to fix. Its platform, called Stateful, replaces that with constant monitoring: edge sensors on poles, pipelines, compressors and tanks, plus autonomous robots, drones and wearable AI assistants for field crews.

The company says it runs at more than 50,000 locations, and founder Alex Hawkinson, who previously started SmartThings, told Fortune that customers on average spot trouble 51 days before what would have been a catastrophic failure. Customers include GE Vernova, Reworld, Osmose Utilities Services, Azuria Water Solutions and Kodiak Gas Services. GE Vernova plans to use BrightAI across its Power, Wind and Electrification businesses.

## Where the money goes

Most of the new funding will go toward expanding internationally. BrightAI says nearly 60% of its addressable market is outside North America, and it is opening offices abroad.
