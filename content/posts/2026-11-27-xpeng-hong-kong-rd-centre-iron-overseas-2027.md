Two days after [XPENG debuted its IRON humanoid at AI Day](/posts/2026-11-25-xpeng-iron-humanoid-turing-physical-ai), the company used the Guangzhou Auto Show on November 27 to add an international timeline and a new research centre.

## What's new

- **Hong Kong R&D Centre.** XPENG's ninth R&D centre will focus on humanoid robots and general-purpose intelligence, including whole-body motion control, mobile manipulation, world models and embodied intelligence. IRON itself announced it on stage while hosting the press conference.
- **IRON goes abroad.** After starting showroom work at XPENG stores in China in Q1 2027, IRON is set to deploy in XPENG's overseas showrooms in the second half of 2027.
- **Booth trial.** Several IRON robots worked the booth together as sales assistants, receptionists and guides, which XPENG says is the first time multiple units took customer-facing roles at once.

## Beyond the robot

XPENG also said it is on track for more than 100,000 overseas car sales in 2026 and aims to more than double that in 2027, with five new models abroad next year, starting with the New P7 in Europe in Q2 2027. It plans to roll out its NGP driver-assistance system overseas in the first half of 2027, subject to local approval, and is targeting a global launch of its YoYo-01 robotaxi in 2027. Four more X-Energy megawatt charging stations are planned across Hong Kong, Australia, Germany and the Netherlands.
