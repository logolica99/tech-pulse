**Short answer: yes, but not right away.** GTA 6 has cheat codes, but Polygon, GameSpot and PC Gamer all found that they only work once you have finished the main story. Each code then has to be unlocked by completing a challenge.

## How to unlock cheats

All three outlets tested cheats on early saves and found they did nothing. After you roll credits, a Cheats section appears. Polygon found it at the end of the Progression tab in the map menu.

The codes aren't all available at once. Each cheat has a condition, such as catching 100 fish, completing 50 stunt jumps, or spending four hours holding hands with your partner. When you meet the condition, the game shows the code. GameSpot and Polygon say challenges you complete before the ending count too, so you will probably have a few codes ready when cheats open up.

Polygon groups the cheats into five types: player, combat, vehicle spawns, environment (time and weather), and screen filters.

## How to enter a code

Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone. Hold L1 and R1 on PS5, then enter the code with the D-pad. Polygon tested only on PS5 and assumes Xbox uses LB and RB, but has not confirmed that.

All three outlets list the same code for these cheats:

- **$250,000:** Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right
- **Max Ammo:** Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Down, Left
- **Spawn MTL Pumper:** Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Up
- **Dollhouse screen filter:** Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Down, Right

The guides are still filling in many codes and unlock conditions, and they disagree on some details. The game's Cheats menu is the safest place to check.

## The catch

Once you use a cheat, you can't save for the rest of that session, according to all three guides. Polygon also says you can't earn achievements while cheats are on. Save before you use a cheat.

## Official or not?

- **From the game, as tested by outlets:** cheats unlock after the main story, each code has an unlock challenge, codes are entered on the phone, and cheats disable saving. Polygon, GameSpot and PC Gamer each played the game and agree on these points.
- **Not from Rockstar:** we found no Rockstar announcement listing the codes. The lists come from the outlets' own testing and are incomplete. Treat any "full list" from an unknown site with caution.
- **Unconfirmed:** the Xbox buttons (LB and RB) are Polygon's assumption.
