**Short answer: once you finish the Chapter 2 mission Moses, press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, open Scooter Bros (the green car icon), pick one of your registered cars and select Request. It is brought to where you are for a fee paid from your BuckMe account.**

## When you get the app

Scooter Bros is not there from the start. PowerPyx and Tech4Gamers both say it unlocks after the Chapter 2 main mission Moses, the one where you take a car to Moses and register it. That first car is the only one on the list at the beginning; every car you register afterwards is added.

If you are still in Chapter 1, Tech4Gamers says the mechanic Joey does the same job for Jason's truck: once his number is in your contacts, call him and he repairs and delivers it.

## How to request a car

1. Press Up on the D-pad to bring out the phone.
2. Open Scooter Bros, the green car icon.
3. Pick the car you want.
4. Select **Request**. The car is dropped off near you.

## What it costs

Gameta reports a $100 base rate plus a charge for the distance the car has to travel, and Tech4Gamers says repair costs are added if the car was damaged. The money comes from your BuckMe bank account, so keep some cash deposited at an ATM (see [how to bank money in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-bank-save-money)).

Tech4Gamers also reports a $12,500 Scooter Bros Premium upgrade inside the app that gives you three free deliveries a day.

## Only registered cars show up

The app lists your personal vehicles, not every car you have driven. A stolen car you never registered cannot be called back. To make a car yours, register it first: see [how to keep a stolen car in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-keep-register-stolen-car).

If the police have taken your car, the same app can retrieve it from the impound for an extra fee: see [how to get an impounded car back](/posts/2026-11-22-gta-6-impounded-car-back).

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar has not published a guide to Scooter Bros or car delivery. The app name, menus and prices come from the game itself, as reported by people who played it.
- **Agreed across guides:** the unlock after the Chapter 2 mission Moses, the D-pad Up phone and the Request option, and that only registered cars can be called.
- **From single guides:** the $100 base rate plus distance (Gameta), the added repair cost and the $12,500 Premium upgrade (Tech4Gamers), and Joey's Chapter 1 deliveries (Tech4Gamers). Fees may vary in your game.
