China's National Data Administration plans to improve standards for how AI tokens are measured, billed and checked for quality. The plan came in remarks by the agency's director, Liu Liehong, posted on its website on November 21, The Next Web reports, citing Bloomberg.

## Why tokens

Tokens are the small units of text, audio and video that AI models process, and Chinese officials increasingly use them to measure and charge for AI use. Liu said an industry built around producing, selling and using tokens is taking shape. So-called token factories combine rented computing power, AI models and data to sell tokens as a service.

The agency said it will also widen the uses of token services and try to prevent low-quality duplication across the industry. It did not say when the new standards would be published.

## A growing token economy

Beijing has promoted the idea of a token economy throughout the year. According to the report, Liu said in March that China's daily token use had passed 140 trillion, more than 1,000 times the level of early 2024, and agency data cited by People's Daily put it above 500 trillion by June. Some Chinese companies have also begun selling tokens to consumers through banks and telecom carriers.
