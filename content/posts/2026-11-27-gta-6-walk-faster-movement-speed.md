**Short answer: open Settings, switch to the Gameplay tab, scroll to the Movement section, and change Movement Speed from Walk (the default) to Fast Walk, Jog or Run. It needs no unlock and works right away.**

## Why people look for this

Jason and Lucia have a slow, relaxed default walk. Both PC Gamer and The Guardian flagged it in early tips, and both point to the same fix: a menu setting rather than holding a button.

## How to change it

1. Pause and open **Settings**.
2. Move to the **Gameplay** tab.
3. Scroll down to **Movement**.
4. Pick **Movement Speed** and choose Walk, Fast Walk, Jog or Run.
5. Back out and confirm when the game asks to save your settings.

## What the setting does

- It changes how fast you move when you push the left stick all the way. Each option is faster than the one before.
- It does not change your sprint, the stamina-burning top speed.
- You can still move slowly by tilting the stick only a little.

PC Gamer suggests Fast Walk for a small bump that doesn't feel rushed, with Jog or Run if you just want to cover ground. The Guardian's reviewer also went with Fast Walk.

## Combat Movement Speed

Right below it is **Combat Movement Speed**, which sets your pace in gunfights. It defaults to Fast Walk and can go up to Jog or Run. According to Nerds Chalk, if your normal Movement Speed is set faster than your combat speed, the faster normal speed wins.

## Official or not?

- **In the game itself:** the Movement Speed and Combat Movement Speed options are real menu settings in GTA 6, as shown in the outlets' screenshots and guides.
- **Not from Rockstar:** Rockstar has not published a guide to these settings. The descriptions and the recommendations about which option to pick come from PC Gamer, The Guardian and Nerds Chalk.
