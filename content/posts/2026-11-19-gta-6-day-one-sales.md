**Short answer: about 24.6 million copies, but that's an estimate, not an official number.** Analytics firm Sensor Tower put GTA 6's launch-day sales at about 24.58 million units, worth about $2.36 billion, as of Thursday evening, Reuters reported. Take-Two and Rockstar have not released a sales figure.

## How the estimate grew during the day

Sensor Tower's number went up over launch day. On Thursday morning, Reuters reported its estimate as 19.1 million copies and about $1.84 billion in revenue. USA Today, citing the same firm, said PlayStation made up about $1.38 billion of that early total and Xbox about $464 million. By Thursday evening, Reuters had updated the estimate to 24.58 million copies and about $2.36 billion.

## How that compares to GTA 5

Reuters says GTA 5 sold about $800 million worth of copies on its first day in 2013, widely seen as the best 24-hour launch for a video game. If Sensor Tower is right, GTA 6 made almost three times as much on day one.

## What Take-Two has said

Take-Two's launch press release on November 19 doesn't give a GTA 6 sales number. It says GTA 5 has sold-in nearly 235 million units and the series nearly 480 million. According to Reuters, Take-Two has only called demand "unprecedented" or "far exceeding expectations" since pre-orders opened in June. CEO Strauss Zelnick said on the company's earnings call this month that "we still aren't ready to declare victory."

Take-Two usually reports sales in its earnings releases, so an official number may come later.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Take-Two):** GTA 6 launched on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. GTA 5 has sold-in nearly 235 million units. Take-Two has not shared GTA 6 sales.
- **Estimates:** the 19.1 million and 24.58 million unit figures and the dollar amounts are Sensor Tower estimates, reported by Reuters and USA Today. Take-Two has not confirmed them, and they may change.
- **Be careful with:** any "official" GTA 6 sales number you see from social media posts before Take-Two announces one.
