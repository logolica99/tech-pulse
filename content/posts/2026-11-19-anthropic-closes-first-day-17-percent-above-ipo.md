Anthropic's first day as a public company ended well above its offer price, but far below its early peak. The Claude maker's shares closed at $97.17 on Thursday, about 17% above the $83 IPO price, Nikkei Asia reports.

The stock opened at $111.50 shortly before noon Eastern time and quickly rose as high as $115, or 38.6% above the offer price, according to Reuters. At that point Anthropic's market value briefly passed $2 trillion before the gains faded through the afternoon.

## A rough day for the AI trade

The debut, the largest IPO on record, had been seen as a test of investor appetite for AI. It did not lift the wider market. All three major US stock indexes fell at least 2%, and the S&P 500 posted its sixth losing session in a row, Reuters reports.

Chip and AI infrastructure stocks were among the weakest. Nvidia fell 3.3% for its sixth straight decline, AMD dropped 7.7% after the investigative outlet Hunterbrook disclosed a short position, and neocloud firms CoreWeave and Nebius lost 6.1% and 9.4%. One portfolio manager quoted by Reuters cautioned against reading too much into the first day of trading, since investors were shifting money around to buy into Anthropic.
