**Short answer: Yes. Once the finale and credits are done, GTA 6 rewinds to the point just before the final mission. Jason and Lucia decide they are not ready yet, and you are back in free roam on the same save.**

## How the post-game works

Before the last mission, the game clearly asks whether you want to go ahead with the finale. After you finish it and see one of the endings, Kotaku and GamesRadar+ report that the game drops you back at that point-of-no-return moment. This is different from GTA IV or GTA V, where you simply carried on after the story, and from Red Dead Redemption 2, where you switched to another character.

Rockstar handles it this way because the game has many possible endings, shaped by things like your relationships, your Criminal Profile and whether you paid into family savings, according to GamesRadar+. Rewinding avoids having to build a world that reflects every outcome. The trade-off, Kotaku notes, is that the world feels a bit frozen in time afterward.

## What carries over

According to Dot Esports:

- Both Jason and Lucia stay playable and switchable.
- Your money, weapons, vehicles and garages carry over.
- Unfinished side quests, collectibles and activities are still there.
- The finale marker stays on the map, so you can replay the ending and try for a different result.

What you can't do: replay earlier story missions from a menu, and there is no New Game Plus at launch. To revisit an older mission, load a manual save from before it.

## What unlocks after the credits

Kotaku and GamesRadar+ both say finishing the story opens up tracking for 100% completion and a set of challenges that unlock in-game cheats from the Progress menu. Our earlier post explains [how cheats work in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-cheat-codes).

## Tip: protect your save

GamesRadar+ recommends making a new manual save right after you are dropped back into free roam, because that autosave can be lost if you load an older save or start a new game. GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots ([how to save](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-how-to-save-game)), so keeping a spare save from before the finale is also a good idea if you want to see more than one ending.

## Official or not?

- **Not from Rockstar directly:** Rockstar has not published its own explainer on the post-game. Everything above comes from reviewers and guide writers at Kotaku, GamesRadar+ and Dot Esports describing what happens in the released game.
- **Consistent across sources:** all three agree on the rewind to before the finale, both characters staying playable, and the replayable final mission.
- **Could change:** "No New Game Plus" describes the game at launch; Rockstar has not said anything about adding one.
