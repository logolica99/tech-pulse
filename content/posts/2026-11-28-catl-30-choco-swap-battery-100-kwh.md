CATL has added a much bigger battery to its "Choco-SEB" swapping system for passenger cars. It showed the 30# pack on November 26 at Huawei's Qiankun ecosystem conference, CarNewsChina reports.

## The pack

CATL's existing car packs are the 20# (42 kWh LFP or 52 kWh NMC) and the 25# (56 kWh LFP or 70 kWh NMC). The most common at its stations is the 56 kWh LFP version. The new 30# holds 100 kWh. CATL's claims for it:

- About 700 km of range on the Chinese CLTC cycle
- Up to 600 kW peak discharge
- A swap in 99 seconds, or fast charging that adds 400 km of range in 10 minutes at a normal charger
- Room to move to sodium-ion or solid-state cells later

CATL has not said what cell chemistry the pack uses, what it costs or which cars will take it. CarNewsChina points to a battery-swap version of BAIC's Xingtan 5X in recent Chinese type-approval filings as a likely first user.

## The bigger push

CarNewsChina says CATL runs more than 2,500 swap stations in China and aims for 30,000 in the long run. electrive notes CATL announced this month that it wants 6,000 passenger-car swap stations by 2027, which would likely pass NIO's network. BAIC has signed up to build compatible models and stations, and Hyundai plans a China-market Ioniq SUV in 2027 that can use CATL swap packs.
