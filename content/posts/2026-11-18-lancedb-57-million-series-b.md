LanceDB has raised a $57.5 million Series B, co-led by Bessemer Venture Partners and Innovation Endeavors, with Theory Ventures, Dimension, Swift Ventures, CRV and Y Combinator also taking part. SiliconANGLE reports the round brings the company's total funding to $98.5 million.

## The problem it targets

Training data for modern AI is increasingly not just text. Robots, autonomous vehicles and world models produce video, lidar point clouds, time-series sensor logs and annotations, and teams often keep the pieces in separate systems. CEO Chang She told SiliconANGLE that research teams can lose weeks or months stitching a dataset together before a single training run.

LanceDB's answer is Lance, an open-source format that keeps raw images or video in the same table as their metadata, embeddings and features, and supports fast lookups and edits of individual records. On top of it, LanceDB Enterprise adds distributed indexing, ingestion, training data loaders and feature engineering, running in the customer's own cloud or on-premises so data doesn't have to be copied.

## Who uses it

According to Bessemer, the open-source library is downloaded tens of millions of times a month, up sixfold in a year. Named users include Runway, Netflix and Microsoft, per SiliconANGLE, and WorldLabs, Applied Intuition and ByteDance, per Bessemer.

The company says it will use the money to improve large-scale data curation, feature engineering and data loading, and to deepen support for video generation, world models and robotics workloads.
