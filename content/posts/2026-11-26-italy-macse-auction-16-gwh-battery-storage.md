Italy's second auction under MACSE, its mechanism for procuring long-term electricity storage, has awarded all 16 GWh of capacity on offer, for delivery in 2029. The auction, run by grid operator Terna on November 24, picked 16 battery projects averaging about 1 GWh each, pv magazine reports.

## Strong competition

- Around 67 GWh of projects qualified, and bids totaled about 43 GWh, nearly three times what was needed.
- The weighted average award price was €11,382 per MWh-year, 12% below the €12,959 of the first MACSE auction (for 2028 delivery) and far below the €27,000 reserve price.
- The Center-South region cleared lowest, at €10,716 per MWh-year. South and Calabria was highest, with a weighted average of €11,575.

## Who won

Enel Produzione took 7,337 MWh, almost half the total, including the largest single project at 3,399 MWh in South and Calabria. A2A Storage won 2,244 MWh and A2A Energiefuture another 364 MWh. Other winners included BESS by Erry and Field Melfi.

pv magazine says the low prices surprised parts of the industry. Projects behind the roughly 27 GWh that missed out will now have to look to merchant markets, tolling deals or Italy's capacity market instead.
