**Short answer: 99 out of 100.** On the afternoon of launch day, Metacritic lists Grand Theft Auto VI at a Metascore of 99 from 151 critic reviews on PlayStation 5, all of them positive. The Xbox Series X|S version also shows 99, from 14 reviews.

## Tied for the best ever

A 99 Metascore puts GTA 6 level with the original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time from 1998. Polygon notes it is also higher than Rockstar's last two big games, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, which both sit at 97. GameSpot says it is now the highest-rated game of 2026 on Metacritic.

## OpenCritic

OpenCritic shows a Top Critic Average of 99, with 99% of critics recommending the game. Early in review day Polygon saw 98 there, so the number has moved as more reviews came in.

## What critics liked, and the few complaints

Most big outlets gave full marks, including IGN (10/10), GameSpot (10/10), Eurogamer (5/5) and Game Informer (100). Reviewers praise the detail of Leonida and the relationship between Jason and Lucia.

The complaints are mostly about pacing and performance. The lowest score on Metacritic right now is ScreenHub's 80, which says the opening hours are slow and limited before the game opens up. One review on Metacritic also mentions a 30fps cap. Metacritic user reviews do not open until 12:00 PM PST on November 20.

## Official or not?

- **Not from Rockstar:** these scores are aggregates of independent reviews, read from Metacritic and OpenCritic on November 19. Both numbers can still change as more reviews are added.
- All reviews so far are of the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions, the only platforms Metacritic and OpenCritic list for the game.
