Anthropic's record IPO got even bigger when it formally closed on Friday. The company said underwriters fully exercised their option to buy an extra 147 million shares, bringing the offering to 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83 each.

## The final numbers

According to Anthropic's announcement, the offering raised about $93.5 billion in gross proceeds, up from the roughly $81.3 billion reported when it [priced](/posts/2026-11-18-anthropic-prices-ipo-83-dollars). Of that, about $91.6 billion goes to Anthropic; the rest went to existing shareholders who sold about 23.6 million shares.

At the same time, Anthropic closed a private sale of about 313 million Class N shares, also at $83, to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners, for about $26 billion. Counting both, the company says gross proceeds came to roughly $119.5 billion before fees and expenses. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led the offering.

## A rocky second day

The stock has had a bumpy start. After [closing its first day](/posts/2026-11-19-anthropic-closes-first-day-17-percent-above-ipo) at $97.17, Anthropic shares fell about 10% on Friday as AI-related stocks, including Nvidia and AMD, slid again, CNBC reports. Analyst views are split: CNBC says Seaport Research started coverage at neutral over concerns about revenue growth, while William Blair rated the stock outperform.
