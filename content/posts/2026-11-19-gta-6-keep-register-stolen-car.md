**Short answer: register it.** Polygon says a stolen car is lost if you leave it behind, destroy it or quit the game before it's registered in your name. Registration unlocks in Chapter 2 and gets broader in Chapter 3.

## When you can register cars

- **Chapter 1:** you can drive stolen cars, but KeenGamer says Jason's own car is your only personal vehicle until registration unlocks.
- **Chapter 2, Moses's garage:** the story mission that takes a car to Moses, in Port Gellhorn, unlocks registration. Polygon and KeenGamer say he only takes cars with **no tracker**. KeenGamer says your first registration there is free.
- **Chapter 3, Pay 'n' Spray:** KeenGamer says Pay 'n' Spray shops can then register cars with a **standard tracker**. TechRadar's launch-day roundup also lists registering at a Pay 'n' Spray as a key tip.
- **Advanced trackers:** Polygon and KeenGamer say high-end cars with advanced trackers need later Chapter 3 progress through a character named Randy.

## Step by step

1. **Scan the car.** KeenGamer says to open the WAiNK phone app (hold Up on the D-pad) and scan with R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. It shows whether the car has a tracker.
2. **Steal it far from the garage.** KeenGamer says Moses turns down cars stolen too close to his shop.
3. **Lose the police** before you drive in.
4. **Drive in and choose Register.** At Moses's garage, respray the car first.
5. **Pay the fee.** Polygon says it ranges from a few thousand to tens of thousands of dollars, depending on the car's value. KeenGamer says some sports cars cost more than $20,000.

## Garage space and getting your car back

- **You need a free slot.** KeenGamer says you start with limited space. Sell a registered car or buy another garage in the Vice City Real Estate app, which unlocks later in Chapter 2.
- **Parking isn't owning.** Polygon says you can leave an unregistered car in your garage until you can afford to register it. If you take it out and abandon it, it's gone.
- **Delivery and recovery.** KeenGamer and TechRadar say the Scooter Bros app delivers your registered cars for a fee. KeenGamer says it can also recover destroyed or impounded registered cars.
- **Weapons work differently.** Polygon says looted guns stay only if you store them in a vehicle trunk. Guns you buy at Ammu-Nation are yours for good.

## Official or not?

- **Rockstar:** Rockstar hasn't published a vehicle registration guide. Nothing here comes from a Rockstar statement.
- **Guides, not Rockstar:** the chapter unlocks, tracker rules, fees and garage details come from Polygon, KeenGamer and TechRadar, based on the launch version. Exact fees vary by car.
