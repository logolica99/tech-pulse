VectorSingularity, a Beijing company building neutral-atom quantum computers, has closed a Series A round worth "hundreds of millions of yuan," according to a report by PE Daily, published by 36Kr. No exact amount was given, and no release from the company or Alibaba was found.

## Who invested

The round was led by a group of state-linked funds, including the Beijing Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund, Beijing Beigong and its Future Quantum Fund, a small-business fund under China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and the Zhongguancun Science City Fund. China Merchants Venture Capital and Alibaba Group joined as industrial investors. 36Kr describes it as Alibaba's first investment in a quantum company.

## The company

VectorSingularity was founded in May 2026 and raised an angel round of more than 100 million yuan led by IDG Capital in August, so this is its second round in roughly six months. It runs two hardware platforms in parallel, using rubidium and ytterbium atoms, and says it aims to demonstrate quantum error correction next year on the rubidium platform. That goal is a company plan, not a result.

CEO and chief scientist Li Xiangliang previously led neutral-atom research at the Beijing Academy of Quantum Information Sciences.
