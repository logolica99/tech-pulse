**Short answer: GTA+, Rockstar's $7.99-a-month membership, now unlocks bonuses inside GTA 6's single-player game. Members get a '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy with an exclusive widebody design and a single-car garage in Crosstown (both from Chapter 3), free rentals and public transit, premium RydeMe and Scooter Bros service, 10% off at select shops and a 10% bonus when selling eligible cars. Everything goes away if you cancel.**

## Why this is new

GTA+ used to be mostly a GTA Online perk. Rockstar's launch post on November 19 said GTA+ "now contains GTAVI bonuses for Members", and that a free month of GTA+ came with purchases up to launch day. Because GTA 6 has no online mode yet, all of these perks land in story mode.

## What members get

GamesRadar+ and Polygon list the same set:

- **Car and garage.** A '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy sports car with an exclusive widebody design, plus a single-car garage in Crosstown. Polygon says the garage has a weapon locker and lets you fence stolen goods. Both become available after you reach Chapter 3.
- **Getting around.** Free bike, Seashark and scooter rentals, free rides on public transport, premium access in the RydeMe rideshare app, and upgrades from Scooter Bros Premium vehicle care.
- **Selling cars.** A 10% bonus when you sell eligible owned vehicles. GamesRadar+ notes that Ultimate Edition vehicles can't be sold.
- **Discounts.** 10% off at select weapon, clothing, hair, tattoo and vehicle mod shops in Leonida. GamesRadar+ says eligible stores have a small yellow icon next to their name in the game's index.

## How to claim the Elegy

The car isn't handed to you right away. According to RockstarINTEL, once you're a member and in Chapter 3, visit Brian at your apartment to start the mission "Meet the Heders". After that, finish Raul's mission "Car Service". You then get a notification and a map blip to go and pick up the Elegy.

## What it costs and the catches

- GTA+ costs $7.99, €7.99 or £6.99 a month on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per GamesRadar+. It renews monthly until you cancel.
- You need an active membership and an internet connection to claim the bonuses.
- Polygon and RockstarINTEL say cancelling removes the car, the garage and the other perks.
- If you'd rather play without them, Polygon says you can turn them off: pause menu, **Account** tab, **GTA Plus Boosts**.
- If you got the free month with a pre-order, GamesRadar+ says you redeem it from the pause menu's Store tab, and it auto-renews.

Players haven't all welcomed this. Polygon points to a Reddit thread with thousands of upvotes arguing the car and garage should be in the base game, even for people who bought pricier editions. Rockstar hasn't responded to that criticism.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** The November 19 Newswire post says GTA+ now includes GTA 6 bonuses for members and that a free month came with purchases through launch day. Rockstar's GTA+ page lists the perks; the list above is how GamesRadar+ and Polygon report it.
- **Reported by outlets:** The price, the Store-tab redemption, the toggle in the Account menu and what happens when you cancel come from GamesRadar+ and Polygon.
- **Player-found:** The exact missions that unlock the Elegy come from RockstarINTEL's testing, not from Rockstar.
- **Opinion:** The backlash comes from Reddit players, as reported by Polygon.
