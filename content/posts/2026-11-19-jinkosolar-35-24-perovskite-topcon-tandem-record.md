JinkoSolar says it has set a new world record for tandem solar cells that stack a perovskite layer on top of its N-type TOPCon silicon cell. The company reports a conversion efficiency above 35.24%, certified by the Shanghai Institute of Microsystem and Information Technology, part of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

## A record it already held

The previous best for this kind of cell, 34.82%, also belonged to JinkoSolar and was set about five months earlier. The company counts this as the 34th time it has broken a world record for cell efficiency or module power.

## What changed

According to JinkoSolar's announcement, the gain came from several changes working together:

- selective passivation of the textured silicon surface in the TOPCon bottom cell
- a strategy for keeping the crystallization of the multi-component perovskite in sync
- nanoscale interface passivation materials and better light management

## Why it matters

TOPCon is now the mainstream silicon cell technology in mass production, so a perovskite top layer that fits on it is one of the more direct routes to panels that beat the efficiency limits of silicon alone. JinkoSolar frames the result as a step from lab efficiency toward industrial use, but this is a cell-level record, not a commercial module. The company also says it has set up a team to explore space photovoltaics using its silicon and perovskite know-how.
