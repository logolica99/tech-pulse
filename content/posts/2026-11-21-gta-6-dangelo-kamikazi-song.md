**Short answer: yes. GTA 6's Worldwide FM radio station plays "Kamikazi", a D'Angelo song that had never been released before. A second D'Angelo track, "Lady", plays on Honey FM.**

## What players found

Players heard the song on the in-game radio right after launch. The Washington Post reports that "Kamikazi" appears in Rockstar's official music credits for the game, credited "courtesy of RCA", D'Angelo's label. Rolling Stone says D'Angelo collaborator DJ Dojah confirmed that the song is his and that its title is "Kamikazi".

D'Angelo died of pancreatic cancer in October 2025, more than a year before GTA 6 came out. He had worked with Rockstar before: he wrote "Unshaken" for Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018, and Polygon notes he performed it live with Rockstar at the 2021 Tribeca Film Festival.

## How to hear it

Tune your car radio to **Worldwide FM** and keep listening until "Kamikazi" comes up, as Polygon suggests. For "Lady", try **Honey FM**.

You can't stream "Kamikazi" anywhere else yet. Rolling Stone reports that YouTube uploads of it have been hit with takedown notices from Sony Music Entertainment, RCA's parent company. The Washington Post says RCA didn't immediately answer whether it will come out as a single. Our [radio stations post](/posts/2026-11-20-gta-6-radio-stations-spotify) covers the Spotify hub for the game's stations.

## Was it made for GTA 6?

Nobody knows yet. Rolling Stone says it's unclear whether D'Angelo recorded "Kamikazi" for the game or whether it came from the unreleased music he left behind. His estate and collaborators have talked about more of that music to come, and Rolling Stone calls "Kamikazi" the first taste of it.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** the song is in Rockstar's music credits for GTA 6, credited to RCA, according to The Washington Post. Rockstar hasn't otherwise commented on it.
- **Confirmed by a collaborator:** DJ Dojah confirmed the song and its title, per Rolling Stone.
- **Unknown:** whether it was written for the game, and whether or when it will be released outside GTA 6.
