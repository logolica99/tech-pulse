Anthropic, the maker of Claude, has set the price for its stock market debut at $83 per Class A share, above the $70 to $78 range it had marketed to investors.

## The numbers

According to Anthropic's own announcement, the offering covers 980 million shares: about 956.4 million sold by the company and about 23.6 million sold by existing stockholders, whose proceeds go to them rather than to Anthropic. Underwriters also have a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more shares.

NPR reports the company itself raised roughly $79.4 billion, and more than $81.3 billion including the stockholder sales, which tops the $75 billion SpaceX raised in its IPO earlier this year. The prospectus had originally targeted about 825 million shares, so the deal was both upsized and priced higher. The AP, citing multiple reports, says the price values Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion.

## What's next

Shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Thursday, November 19, under the symbol ANT, with the offering expected to close on November 20. The AP notes the strong pricing comes despite market jitters over the ouster of Nvidia's CEO and worries about an AI bubble.
