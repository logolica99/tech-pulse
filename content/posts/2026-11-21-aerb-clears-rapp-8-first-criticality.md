India's Atomic Energy Regulatory Board (AERB) has granted permission for the First Approach to Criticality of Rajasthan Atomic Power Project Unit-8 (RAPP-8), the government said on November 20. The approval was given on November 19.

## What it means

First criticality is when a new reactor sustains a controlled nuclear chain reaction for the first time, at low power. The AERB says it gave its permission after reviewing the reactor's design, construction, commissioning test results and operational readiness, with input from its safety committees and inspections.

It is not yet the start of commercial power. More commissioning tests and regulatory approvals are needed before RAPP-8 can send electricity to the grid commercially, and the AERB says its resident site observers will keep monitoring the work.

## The reactor

RAPP-8 is the fourth unit in India's indigenously designed and built 700 MWe Pressurised Heavy Water Reactor series. The other three are Kakrapar units 3 and 4 in Gujarat and RAPP-7 in Rajasthan. The unit is at the Rawatbhata site in Chittorgarh district, which now has eight nuclear units.
