**Short answer: go to the bed at your current safehouse, lie down, choose Sleep (Square on PS5, X on Xbox) and set an alarm for when Jason or Lucia should wake up.**

Some missions only start at certain times, and some businesses are only open part of the day, so skipping time comes up a lot.

## How to sleep

1. Go to the safehouse the story currently gives you. In Chapter 1 that's Jason's house in Key Lento.
2. Walk up to the bed and interact with it to lie down.
3. From the bed you can check your phone, sleep or get up. Pick **Sleep**.
4. Set the alarm. Polygon says you can pick a specific time or one of four presets: morning, noon, evening or night.

The Games Wiki lists the presets as 7:00 AM, 12:30 PM, 6:00 PM and 12:01 AM, and says waking up also restores health and Focus. It also notes that sleeping does not save your game; use the pause menu for that (see [how to save in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-how-to-save-game)).

## Other ways time moves

- **Just keep playing.** The clock runs on its own outside missions. Polygon timed one in-game minute at about six real seconds, so a full day takes about 2 hours 24 minutes.
- **Long rides.** GamesRadar+ says skipping a long trip with [fast travel](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-fast-travel) moves the clock forward too.
- **Missions set their own time.** Polygon found a mission it started at 8 a.m. jumped the clock to 3 p.m. to fit the story.
- **After the story.** One of the [cheats](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-cheat-codes) unlocked in the endgame changes the time of day, GamesRadar+ says.

## Official or not?

Rockstar has not published a guide to the day-night cycle. The steps above come from hands-on guides by Polygon, GamesRadar+ and The Games Wiki. The day length is Polygon's own stopwatch test, not an official figure, and the exact preset wake-up times come from The Games Wiki.
