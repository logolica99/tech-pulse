**Short answer: Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval and Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos.** Both names sit at the top of the Main Cast in Rockstar's official GTA 6 credits, which went up on rockstargames.com at launch. Fans had guessed both names for years. Now Rockstar has confirmed them.

## Who are they?

Polygon says both leads come from TV. Rourke has been on shows like 9-1-1: Lone Star, Station 19 and Grey's Anatomy, and GTA 6 is by far his biggest role. Perez has been on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, Chicago P.D. and The Blacklist. She has worked with Rockstar before, with small voice roles in GTA 5 and GTA Online.

## The rest of the main cast

A few familiar names from Rockstar's Main Cast list:

- **Cal:** Bobby Moynihan (Saturday Night Live)
- **Brian Heder:** Stephen Root (Office Space, Barry, King of the Hill)
- **Lori Heder:** Orlagh Cassidy
- **Vicki:** Taryn Manning
- **Kordell Grant:** Michael Jai White
- **Mia Caminos:** Roselyn Sanchez
- **Dre'Quan:** Naiqui Macabroad
- **Bae-Luxe and Roxy Rashad:** rappers Hood Brat and Krissy Celess
- **Boobie Ike:** Mike Forbs
- **Raul Bautista:** Manny Perez
- **DWNPLY:** Denzel Curry

The full cast list is much longer. It includes Bad Bunny as Mitico Mario, comedian Bobby Lee as Andre Ye, and rapper Freddie Gibbs as Freddie.

## How to see the credits yourself

You don't need to finish the story. Polygon says you can open Settings from the pause menu, go to Account, then Credits, and scan a QR code. Or open Rockstar's credits page directly (linked below). Polygon adds that the in-game end credits run for over an hour.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** every actor and role named above comes from Rockstar's own credits page for Grand Theft Auto VI.
- **Not stated by Rockstar:** the credits list each actor next to a character, but they don't say who did the voice and who did motion capture. Polygon describes Rourke as Jason's "voice actor". Treat that as Polygon's wording, not Rockstar's.
- **From Polygon:** the actors' past TV roles and the steps for finding the credits in the menu.
