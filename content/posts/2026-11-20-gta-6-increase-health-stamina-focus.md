**Short answer: lift weights to raise health, do cardio (running, cycling, swimming or gym machines) to raise stamina, and play calm activities like the shooting range, pool, golf or fishing to raise focus. Then eat protein and sleep so you don't lose the gains.**

## The three stats

Dexerto and GTABase both describe three stats for each character, each with four levels:

- **Strength** (pink heart) raises your maximum **health**.
- **Cardio** raises your maximum **stamina**, so you can sprint longer.
- **Focus** (purple eye) sets how long you can slow down time in combat. See [how Focus works](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-focus-mode).

Jason and Lucia train separately and start at different levels. You can check your levels in the **Fitness app** on your phone, or under **Jason & Lucia** in the pause menu.

## Health: lift weights

You can start in Chapter 1. Jason's first safehouse has a squat rack, a bench press and kettlebells outside, so walk up to one and follow the button prompts. GTABase says three exercises make up a full daily workout, and the game tells you when it is done. Eating and drinking only extends your health bar for a while; strength training is what raises it for good.

## Stamina: cardio

Running, cycling, swimming and kayaking around the map all count. For faster gains, buy a gym membership at a fitness studio's front desk and use the treadmills, exercise bikes and punching bags. GTABase says the nearest one at the start is the Grand Majestic Gym, about three miles from Jason's house, with a $250 membership. Jason's house also has a punching bag.

## Focus: calm activities

Focus doesn't use the gym. Shooting range challenges, golf, pool and fishing raise it; GTABase also lists basketball and meditating. Dexerto says Focus has no workout window, so it builds up over your playthrough instead of needing upkeep.

## Don't lose your gains

Strength and Cardio each have a **workout window** shown in the Fitness app. Keep exercising until the game says the workout is finished. Then have a protein shake (in the fridge) or a protein bar, and sleep at a safehouse. Dexerto says protein shortens the wait for the next window. If a window closes before you finish, you can lose that session's gains, and skipping windows too often can lower your level.

Later, partner workouts let you train both characters in one gym visit.

## Official or not?

- **In the game, from Rockstar:** the stats, the Fitness app, workout windows and the activities above are game systems, as described by Dexerto and GTABase. Rockstar has not published an official guide with exact numbers.
- **Guide writers' findings, not Rockstar:** details such as three exercises making a full workout, the gym's distance and price, and which activities give the most progress come from these guides' own play.
