OpenAI is pitching ChatGPT directly at sales teams. On Tuesday it announced ChatGPT for Sales: a Sales plugin built by OpenAI for ChatGPT Work, along with what the company describes as more than 30 new and expanded partner plugins.

## What the plugin does

According to OpenAI's plugin page, Sales is meant to prepare account plans, meeting briefs, outreach, follow-ups and pipeline summaries from the context a team already has. It comes with prebuilt skills such as analyzing account signals, building a business case or competitive brief, finding customer quotes and following up after a call.

The plugin pulls from connected apps. The page lists Slack, Teams, Zoom, Granola, Fireflies, Otter.ai, Salesforce and HubSpot, plus 19 more. OpenAI notes that availability depends on the plugin, the customer's plan and workspace settings, and that some connections need an admin to set them up or approve them.

## Part of a bigger business push

TokenPost reports that the launch follows the Nov. 10 beta of Businesses in ChatGPT, which gives verified companies a Business Hub to claim their profile and manage product details such as prices and availability. Eligible U.S. e-commerce and online service businesses can also create branded Business Agents there.

AveNews, working only from OpenAI's announcement, notes that OpenAI did not spell out pricing, eligible plans or rollout details for the sales tools.
