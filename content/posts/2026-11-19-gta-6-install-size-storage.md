**Short answer: up to 212GB.** Rockstar says the GTA 6 download needs up to 212GB of free space "depending on your device", and Sony tells PS5 owners to clear a minimum of 212GB before downloading.

## What Rockstar and Sony said

Rockstar gave the figure on November 3, ahead of pre-loading, and told players to make sure they had "ample space", The Verge reports. The same day, the PlayStation Blog advised PS5 owners to clear at least 212GB and pointed to storage expansion options.

Rockstar's number is a ceiling, not an exact size for every console. Radio Times reports that on launch day, version 1.002.001 on PS5 took up about 210.5GB including add-ons. Expect the size to change as patches arrive.

For comparison, Radio Times notes that Rockstar's previous game, Red Dead Redemption 2, was about 105GB, so GTA 6 is roughly double.

## No disc install

You can't avoid the download with a physical copy. Take-Two's pre-order announcement says the boxed version contains a download code, and The Verge notes the game is only available via digital download. So whichever edition you buy, plan for the full install on your console, or add storage first; Sony's blog points PS5 owners to storage expansion options.

If you play on Xbox One, or don't want to give up the space on Xbox Series X|S, Microsoft says Xbox Cloud Gaming lets you stream the game on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S instead of installing it.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** up to 212GB depending on device, as reported by The Verge.
- **Official (Sony):** clear a minimum of 212GB on PS5.
- **Official (Take-Two):** boxed copies contain a download code.
- **Report:** the 210.5GB launch-day PS5 size for version 1.002.001 comes from Radio Times, not Rockstar.
