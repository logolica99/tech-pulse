Embo has come out of stealth with a $110 million seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz. The company says Khosla Ventures, DST Global, NVIDIA and other strategic investors also took part.

## The pitch

Embo wants to build a general "brain" for robots that works out of the box in places it has never seen. Its argument is that today's robot skills, often learned from demonstrations, are fragile and break down when an object, room or lighting changes. Instead, it is betting on world models: AI systems that learn how the physical world behaves by predicting video.

The company plans one unified model, pretrained from scratch on internet-scale video, fine-tuned on experience from its own humanoid fleet, and then improved with reinforcement learning. A world model also gives robots a place to practice motor skills in imagined scenarios, which Embo says should make them more robust to surprises.

## The team

Embo says its team created research including Dreamer, VideoGPT and Diffusion Forcing, and has worked at Google DeepMind, Meta Superintelligence Labs, NVIDIA Cosmos, UC Berkeley and MIT. In its investment note, a16z highlights Danijar Hafner, whose Dreamer agents learn by practicing inside a learned world model; the latest, Dreamer 4, learned to get diamonds in Minecraft purely from previously collected data. Embo says the team has trained multimodal models across 70,000 GPUs before.
