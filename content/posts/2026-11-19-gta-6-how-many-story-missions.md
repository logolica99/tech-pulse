**Short answer: about 91 main story missions.** That is the count in two launch-day mission lists, one from Red Bull and one from GameSpot, and both split the story into the same five chapters. Rockstar itself has not published an official mission count, so treat 91 as a count by players who finished the game, not a number from the studio.

## Missions per chapter

Both lists break down the same way (spoiler-free, chapter titles only):

| Chapter | Title | Missions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Just Like Paradise | 22 |
| 2 | Paradise Lost | 11 |
| 3 | Tits, Ass, Drug Money | 26 |
| 4 | Vice City, Baby | 25 |
| 5 | Top of the World, Ma | 7 |
| | **Total** | **91** |

Red Bull says Chapter 1 leans mostly on Jason while Lucia is still in prison, Chapter 2 follows Lucia after her release, and from Chapter 3 the pair work together in Vice City and you can switch between them.

## Why some sites say 93

SVG's mission list counts 93. Most of its list matches the other two, but it puts 13 missions in Chapter 2 instead of 11 and names a few missions differently. Until Rockstar gives a figure, "91 to 93 depending on who is counting" is the honest answer.

## How it compares

Red Bull notes that 91 missions puts GTA 6 closer in scale to Red Dead Redemption 2 than to GTA 5. Side missions and activities are extra and not part of this count.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** nothing here. Rockstar has not published a mission count or chapter list.
- **Reported by outlets that finished the game:** the 91 count and chapter split (Red Bull, GameSpot) and the 93 count (SVG).
