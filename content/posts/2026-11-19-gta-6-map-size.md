**Short answer: roughly twice the size of GTA 5's map, by Rockstar's own comparison.** Rockstar has not given an area in square miles or kilometres, but on launch day it posted the full map of Leonida, and reviewers have started timing how long it takes to cross.

## What Rockstar has said

In Game Informer's cover story, Rockstar's Aaron Garbut said the map is about twice as big as GTA 5's, Forbes reports. The state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida, was described before launch as six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

GameSpot says the game has more playable space than GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. PC Gamer's reviewer, after 100 hours, put it this way: Rockstar said twice as big as GTA 5, but it feels bigger than that.

## The official map

On November 19, Rockstar put a high-resolution Leonida map poster on the GTA VI downloads page, free to download and print. It shows Vice City, Vice Beach and the VCIA airport in Vice-Dale County, plus Mariana, Kelly, Leonard and Lummox counties. In the game itself the map starts under fog that you clear by exploring, which is why PC Gamer suggests keeping the official map handy.

## How long it takes to cross

Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen timed trips from the southernmost reachable land to the northern edge:

| How | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Walking | 3 hours, 21 minutes |
| Running and sprinting | 1 hour, 41 minutes |
| Driving the highway, fast | 10 minutes, 53 seconds |
| Prop plane | 5 minutes, 51 seconds |

For comparison, Kotaku says a fast drive across GTA 5's map takes about eight minutes. Kotaku also points out that crossing times undersell the size, because GTA 6 has far more buildings to enter, underwater areas and wilderness.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** the "about twice GTA 5" comparison and the full Leonida map on rockstargames.com.
- **Not official:** Rockstar has not published a square-mile or square-kilometre figure, so any exact area you see is someone's estimate. The crossing times are Kotaku's own test, and the "bigger than three games combined" line is GameSpot's description.
