**Short answer: yes. On Monday, November 23, Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records released Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition). It adds 23 more songs from the game, which brings the album to 57 tracks. It is out on all the major streaming services.**

This is an update to our earlier post on [the original 34-track GTA VI: The Album](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-soundtrack-album). What's new is the second batch of songs.

## What's on it

Rockstar's Newswire post names The Dare, Slayyyter, Denzel Curry, HEALTH, Power Trip, and Real Dimez x Sexyy Red among the artists. The full list of bonus tracks:

1. Burn This Shit Down – Travis Scott
2. INTERNET $TAR – Slayyyter
3. Interesting – The Dare
4. Touch & Go – Anitta
5. I JUST CAME TO POP IT – Offset
6. RCKSTRL!VING – HXG, tana & Quavo
7. Cookin – 21 Savage
8. Got Dat – Pooh Shiesty
9. Don Da Da – Young Nudy
10. CASHOUT – SoFaygo
11. Cooties (Remix) – Real Dimez x Sexyy Red
12. 50 Bands (feat. Flo Milli) – Latto
13. Tèt Kale Bobis – Kodak Black & Michaël Brun
14. FLA Flow (feat. 454) – Denzel Curry
15. You Move Me – NEIL FRANCES
16. Don't Play Around – CHRIS STASSY & KETTAMA
17. NIGHTMARE – HEALTH
18. Mutual Death Warrant – Power Trip
19. Granforma – Overmono
20. Windsor Drop – Overmono
21. Eighty Eight Comet – Overmono
22. Banshee – Overmono
23. Welcome to Leonida – Overmono

The Music page on the official GTA VI site numbers these 35 to 57, right after the original album.

## Where to listen, and is there vinyl?

Rockstar sends listeners to gtavi-thealbum.lnk.to/deluxeedition, which links to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Tidal, Amazon Music and other services. Rolling Stone notes that only the original edition is sold on vinyl and CD. Leonida Playbook checked the official album store on November 23 and found no Deluxe Edition physical product.

This is not the game's score. Justice's Original Score is a separate album, due December 11. See [when the GTA 6 original score comes out](/posts/2026-11-26-gta-6-original-score-justice-release-date).

## Official or not?

The release, the count of 23 bonus tracks, the artists it highlights, and the streaming link come from Rockstar's own Newswire post of November 23 and its official GTA VI Music page, as quoted by Leonida Playbook. Consequence and Rolling Stone published the same 23-song list. The missing vinyl or CD is an observation from those outlets, not a statement from Rockstar, so a physical release could still be announced.
