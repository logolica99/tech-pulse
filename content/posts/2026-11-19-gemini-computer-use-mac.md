Google has given the Gemini app for Mac the ability to use the computer itself. Users describe a multi-step task, and Gemini works through menus, forms, files and websites to get it done, the company announced on November 19.

## How it works

Gemini runs the task inside an isolated window in the background, with a live preview shown in the chat. Google says this leaves the user's own screen and cursor free, so they can keep working while the agent clicks and types elsewhere.

Google lists several controls:

- users pick which apps Gemini is allowed to access
- every step can be watched live and paused at any time
- key actions need user approval before they happen

## What Google suggests using it for

The company's examples include batch color-grading photos in design software, pulling weights and addresses out of local PDFs to fill in shipping quote forms, building a Google Slides deck from live dashboard metrics and screenshots, and staging resale listings from a folder of clothing photos.

## Availability

The feature is rolling out in English in the US. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later and a personal Google account for someone 18 or older, and it is limited to Google AI Plus, Pro and Ultra subscribers.
