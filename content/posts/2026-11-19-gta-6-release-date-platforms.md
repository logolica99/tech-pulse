**Short answer: it is already out.** Grand Theft Auto VI was released on Thursday, November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That date is official: Rockstar Games and its parent company Take-Two Interactive announced on launch day that the game "is now available" on both consoles.

## What time does it unlock?

There is no single worldwide launch time. The game unlocks at midnight local time on November 19 in each country, CBS News reports, so players in New Zealand were in hours before players in Europe or the Americas.

Pre-loading opened on November 10 for people who pre-ordered. Sony's store page tells PS5 owners to clear at least 212 GB, and Rockstar's own guidance, as reported by CBS News and Polygon, says to set aside up to 212 GB depending on the console.

## How much does it cost?

The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99, according to CBS News. Sony's store lists the Ultimate Edition at $99.99 and says it adds exclusive vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops inside Jason and Lucia's story. Boxed retail copies contain a download code rather than the full game on a disc.

## What about PC?

There is no PC version at launch, and Rockstar has not announced one. CBS News says Rockstar did not respond when asked. Analyst Andrew Marok of Raymond James told CBS that Rockstar's past PC versions have arrived about 12 to 18 months after the console release, but that is history and analyst opinion, not a Rockstar plan.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony):** the November 19, 2026 release, the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S platforms, the November 10 pre-load and the 212 GB storage figure.
- **Reported, not from Rockstar:** the prices come from CBS News's reporting, and the PC timing is analyst speculation.
