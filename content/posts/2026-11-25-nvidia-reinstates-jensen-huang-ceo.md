Jensen Huang is back in charge of Nvidia. In an announcement dated November 23, the company said its board voted unanimously to reinstate him as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately. This follows our earlier report that [Huang said he would return as CEO](/posts/2026-11-17-jensen-huang-offers-to-return-nvidia).

## Why the board changed course

Nvidia says the decision came after a series of discussions and meetings with Huang, after which the board was satisfied with his candor and ability to lead. The company also says it received input from senior levels of the US government and, based on that, is confident his return poses no national security risk.

Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO, stays on as chief financial officer. Harvey Jones remains board chair.

## The investigation continues

The board said the independent-director committee investigating reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort will still finish its work, and that Huang supports it. Nvidia has also set up a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors and is searching for a new Chief Compliance Officer to oversee compliance across the company.
