**Short answer: press Options (PS5) or Menu (Xbox), go to the Game tab, choose Save Game and pick a slot.** You get 15 manual slots. You don't need to go to a bed or a safehouse like in older GTA games.

## Step by step

1. Open the pause menu.
2. Select **Game**. Output Lag says it holds Save Game, Load Game and New Game.
3. Select **Save Game** and choose an empty slot, or overwrite an old one.

Loading works the same way under **Game > Load Game**. Output Lag says each slot shows the current mission, your completion percentage, the date and time, and a screenshot.

## When you can't save

- **During a mission.** GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and TechRadar all say you can't save mid-mission. The pause menu offers Retry Checkpoint or Restart Mission instead.
- **During the Prologue.** Output Lag says Save Game stays greyed out until the opening is done, so a crash there sends you back to the start of it.
- **After using a cheat.** GamesRadar+ says cheats switch off saving until you quit and reload a clean save.
- **Right after a call or objective.** If it's greyed out in free roam, GamesRadar+ suggests leaving the building or vehicle you're in and trying again.

## How autosave works

GTA 6 autosaves after missions. GamesRadar+ and TechRadar say it also saves after big open-world moments, such as getting Wasted or Busted. There is only one autosave and each new one replaces the last, so it's a safety net, not a way to go back. Output Lag and GamesRadar+ describe it as a separate entry on top of your 15 manual slots, and Output Lag says you can't turn it off.

There's no in-game quit option, according to GamesRadar+. You close the game from the console home screen, so save first.

## Tips

- **Use more than one slot.** GamesRadar+ notes there's no way to replay missions, so an older manual save is the only way back to one.
- **Save before big choices.** GameSpot gives the example of the house renovation tasks early on, which affect Jason and Lucia's relationship and can't be redone later.
- **Save after long free-roam sessions.** Output Lag says autosave doesn't cover side activities between missions, so a crash can cost you that progress.

## Official or not?

- **Rockstar:** Rockstar hasn't published a save guide for GTA 6. Nothing here comes from a Rockstar statement.
- **Guides, not Rockstar:** the menu path, 15 slots, autosave behavior and the no-saving rules come from GamesRadar+, GameSpot, Output Lag and TechRadar, based on the launch version on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. GameSpot says the autosave uses one of your slots, while GamesRadar+ and Output Lag say it's a separate extra entry.
