**Short answer: Rockstar says that's partly by design. Rob Nelson told Destructoid that its data showed GTA 4 was "a little too punishing in places" and GTA 5 "perhaps too easy," so GTA 6 tries to land in the middle. If you want a challenge, Rockstar points you to the harder open-world content, such as compounds and fronts.**

This is an update to our earlier answer, [Does GTA 6 have difficulty settings?](/posts/2026-11-20-gta-6-difficulty-easy-mode). That's still true: there are no difficulty levels. What's new is that Rockstar has now explained how it tuned the game, after some players said it felt too easy.

## What Rockstar said

Speaking to Destructoid, as reported by GameSpot and GamingBolt on November 25:

- **Rob Nelson**, co-head of Rockstar North, said designers tend to start out with content that is too hard, then tune it down so most players can "enjoy, rather than endure" it. Because the game is so big, the team made some open-world content, "compounds and fronts for example", tougher for players who want something harder.
- **Scott Mitchell**, design producer, said the team made a conscious effort to keep fail states "appropriate" and not overused in missions. It wanted to leave mechanics like stealth open to players while keeping the big cinematic set pieces.
- GamingBolt adds that Nelson said players may find the story missions easy depending on their skill, and that options exist for players who find the game harder.

## Can you make it harder or easier?

There's no difficulty menu. GameSpot notes you can change things like aiming controls, and cheat codes can make the game easier or harder. Cheats only unlock after the story, as covered in our [cheat codes answer](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-cheat-codes).

## Official or not?

- **Official (developer interview):** The GTA 4 and GTA 5 comparison, the "middle" target and the harder compounds and fronts come from Rockstar's Rob Nelson and Scott Mitchell speaking to Destructoid. That's an interview, not a Rockstar Newswire post.
- **Player opinion:** The claim that GTA 6 is "too easy" comes from players' reactions, not from Rockstar. Rockstar hasn't announced a difficulty setting or any plan to change the game's difficulty in a patch.
