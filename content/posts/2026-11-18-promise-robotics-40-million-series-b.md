Promise Robotics, a company applying robots and AI to housing construction, has closed a Series B round of C$40 million. The round was led by White Star Capital, with Horizons Ventures, Radical Ventures and PSP Investments also taking part, according to the company's announcement.

## How it works

Promise does not sell robots to builders. Instead it runs what it calls Factory-as-a-Service: a builder hands over its existing home designs, Promise's software turns them into robotic production plans, and its factories produce precision structural components that are then installed on site. Builders pay for output rather than building and running their own factories.

The pitch is aimed at a labor problem. Housing demand is high, but adding capacity the traditional way depends on skilled trades that are in short supply.

## Where it stands

According to the company:

- it already produces in Calgary and Nisku, Alberta;
- it has more than 2,500 housing units under contract across three markets;
- it recently signed a multi-year agreement with D.R. Horton, the largest US homebuilder by volume;
- its first US facility, in Phoenix, is planned to open in 2027.

The new money will go toward expanding that production network across North America.
