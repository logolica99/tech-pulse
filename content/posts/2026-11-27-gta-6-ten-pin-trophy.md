**Short answer: get on any bicycle or motorcycle, ride into a crowd, and kick 10 pedestrians within 60 seconds. The kick is Square on PS5 or X on Xbox.**

## What the trophy says

Ten Pin is one of GTA 6's 28 hidden trophies and achievements, and it is a bronze. In the game, its only hint is "Bowl 'em over with a melee drive-by." Despite the name, it has nothing to do with bowling. (A different hidden trophy, Let's Go Bowling, covers the bowling alley.)

## How to unlock it

1. As Jason or Lucia, get on a pedal bike or a motorcycle. Any bike counts.
2. Ride to a crowded spot. Insider Gaming suggests the beach at Vice Beach in Vice-Dale County, where there are lots of people and open space to turn around.
3. Ride up close to people and press Square (PS5) or X (Xbox) to kick them as you pass.
4. Land 10 kicks inside one minute. The trophy pops as soon as the tenth kick lands.

It works anywhere, but a busy beach is easier than a city street full of cars and buildings. GTA6Walk adds that riding slowly through the crowd helps, because kicks land best when you pass close by.

## Is it missable?

No. Guides list it as not missable, and you can do it at any time in free roam, before or after the story. Kicking crowds is still a crime, so expect some police attention. Our guide on [losing the cops](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-wanted-level-lose-cops) helps with that, and our [trophy list answer](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-trophies-achievements) covers all 55 trophies.

## Official or not?

- **Official (in the game):** the trophy name Ten Pin and its description, "Bowl 'em over with a melee drive-by," come from the game's own trophy list.
- **From guides:** the exact rule (10 kicks inside 60 seconds) and the button prompts come from players' guides, mainly Insider Gaming and PowerPyx as cited by GTA6Walk. Rockstar has not published walkthroughs for its hidden trophies.
