**Short answer: not on PS4, and only by streaming on Xbox One.** GTA 6 runs natively only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, but they still have to buy the game.

## PS4: no

Sony's GTA 6 page is clear: the game "will only be playable on PlayStation 5 consoles" and "will not be available for PlayStation 4 consoles." Rockstar's launch page also lists only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Sony's page does not mention any cloud streaming option for PS4.

## Xbox One: yes, through the cloud

Microsoft's Xbox page says the game does not run natively on Xbox One, but Xbox One players can play it through Xbox Cloud Gaming. Xbox Wire announced on November 10 that streaming would be available from launch day, starting at 1am ET on November 19.

What you need, according to Microsoft:

- **Your own copy.** GTA 6 is not in Xbox Game Pass. You have to buy it to stream it.
- **Cloud playtime.** Game Pass members can use the cloud hours their plan includes. Without Game Pass, you can buy cloud playtime from the Xbox Store or play with ads.
- **Lower-end visuals.** Xbox Wire says the streamed version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings.

Microsoft adds that cloud gaming availability and purchase options vary by market and device, so check that it works in your country.

Cloud streaming also works on Xbox Series X|S, which helps if you don't want to give up the storage for a full install.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Sony):** GTA 6 is PS5-only and not available on PS4.
- **Official (Microsoft):** no native Xbox One version. Xbox One players can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming if they own it and have cloud playtime, with Series S graphics settings. The game is not in Game Pass.
- **Official (Rockstar):** the launch platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
