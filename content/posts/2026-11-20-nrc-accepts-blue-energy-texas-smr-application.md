The US Nuclear Regulatory Commission has accepted the first part of Blue Energy's construction permit application for its first power plant, in Victoria County, Texas. Acceptance means the NRC considers the application complete enough for a detailed safety and environmental review. It is not yet permission to build.

## Gas first, nuclear later

Blue Energy calls the design a "Blue Hybrid". The site would start on natural gas in 2028, with two GE Vernova 7HA.02 turbines delivering about 1 GW by 2030. From around 2032 it would add up to five GE Vernova Hitachi BWRX-300 small modular reactors for roughly another 1.5 GW, reusing the same balance-of-plant equipment built for the turbines. Large plant modules would be built off-site and delivered by barge.

The company says the NRC has also accepted this gas-to-nuclear approach as part of the review.

## Powering AI

The plant would supply behind-the-meter power to Crusoe's neighboring 1,600-acre AI data center campus and also connect to the Texas grid. The first phase is expected to cost more than $1 billion, with a final investment decision due in 2027. Blue Energy submitted Part 1 in September and plans to file Part 2 in the second half of 2027.

Founded in 2023 and spun out of MIT-linked research, Blue Energy has raised about $425 million, including a $380 million round in April.
