Uber and Nuro have started testing Lucid Gravity SUVs with no one behind the wheel on public streets in the San Francisco Bay Area, the companies said on November 24. Driverless rides for employees are coming soon, ahead of a commercial robotaxi launch targeted for the first quarter of 2027.

## How they got here

Nuro received a driverless testing permit from the California DMV in May 2026 and had spent nearly a year testing the Gravity robotaxi with a safety operator aboard. TechCrunch reports the companies are also testing with safety operators in Houston, and that the combined engineering fleet, about 100 vehicles earlier this year, is growing as Lucid starts building production robotaxis at its Arizona factory.

## The partnership

Uber announced the plan about 16 months ago. According to TechCrunch, Uber's investment in Lucid has grown from $300 million to $500 million, it agreed to invest nearly $500 million in Nuro, and it committed to buy at least 35,000 Gravity SUVs. Nuro supplies the self-driving system, built on cameras, solid-state lidar and radar, while Uber will own and run the fleet.
