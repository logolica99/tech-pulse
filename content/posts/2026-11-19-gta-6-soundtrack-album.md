**Short answer: 34 new songs, released as Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.** Rockstar's official soundtrack album came out with the game on November 19, 2026, through Atlantic Records. Take-Two calls it 34 original tracks from "a genre-defying roster" of artists. It is on all major streaming services, and on vinyl or CD from the official store at gtavi-thealbum.com.

## Who is on it?

The official album store lists the full running order. The best-known names include:

- **Justice**, who opens the album with "Starting From Zero" (featuring Michael McDonald) and returns on "Flower In A Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard
- **Hip-hop:** Playboi Carti ("AHH YEA"), Travis Scott ("RHYNO"), Yung Lean with Future and Metro Boomin ("That's It"), Gunna, Young Thug, Kodak Black, Don Toliver, Cardi B ("Don't Chart"), JT and Tee Grizzley
- **Pop:** Dua Lipa ("Go Easy"), JENNIE ("Power Trip"), Tove Lo ("Blood In Your Eyes"), Zara Larsson, Camila Cabello, Rina Sawayama, Rose Gray and Lana Del Rey ("Crazy For You")
- **Latin:** JHAYCO, Fuerza Regida ("Suzuki"), Rauw Alejandro ("Macacoa 2000"), Arca, Kali Uchis with Helado Negro, and Omar Apollo
- **Others:** Morgan Wallen ("Last Thing You Need"), Burna Boy ("Bigger"), Keith Richards ("Bright Lights, Big City"), and a closing jazz track, "Overtown Lights," by Stanley Clarke, Yussef Dayes and Robert Glasper

## Were any songs out before launch?

Yes. GAME MODE reports that ten tracks came out as singles before the game, the last being Tove Lo's "Blood In Your Eyes" on November 10. GTA Culture reports the full order first surfaced when a store worker posted an early vinyl copy in mid-November. The official store now shows the same list.

## How to get it

- **Streaming:** Take-Two said it would go live at midnight on November 19 on all major services.
- **Vinyl and CD:** sold at gtavi-thealbum.com. The store says the vinyl is two transparent magenta discs with blue splatter.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Take-Two, official album store):** 34 original tracks, Atlantic Records, the November 19 release, streaming plus vinyl and CD, and the full tracklist.
- **Reported:** which songs were released early as singles and how the tracklist first leaked come from GAME MODE and GTA Culture. This post does not say which songs play on which in-game radio station; Rockstar has not listed that on the album page.
