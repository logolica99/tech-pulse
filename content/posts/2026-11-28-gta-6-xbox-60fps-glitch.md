**Short answer: sort of. A Quick Resume trick on Xbox Series X and Series S removes GTA 6's 30fps cap, but it doesn't give you 60fps. In Digital Foundry's tests on Series X, the game mostly ran between 30 and 40fps and averaged about 36fps, and gameplay can run faster than normal.**

## What the glitch is

GTA 6 is capped at 30fps on every console (see [our earlier answer on 30fps vs 60fps](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-30fps-60fps-performance)). Shortly after launch, Reddit user Meowwie64 found a way around the cap on Xbox. It uses Quick Resume and the console's display settings, so it doesn't work on PS5. You also need a display that supports 120Hz.

## How people do it

The steps reported by RockstarINTEL, Pure Xbox and Digital Foundry:

1. In the Xbox TV and display settings, set the output to 60Hz.
2. Start GTA 6.
3. When the six stars show up in the intro, switch the output back to 120Hz.
4. Without quitting GTA 6, open the Xbox Guide and start a different game that isn't already in Quick Resume.
5. Switch back to GTA 6. The frame rate should now be unlocked.

Digital Foundry noticed the trick also works the other way: going from 120Hz to 60Hz drops the game to 15fps.

## What you actually get

- **No steady 60fps.** On Series X, Digital Foundry mostly measured 30 to 40fps, with an average of about 36fps in the first hour. It got close to 40fps in quiet countryside or at sea, and only approached 60fps when looking straight up over the ocean. Busy city areas, fast driving and explosions still dropped below 30fps.
- **Ignore "90fps" claims.** Digital Foundry says reports of near-60 or higher frame rates likely come from inaccurate frame counters, or from a TV's VRR readout doubling the number.
- **Gameplay speeds up.** Cutscenes play normally, but gameplay can run faster as the frame rate rises. RockstarINTEL and Pure Xbox describe some animations running at double speed.
- **Less input lag.** In one Series X test with VRR on, Digital Foundry measured about 170ms of input lag at 30fps and about 120ms in a scene running near 40fps.
- **Series S gains little.** The trick works there too, but the game mostly goes above 30fps only in calm scenes and cutscenes.

Pure Xbox points out that the game was never meant to run this way, so crashes or bugs are possible. RockstarINTEL reported that the November 23 update ([patch 01.002.002](/posts/2026-11-25-gta-6-update-01-002-002-patch-notes)) didn't block the glitch. Digital Foundry thinks Microsoft or Rockstar may fix it eventually.

## Official or not?

Rockstar's only official statement is the 30fps cap on all consoles. The glitch is unintended and unsupported: Rockstar and Microsoft haven't commented on it. The steps and measurements above come from a Reddit user and from Digital Foundry, RockstarINTEL and Pure Xbox. Digital Foundry's conclusions are its own analysis: a 40fps mode might be possible, and a 60fps mode looks out of reach on current consoles.
