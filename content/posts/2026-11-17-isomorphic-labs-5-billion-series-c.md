Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug-discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind in 2021, said on Tuesday it has raised $5 billion in a Series C round.

## Who invested

According to the company, Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital co-led the round, joined by Andreessen Horowitz and several other new investors. Alphabet kept investing heavily, and every existing backer came back: Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund.

Isomorphic did not give a valuation. TechCrunch notes that Bloomberg reported earlier this month that the company was looking to raise at about $50 billion. The round follows a $2.1 billion Series B in May.

## What the money is for

Isomorphic says it is scaling its own drug-design engine, IsoDDE, and its pipeline of candidates designed with AI. TechCrunch reports that the engine uses a set of simulation models built on DeepMind's AlphaFold work. The company says its internal programs have produced "positive preclinical results" in oncology and immunology, and it plans to use the money to bring candidates into clinical trials and to work on more kinds of medicine. Isomorphic already has partnerships with Eli Lilly, Novartis and Johnson & Johnson.

This is the first raise since founder and CEO Demis Hassabis stepped down as head of DeepMind to focus on drug discovery. He now chairs DeepMind.
