**Short answer: buy one. The earliest masks are on the "Neckwear" shelf at Chuck's Tackle & Shot, the Ammu-Nation just down the road from Jason's house in Key Lento. Other gun stores and some clothing shops sell more. After you buy it, change its "Wear Style" so it covers your face. Bought and worn as-is, it just hangs around your neck.**

Appearance matters a lot more in GTA 6's Wanted system than in earlier games. Police track what you look like, so hiding your face before a robbery makes it harder for them to recognize you.

## Where to buy one

- **Chuck's Tackle & Shot (Key Lento).** This is the first shop that sells them. Look for the central Neckwear stand with an alligator head on top. GAMES.GG says the shop has two options, including a $35 bandana. It also says Chuck's won't sell you anything else until you've bought the longarm the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up asks for. So if you can't shop yet, do that step first.
- **Big Shots (Hamlet).** According to GAMES.GG, its Neckwear display has six options. The story brings you here at the end of the mission Unwanted Attention.
- **Any other Ammu-Nation.** To find them, open the map index and pick the Ammu-Nation (weapons) entry.
- **Vice City and Vice Beach clothing shops.** Thrift stores sell novelty masks, and high-end shops sell pricier face coverings. GAMES.GG says Stock 305, a shop only in the Ultimate Edition, sells ski masks and bandanas.

## How to put it on

1. Buy it with **Buy & Equip** so it goes straight into your loadout.
2. Hold **L1** (PS5) or **LB** (Xbox) to open the wheel, then press **R1/RB** to switch to Items.
3. Highlight the neckwear slot at the top of the wheel. Press **up on the D-pad** to set Wear Style to 1, which covers your face. Press down to lower it again.

Is a mask you own missing from the wheel? Go to a wardrobe at a residence and open Accessories, then Masks, to add it to your loadout. You can also save it with an outfit in your personal vehicle's trunk (**Square** on PS5, **X** on Xbox). GAMES.GG notes that Jason and Lucia each need their own mask.

Keep it lowered when you're shopping normally, because a covered face makes shop clerks panic. Taking it off after a getaway helps clear Appearance Heat. For the rest of the heat system, see [how to lose the cops in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-wanted-level-lose-cops). For what Ammu-Nation sells besides masks, see [how to unlock more guns](/posts/2026-11-21-gta-6-unlock-more-guns-ammu-nation).

## Official or not?

Rockstar has not published a mask guide. Everything here comes from what PC Gamer, GamesRadar+ and GAMES.GG found playing the PS5 launch version. The shop names, controls and Wear Style step match across all three. The prices, the six-option count at Big Shots, and the Gear Up purchase requirement come from GAMES.GG alone, and a patch could change them.
