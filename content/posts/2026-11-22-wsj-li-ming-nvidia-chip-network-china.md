A Chinese businessman facing fraud charges in Singapore was a central figure in a network of companies that supplied Nvidia-powered computing to Chinese customers, according to a Wall Street Journal report summarized by India Today. The report links him to Megaspeed, the Singapore company where Alice Huang, identified in reporting as the romantic partner of former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, was managing director.

## Who Li Ming is

Li Ming, 52, was charged with fraud by Singapore prosecutors in February 2025. Prosecutors allege he bought servers with Nvidia chips while telling the US supplier they were for his Singapore company, then shipped them to Malaysia and on to an unnamed third destination. He is out on bail and his lawyers declined to comment. Megaspeed was not named in those charges.

The WSJ says Li was closely involved in Megaspeed's operations and major decisions, and had worked for years with Alice Huang and her husband, Kang Wei. A 2023 photo described him as chief strategy officer of Century Longteng, a cloud venture the couple set up in 2021. Century Longteng was a key shareholder until 2024 in Shanghai's Century Litong, which, according to the WSJ's earlier reporting, received Megaspeed servers in violation of US export controls.

## The network after Megaspeed

Nvidia put Megaspeed on an internal "no sell" list in June 2025. Megaspeed says it operates lawfully and that Huang left in August 2025. The WSJ reports that the team moved on to a new company, Novagate Cloud, which shipment records show received at least $300 million of Nvidia-equipped hardware at a Malaysian entity in the first five months of 2026, and which has corporate links to companies associated with Century Litong. Novagate, Megaspeed, Century Litong and Century Longteng did not respond to the WSJ, and Huang and Kang could not be reached.
