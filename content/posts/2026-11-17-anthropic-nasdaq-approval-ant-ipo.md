Anthropic's public listing took another step on Tuesday. An amended S-1 filed with the SEC says the Claude maker's Class A common stock has been approved for the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT", and that the proposed price range is still $70 to $78 per share.

## The offering

The deal covers 825 million shares, including shares sold by the company and by existing holders. The Economic Times reports that Anthropic wants to raise up to $64 billion in the IPO plus another $26 billion through a concurrent private placement, and that the money is earmarked for general corporate purposes such as computing capacity, AI infrastructure and hiring. Citing Bloomberg News, it adds that bids are running at almost seven times the shares on offer and that existing investors are getting priority in allocations.

Stocktwits, in a story carried by Yahoo Finance, reports that pricing is expected Wednesday evening, with trading set to begin Thursday.

## Who owns what

According to the filing as summarized by Stocktwits, Amazon will hold 14.7% of Anthropic's total capital stock after the IPO and private placement, and Google will hold 13.7%, for 28.4% combined. Amazon's exposure is larger on paper: if its convertible notes were fully converted it would own about 23.8%, but the filing says Amazon has chosen not to waive an ownership cap. Both companies hold non-voting Class N stock, so control stays with Anthropic's founders through a separate share class. Google is also one of seven tech companies buying the $26 billion of shares in the private placement.
