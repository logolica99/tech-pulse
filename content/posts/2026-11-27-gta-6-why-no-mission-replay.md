**Short answer: Rockstar left it out on purpose. Senior design director Scott Butchard says mission replay "felt at odds with the story" and "really diminished the choices you had made."**

This is an update to our earlier answer, [Can you replay missions in GTA 6?](/posts/2026-11-20-gta-6-replay-missions), which covered the missing menu and the manual-save workaround. What's new is Rockstar's own explanation.

## What Rockstar said

In an email to Kotaku sent before launch and published on November 25, Butchard said Rockstar "certainly" looked at mission replay early in development, and that the game does "not currently support it." As the game came together, he said, the feature "felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected." Stepping outside the game to replay missions "really diminished the choices you had made."

## Why it's a change for the series

Replayable story missions arrived with GTA 4's The Ballad of Gay Tony and returned in GTA 5, where a medal system rewarded players for going back to hit extra objectives, Kotaku notes. GameSpot points out that Red Dead Redemption 2 had a replay option too.

## What you can still do

- **Load an earlier save.** Manual saves made before a mission are the only way to play it again. Our [save guide](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-how-to-save-game) has the steps.
- **Restart mid-mission.** If a mission is going badly, GameSpot says you can open the menu and pick retry, quit or restart.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Butchard's statement, given by Rockstar to Kotaku, that replay was considered and dropped for story reasons, and that it is not currently supported.
- **Not official:** Kotaku's read that Rockstar doesn't seem to plan to add it later. Rockstar has not announced or ruled out a replay feature in a future update.
