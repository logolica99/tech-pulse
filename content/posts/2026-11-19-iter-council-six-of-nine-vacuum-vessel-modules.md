The international ITER fusion project in southern France says its assembly work is running ahead of plan. The ITER Council, which met on November 18 and 19, said sector module sub-assembly and installation are ahead of the Level-1 schedule in the project's Baseline 2024 plan.

## Where assembly stands

Six of the nine vacuum vessel sector modules are now in place in the tokamak pit, and ITER expects a seventh to go in later this month. The organization also reports 212 Level-1 milestones achieved against a target of 202.

## Commissioning and testing

According to the Council statement, ITER has completed its first successful gyrotron pulses and started long-pulse operation. Gyrotrons are the high-power microwave sources used to heat the plasma. At the Magnet Cold Test Facility, another superconducting coil was cooled and energized.

## Licensing

ITER says talks with France's nuclear safety regulator, ASNR, are progressing. It has submitted a request to update the regulatory framework so operations can be authorized in stages, and it is preparing an updated Preliminary Safety Report planned for 2027. These figures and assessments come from the ITER Organization's own statement.
