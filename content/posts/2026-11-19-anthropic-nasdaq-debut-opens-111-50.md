Anthropic, the company behind the Claude chatbot, is now a public company. Its shares started trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT" shortly before noon Eastern time on Thursday, opening at $111.50, according to Bloomberg figures cited by CBC News. That is about 34% above the $83 IPO price set late Wednesday.

By 12:50 p.m. Eastern, the stock was up about 19% from its IPO price, CBC reported, while the broader Nasdaq and AI-linked names such as Nvidia, Micron and CoreWeave were slightly lower.

## The biggest IPO yet

The $83 price was above the $70 to $78 range Anthropic gave in filings earlier this month, and the company also increased the number of shares on offer. The offering raised about $81 billion and valued Anthropic at roughly $1.5 trillion, making it the largest IPO on record, ahead of SpaceX's raise of about $75 billion in June, AP and CBC report.

## Big promises, open questions

In its filings, Anthropic points to more than $40 trillion a year in economic value produced by knowledge workers worldwide as the opportunity for its technology. Coverage of the debut notes that investors are weighing that enthusiasm against the company's huge valuation, an uncertain path to profitability, and wider worries about an AI bubble.
