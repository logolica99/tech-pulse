**Short answer: yes. A wall of names at the Deep Roots Memorial Sanctuary, just north of Hamlet in Leonida, includes relatives of Rockstar developers who died during GTA 6's development. Two developers have confirmed three of the names.**

## Where it is

Rockstar QA developer Matt Green said in an Instagram post that the memorial sits "just north of Hamlet." Reports name the spot as the Deep Roots Memorial Sanctuary. You can walk up to it in free roam. Reports so far mention no mission marker, objective or reward tied to it. It is a place to visit, not a collectible.

## Whose names are on it

- **Bev Green**, Matt Green's mother. Her name appears with the date she died, March 30, 2026, about eight months before the game launched on November 19. Green wrote that she had been proud and excited to see his work but that he never got to show her the game, and that she was the one who finally agreed, as a kid, to buy him GTA: Vice City. "I'm incredibly grateful that she's able to be honoured inside the world of VI," he wrote.
- **Elhuntry Radcliff and Jade Radcliff**, the father and first daughter of production designer Terrance Radcliff. Both died during development, and their names sit side by side. "To have them be a part of such a huge moment in gaming history means everything to me," Radcliff posted on X on November 22.

## What about the other names?

Nobody has explained them yet. RockstarINTEL and The Tab both note that it is unclear whether every name on the wall belongs to a developer's loved one. For more hidden details, see our answer on [GTA 6 collectibles](/posts/2026-11-27-gta-6-how-many-collectibles).

## Official or not?

- **Confirmed by the people involved:** the three names above and why they are there come from public posts by Rockstar staff Matt Green (Instagram) and Terrance Radcliff (X), as reported by RockstarINTEL, games.gg and The Tab.
- **Not official from Rockstar:** Rockstar Games itself has not commented on the memorial. The sanctuary's name and exact location come from press reports. Any claim about who the other names belong to is unverified.
