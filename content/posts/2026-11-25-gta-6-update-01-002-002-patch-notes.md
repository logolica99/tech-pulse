**Short answer: GTA 6's first post-launch update, which came out on November 23, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, fixes seven bugs. They cover crashes, weather that stopped changing, autosaves that failed, phone calls that never came, a Wanted Level you couldn't lose, the gym deadlift bar, and partners who wouldn't hand over bags for fences. It adds no new content.**

## What changed since our last update post

When we answered [what the latest GTA 6 update is](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-latest-update-patch-notes) on launch day, Rockstar had not published any patch notes. That has now changed. On November 23 Rockstar posted a support page called "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Updates (PS5 / XBOX Series X|S)" with its first list of fixes.

## The seven fixes

Rockstar lists all of them under "General / Miscellaneous." In short, the update:

- fixes several issues that made the game crash
- fixes weather that did not change for long stretches
- fixes the game not autosaving correctly
- fixes several phone calls that did not trigger, including calls that move the story forward or unlock mission content
- fixes a bug where you could not lose your Wanted Level
- fixes a bug where you could not use the deadlift bar at the gym
- fixes partners not handing over bags, so you could not sell them to fences

IGN called the weather fix the most notable change, since players had complained that it was not changing as often as it should.

## Version number and download size

Rockstar's page gives no version number or file size. IGN and RockstarINTEL report the update as version 01.002.002 on PS5 (about 603.8 MB) and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S (about 1.6 GB).

## What the notes don't cover

The notes say nothing about frame rate, graphics modes, or new content. They also don't say whether an existing save that got stuck because of a missing phone call moves on by itself after the update. Make a fresh manual save after you install it to be safe.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** the seven fixes and the November 23 date, from Rockstar's customer support page.
- **Reported:** the version numbers (01.002.002 on PS5, 1.0.2.2 on Xbox) and the download sizes. These come from IGN and RockstarINTEL, not from Rockstar.
