**Short answer: no. GTA 6 doesn't let you buy a house or apartment. The homes Jason and Lucia live in are handed to them by the story. The only property you can buy is garages, through a phone app called Vice City Real Estate.**

## Homes come with the story

Buying property has been a staple of recent GTA games, but GTA 6 keeps Jason and Lucia's living situation tied to the plot. Polygon's list of phone apps says it plainly: you can't buy your own home. TheGamer adds that because the pair are on the run, money isn't spent on property other than garages.

That matches what Rockstar showed before launch. VICE notes that Rockstar's character page for Brian Heder says he lets Jason live at one of his properties in the Leonida Keys rent-free, in exchange for help with local shakedowns. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson also told IGN that GTA 6's safehouses are closer to Red Dead Redemption 2's camps, in how interactive and lived-in they feel.

## What you can buy: garages

1. **Play to Chapter 2 and finish Daily Driver.** In this story mission you steal an '82 Crawler for Moses. Polygon and TheGamer both say the Vice City Real Estate app unlocks after it.
2. **Open your phone.** Press up on the D-pad, then pick Vice City Real Estate.
3. **Buy a garage.** Each one adds vehicle space, so you can keep more of the cars you register.

TheGamer counts 12 garages in total. Eight are sold in the app and the rest are tied to the story. Buying all eight costs $160,000 in game money and unlocks the Little Boxes trophy or achievement.

## Is it worth it?

If you like collecting cars, yes. Every registered vehicle needs a free slot, and TheGamer says it's easy to end up with more cars than space. See our guide to [keeping a stolen car](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-keep-register-stolen-car) for how registration works, and [making money fast](/posts/2026-11-20-gta-6-make-money-fast) if $160,000 sounds like a lot.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** Jason living rent-free in a Brian Heder property comes from Rockstar's own character page, as reported by VICE. Rob Nelson's comparison to Red Dead Redemption 2's camps was made to IGN before launch.
- **Reported by outlets that played it:** no house for sale, the Vice City Real Estate app, the Daily Driver unlock, the 12 and 8 garage counts and the $160,000 total all come from Polygon and TheGamer, based on the launch version. Rockstar hasn't said whether a future update could add property to buy.
