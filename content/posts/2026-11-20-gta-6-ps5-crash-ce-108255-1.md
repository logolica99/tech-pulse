**Short answer: CE-108255-1 is a general PS5 error that means a game or app crashed. Some players are hitting it over and over in GTA 6, and so far there is no official fix. Sony says to update the game, Rockstar hasn't commented, and no patch for it has been announced.**

## What players are seeing

GameSpot and GamesRadar+ both report players on PS5 getting error CE-108255-1, either mid-game or when the game won't load at all. Many say it happens again and again at the same point early in the story, which stops them from moving forward. One player quoted by GamesRadar+ couldn't get past the opening cutscene.

GamesRadar+ says the reports it found came from owners of the original, digital and Slim PS5 models, and that it couldn't find recent reports of the same error in other games. Some players online think GTA 6 is so demanding that it exposes older consoles that weren't fully stable, GameSpot notes, but that is a theory, not a confirmed cause.

Both outlets stress it doesn't look widespread. GamesRadar+'s own tester had no crashes in several hours.

## What you can try

- **Update the game.** That is Sony's official advice for CE-108255-1. Our post on [the latest GTA 6 update](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-latest-update-patch-notes) has the current version.
- **The usual PS5 fixes.** Players quoted by GamesRadar+ tried clearing the cache, rebuilding the database, reinstalling and restoring licenses. Some say none of it helped, so don't count on it.
- **Save often.** A crash loses anything since your last save. See [how to save in GTA 6](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-how-to-save-game).
- **Contact support.** GameSpot says you can file a ticket with Rockstar Support. One player said Rockstar sent them to Sony instead.

Sony's error page also points to PlayStation Repairs if updating doesn't work. GamesRadar+ says one player was quoted £300 for a repair, so it's worth waiting for a patch before going that route.

## Official or not?

- **Sony, official:** CE-108255-1 means "an error occurred with the game/application." Its advice is to update the game, then visit PlayStation Repairs.
- **Rockstar:** no public statement on this crash yet, according to GameSpot. Its GTA 6 troubleshooting page only links to PlayStation and Xbox support. No patch for it has been announced.
- **Player reports, not confirmed:** how common the crash is, which PS5 models are affected and the idea that older hardware is to blame come from Reddit and social posts gathered by GameSpot and GamesRadar+.
