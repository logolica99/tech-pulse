**Short answer: yes, but it costs money.** GTA 6 doesn't let you pick a spot on the map and teleport for free. Polygon, GameSpot and VGC all say you fast travel by taking a ride and paying extra to skip the trip.

## RydeMe and Paradise Taxi

The main way is the RydeMe app, the game's version of Uber. VGC says it unlocks during the early story mission Buck Me, because it needs a bank account, which you get in that mission. After that the app appears on your phone (press Up on the D-pad).

In RydeMe you can choose your safehouse, your map marker, or a new spot on the map. The price shows before you book, and it goes up with distance. Wait for the car, get in, and you can either ride there in real time or pay **$100 extra to skip the ride**. Polygon says the money comes out of your BuckMe checking account.

Before RydeMe unlocks, you can call Paradise Taxi from your phone contacts. GameSpot says it works the same way once you're in the car. You can also pay $5 to make the driver go faster. The difference is that the taxi only shows the fare after you get in.

Things that can go wrong:

- Stand near a road. The guides say a ride gets refused or canceled if no car can reach you.
- Don't attack your driver. Polygon says that locks you out of RydeMe for a while.

## Buses and trains

Buses and trains are cheaper. Polygon says you find the nearest stop or station in the map's vehicle tab, wait for it to arrive, sit down and choose "skip journey". Then pick a stop and pay a fee that depends on how far it is. The catch: you can only arrive at stops along the line, and you may wait a long time for the bus or train to come.

## Other shortcuts

- **Switch characters.** Later in the story you unlock switching between Jason and Lucia. Switching puts you wherever the other character is, often their safehouse. Polygon says you can also choose "meet up" to bring your partner to you.
- **Have your car brought to you.** Polygon says the Scooter Bros app delivers your vehicle to you for $100 plus a fee that depends on distance.
- **Reload the game.** Polygon says that if you quit and restart, you usually load in at home, in a town or on a road. That helps if you're stuck in the wild.

## Official or not?

- **From the game, as tested by outlets:** Polygon, GameSpot and VGC played the game and agree on the main points: no free fast travel, the $100 fee to skip RydeMe and taxi rides, cheaper skips on buses and trains, and character switching as a shortcut.
- **Not from Rockstar:** we found no Rockstar post about fast travel. Exact fares and when each option unlocks come from the guides' own play and may vary.
