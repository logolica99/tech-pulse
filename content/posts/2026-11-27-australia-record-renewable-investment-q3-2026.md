Australia's Clean Energy Regulator (CER) says investment in large-scale clean energy and home solar both hit records in the July to September quarter, according to its latest Quarterly Carbon Market Report.

## Large-scale projects

- **2.1 GW** of large-scale renewable capacity reached final investment decision in Q3, a new quarterly record.
- **Wind** had its strongest quarter since tracking began, with 1.1 GW reaching a final investment decision.
- Further deals after the quarter ended lifted the **year-to-date total to a record 5.5 GW**.

## Homes and batteries

- A record **890 MW** of rooftop solar went in during Q3, across about 90,000 systems.
- More than **10 GWh** of home battery capacity installed through the end of Q3 has been approved under the Cheaper Home Batteries Program.

CER Chair Tony Chappel said homes with solar and batteries are drawing less from the grid during the evening peak. He said average Q3 wholesale spot prices in the National Electricity Market were the lowest since 2021, helped by lower evening demand, more large batteries and less price volatility.
