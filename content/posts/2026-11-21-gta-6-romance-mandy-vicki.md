**Short answer: no. The only romance in GTA 6 is the one between Jason and Lucia, and even that is optional.** Mandy, Vicki and every other character are off the table.

## Mandy

Mandy is one of the tenants Jason visits to collect rent in the Chapter 1 mission "Pipework". She flirts with him openly, but GameSpot says Jason can never steer the conversation toward romance. When she asks him to stay at the end, he says no, and you get no choice in it.

GameSpot adds that you can drop by her house again later and the flirting may pick up where it left off. It still never becomes a real romance.

## Vicki

Lucia meets Vicki in Chapter 2. VGC says you can't romance her, whatever your relationship level is. She appears in person for only a few missions, though she keeps texting and calling afterwards.

## Can you cheat on Lucia?

No. VICE says Jason can only romance Lucia, and Lucia can only romance Jason. You can ignore the relationship completely if you want. VICE also notes that Jason can still visit strip clubs alone, and that doesn't affect things with Lucia.

## How the Jason and Lucia romance works

According to VICE, the relationship level goes up the more time the two spend together. A higher level unlocks extra moments at the safehouse, marked by a heart icon over the safehouse on the map. Those scenes play out as cutscenes. You can't trigger them whenever you like.

## Official or not?

Rockstar hasn't published a list of romance options. Everything above comes from guides written by GameSpot, VGC and VICE after playing the game. They all agree, but none of it is a Rockstar statement.
