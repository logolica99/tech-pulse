Elon Musk says Anthropic wants much more computing power from SpaceX. Speaking with Andrew Ross Sorkin at The New York Times DealBook Summit on Nov. 18, the day Anthropic priced its record IPO, Musk said Anthropic told him its "highest probability outcome" is that it would like an additional 10 gigawatts of compute from SpaceX. "So that's a big number," he said. Anthropic has not publicly confirmed the figure.

## Why it matters

Ten gigawatts is roughly 30 times the scale of Anthropic's first SpaceX deal. In May, Anthropic said taking over SpaceX's entire Colossus 1 data center would give it more than 300 megawatts of new capacity and over 220,000 Nvidia chips. According to AI Stock Wire, SpaceX ended September with 1.7 GW of nameplate compute capacity, and Musk said in October he was confident SpaceX could add 10 GW across 2027.

Anthropic's Nov. 17 IPO filing says it agreed to spend up to $120.5 billion with SpaceX's AI unit on terms running into 2029. The contracts have non-cancelable periods ending in January and March 2027, after which either side can exit with 90 days' notice.

## Pushing back on exit talk

Musk's comments answer a report circulated last week, based on unconfirmed notes from an analyst meeting, that Anthropic would not extend its SpaceX contracts past mid-2027. Musk said he thinks that is unlikely, and that Anthropic told him it is "extremely likely" to keep renting and wants "significantly more." He added that Anthropic has to plan for a worst case in which strict limits on AI would leave it unable to use the capacity.

Musk also said he met many Anthropic staff before agreeing to rent them compute, called them "principled," and praised its Opus and Fable models. He noted the deals are short-term and said he would end them if he felt Anthropic was "doing something really terrible for the world."
