Anthropic set the price of its initial public offering on Wednesday at $83 per Class A share, above the $70 to $78 range in its earlier filings. The Claude maker says the offering is 980 million shares: about 956.4 million sold by the company and about 23.6 million sold by existing stockholders, whose proceeds go to them rather than to Anthropic.

## A record raise

NPR puts the company's own take at roughly $79.4 billion and the full offering at more than $81.3 billion, ahead of the $75 billion SpaceX raised in its IPO earlier this year. NPR also reports the deal was oversubscribed and that both the share count and the price beat the original plan of 825 million shares at $70 to $78.

## What happens next

According to Anthropic, the SEC declared its registration statement effective on November 18. The stock is expected to start trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19 under the ticker ANT, and the offering is expected to close on November 20. Underwriters have a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million more shares. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are named as contacts for the prospectus.

## Compute on the side

Speaking at the New York Times DealBook Summit the same day, Elon Musk said SpaceX rents computing capacity to Anthropic under short-term agreements either side can end, and that Anthropic has expressed interest in an additional 10 gigawatts, CNBC reports. Anthropic has not publicly confirmed that figure.
