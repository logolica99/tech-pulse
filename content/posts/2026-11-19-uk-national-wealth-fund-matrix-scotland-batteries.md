The UK's state-backed National Wealth Fund is putting equity into two large battery storage projects in Scotland, at Eccles and Kilmarnock, alongside developer Matrix Renewables. Matrix keeps a majority stake in both.

## The projects

Together the two sites are planned at 1 GW of power and 2 GWh of storage. They sit along transmission corridors between Scotland and England, where the fund says they will help manage network constraints and soak up more renewable power, much of it Scottish wind.

- **Eccles:** construction is already underway, with commercial operation expected in October 2027.
- **Kilmarnock:** construction is due to start in December 2026, targeting operation in September 2028.

The announcement says the projects should create or support more than 200 jobs during construction and operation, and compares their storage capability to the annual electricity use of about 520,000 households.

## Why it matters

These are Matrix Renewables' first investments in the UK. Its CEO, Luis Sabaté, said the company is planning further multi-gigawatt investment in UK storage and flexible generation. For the National Wealth Fund, it extends a run of energy storage deals aimed at pulling private capital into the grid build-out.
