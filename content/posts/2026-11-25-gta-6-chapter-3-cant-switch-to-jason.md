**Short answer: it's a bug, not something you did wrong. Some players get stuck in Chapter 3 with Jason permanently grayed out in the character switch while playing as Lucia, and the next part of the story needs Jason. Rockstar has not fixed it yet. The workaround most likely to work is to load a manual save from before the bug and play a Jason mission first.**

## What the bug looks like

Polygon reports that after one of Lucia's Chapter 3 missions ("Missing Persons"), the option to switch to Jason can stay grayed out for good. Because the story then needs Jason, you can't move forward. Polygon says it is the most-viewed issue on GTA6Bugs, a site that collects bugs players report. Normally, [switching between Jason and Lucia](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-switch-characters-jason-lucia) works once free switching unlocks in Chapter 2.

## Fixes players are trying

Based on Polygon's report:

1. **Reload an older save (most reliable).** Load a manual save from before you drove to the Port Gellhorn warehouse for the final "Missing Persons" meeting. Switch to Jason and play his mission ("Dealer's Choice") until you can switch back to Lucia freely. Then make a manual save in a **new** slot and finish "Missing Persons."
2. **Other workarounds.** These worked for some players but not all: play another Lucia mission or a mission with both of them if one is available, call Dunk from Lucia's phone, or rob a store as a pair before you start Raul's job as Lucia.

Things that did **not** help the player whose clip spread the bug: leaving the house before switching, saving and reloading the same save, asking Jason to meet up, and sleeping.

## Did the latest patch fix it?

No, as far as anyone can tell. Rockstar's notes for the November 23 update ([what update 01.002.002 fixed](/posts/2026-11-25-gta-6-update-01-002-002-patch-notes)) don't mention character switching. They do fix "phone calls not triggering correctly including those that progressed the story", which is a different problem. Polygon says the update didn't seem to fix this softlock.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** the November 23 patch notes, which say nothing about this bug. Rockstar has not commented on the softlock publicly.
- **Player reports:** the bug and every workaround above come from players, as reported by Polygon. None of them is guaranteed to work.
