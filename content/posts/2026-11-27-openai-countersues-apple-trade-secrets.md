OpenAI hit back at Apple on Friday, filing counterclaims in California federal court in the trade-secret fight between the two companies, Reuters reports.

## What OpenAI alleges

- **A rehired employee took its plans.** OpenAI says Apple rehired a former employee who had spent six months at OpenAI, where he had access to details of a new device and OpenAI's roadmap for future hardware. OpenAI's filing suggests taking those documents "may have been his reason for joining OpenAI in the first place."
- **Pressure on suppliers.** OpenAI says Apple discouraged hardware suppliers from working on future OpenAI devices, which it describes as AI-enabled personal electronics that could "outcompete Apple on what Apple views as its own turf."
- **Employment practices.** OpenAI argues Apple encourages staff to work on personal Apple devices and accounts, then keeps access after they leave, letting it "monitor and ensnare departing employees." It says about 400 people have moved from Apple to OpenAI so far.

## Apple's response

Apple denied using the employee to get OpenAI's confidential information and denied trying to suppress competition in AI device manufacturing. It called the counterclaims "a transparent attempt to distract" from its own lawsuit.

## Background

Apple sued OpenAI and former Apple employees Tang Tan and Chang Liu in July, alleging they misappropriated trade secrets to help OpenAI's move into consumer hardware. Tan, a longtime Apple product design vice president, was part of the group behind io Products, the device startup OpenAI acquired in 2025. OpenAI and the two employees deny the claims.
