**Short answer: 55.** GTA 6 has 55 trophies on PS5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. The same awards are achievements on Xbox Series X|S, worth 1,000 Gamerscore in total. The Platinum is called "Vice Got a Price!" and unlocks when you have every other trophy.

## What the list looks like

According to TrueTrophies, 28 of the 55 trophies are hidden. Many of them are tied to the story, and their names hint at missions, so we won't list them here. GamesRadar has the full list if you don't mind mild spoilers.

The two Gold trophies are for finishing the final story mission and for "A Long Road", which asks for 100% completion. The six Silvers ask for bigger side goals:

- **Cunning Stunts:** complete 250 stunts
- **The Shootist:** earn gold in every shooting range challenge
- **Strangers & Freaks:** finish 8 mission strands for strangers
- **Don't Touch My Deck:** complete 3 collectible card sets
- **You WAiNKer!:** use WAiNK It at 100 locations
- **Maximum Withdrawal:** rob 5 bank vaults and escape

The Bronzes cover story chapters, side characters, races, fishing, robberies, spending money, buying all garages and exploring all of Leonida.

On Xbox, the Platinum's counterpart is worth 0G, according to GamesRadar's list. The 100% completion achievement is worth the most, at 100G.

## How long does the Platinum take?

Push Square estimates 250 hours or more and rates the difficulty 4 out of 10. USA Today cites an Inside Gamer estimate of about 200 hours. "A Long Road" (100% completion) takes most of that time. Push Square says the 100% checklist appears after you finish the story, and you can keep playing after the credits.

Push Square's other notes:

- All 55 trophies are offline. There are no online trophies.
- One playthrough is enough.
- Difficulty and assist options don't affect trophies.
- Using cheats disables trophies, so don't use cheats on a save you want to Platinum.
- There are 15 manual save slots plus an autosave.

## Official or not?

- **From the game's trophy list:** the 55 trophy names, descriptions, grades and Gamerscore values, as published by TrueTrophies and GamesRadar before launch.
- **Guide estimates, not from Rockstar:** the 200 to 250+ hour Platinum time, the 4/10 difficulty and the notes on cheats and saves come from Push Square, Inside Gamer and other guides. They may change as players finish the game.
- **Rockstar** has not published its own trophy guide.
