Spain's PLD Space has its first paying launch customer. The company said on Nov. 18 that it signed a contract with Japan's Mitsubishi Electric to carry a small satellite to orbit on its MIURA 5 rocket, Reuters reported.

## The mission

The satellite will ride on MIURA 5's second test flight to low Earth orbit. No date has been set, and the timing depends on how the rocket's first orbital test goes. PLD is aiming for that first flight in the first half of 2027 from French Guiana. Financial terms were not disclosed.

Mitsubishi Electric plans to use the satellite to help develop and run its own satellite megaconstellation. The Japanese company is already closely tied to PLD: it is a strategic partner and investor, and it led a €108 million extension of PLD's equity round announced in September.

## Why it matters

PLD flew Europe's first fully private suborbital rocket in 2023. A signed commercial customer is a meaningful step for a company that hasn't reached orbit yet, and it comes as Europe tries to build independent launch options for small satellites rather than relying on SpaceX.
