Spanish clean energy developer X-Elio, owned by Brookfield Renewable, has chosen e-Storage, the battery storage arm of Canadian Solar, to supply five grid battery projects. Together they add up to 590 MW of power and 1.43 GWh of storage capacity (DC).

Four of the projects are in Texas and one is in New South Wales, Australia. All five will use e-Storage's SolBank 3.0 battery system, along with power conversion equipment and plant controls built in Canadian Solar factories. SolBank 3.0 units hold about 5 MWh each and can be set up for 2-hour or 4-hour discharge.

One of the Texas sites, a 200 MW / 482 MWh project, will run on e-Storage's own North American-developed energy management software, EQ-S. The deal comes with a 22-year long-term service agreement, and all five projects are expected to start commercial operation within the next two years.

The agreements were signed at the RE+ 2026 clean energy conference in the US. They follow an October deal in which e-Storage agreed to supply a 100 MW / 200 MWh battery for OX2's Muswellbrook Solar Farm, also in New South Wales.
