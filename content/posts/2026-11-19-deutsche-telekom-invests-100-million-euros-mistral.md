Deutsche Telekom is buying into Europe's best-known AI lab. The German telecom group said on Thursday that T.Capital Tech Fund, its strategic investment arm, has put €100 million into Mistral AI's Series D round. dpa put the amount at about $110 million.

## A stake that comes with a plan

Telekom calls the investment a first step toward a future strategic partnership. Its side of the deal is sovereign infrastructure, existing customer relationships and integration work. Mistral brings its frontier models, enterprise software and deployment skills. The two say they want to help German businesses and public bodies adopt AI tailored to them, with a focus on industrial companies and regulated sectors.

The tie-up builds on Mistral's growing presence in Germany, including a recently opened hub in Munich. It also builds on Telekom's own AI infrastructure there. According to dpa, Telekom began running an AI factory in Munich in February and is spending almost another €250 million to double its computing power because demand is high. Siemens and Daimler Truck are among its users.

T.Capital manages more than €2 billion for the group. Mistral CFO Johan Bergqvist said the money is a strong signal that the two companies can bring AI that customers fully control to German industry at scale.

## Context

dpa notes that Mistral raised €3 billion in September as it positions itself as a European alternative to US labs such as OpenAI, Google and Anthropic. Telekom's €100 million is an addition to that Series D.
