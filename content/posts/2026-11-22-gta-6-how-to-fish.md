**Short answer: you can fish almost as soon as the game lets you roam as Jason. Pick up a rod from the small pier behind his house in Key Lento (or from his boat), equip it from the Equipment tab of the weapon wheel, hold L2/LT and press R2/RT to cast, press R2/RT when a fish bites, then push the left stick against the fish and rotate the right stick to reel it in.**

## Where to get a fishing rod

You don't have to buy one or unlock anything. GTABase and Shacknews both say fishing is available right away, unlike Red Dead Redemption 2, where it came later in the story. There are free rods on the jetty just west of Jason's home in Key Lento and on his boat, and Game Rant notes you'll often find more lying around docks where boats are moored. Game Checklists says no fishing licence is needed.

## Step by step (PS5 / Xbox)

1. **Equip the rod.** Near water, hold **L1/LB** to open the weapon wheel and pick the fishing rod from the **Equipment** tab.
2. **Find fish.** Click both sticks (**L3 + R3**) to use Focus. Areas with more fish show up as lighter patches in the water. Shacknews says this helps but isn't essential.
3. **Ready and cast.** Hold **L2/LT** to hold out the rod, then press or hold and release **R2/RT** to cast. GTABase says holding longer casts further.
4. **Attract a bite.** Slowly turn the right stick to reel a little and draw fish toward the lure.
5. **Hook it.** When a fish bites, press **R2/RT**.
6. **Tire it out and reel it in.** Push the left stick in the opposite direction to the way the fish is swimming. When it rests, rotate the right stick as the on-screen prompt shows to pull it in.

No bite? Reel in and cast again somewhere else.

## Lures and where to buy them

You start with a **Small Freshwater Lure** and a **Small Saltwater Lure**. Use freshwater lures in lakes and rivers, and saltwater lures off docks, along the coast and at sea. Better lures are sold at Ammu-Nation stores that stock fishing gear (they have a fish icon on the map), such as **Chuck's Tackle & Shot** near Jason's place. GTABase and Game Checklists list these prices:

- Medium Freshwater Lure: $45
- Medium Saltwater Lure: $56
- Large Saltwater Lure: $125
- Candy Cane Lure: not sold. It's found on a desk in a house southwest of Ambrosia.

Game Rant says bigger lures help with heavier fish, and that taking Jason's boat out into deeper water gives you better odds of landing them.

## How many fish are there, and can you keep them?

Guide sites count **35 species**, each with its own hotspot on the map. They show up under Progress > Animals > Fishing, and Game Checklists says catching all of them completes a challenge called Pro Angler. In free roam, fishing is catch and release, so your character always throws the fish back. Game Checklists says the only time you get a Keep option is on a story fishing trip with Cal.

There's also a trophy for it. Beating Cal in his fishing challenge (catch a Stoplight Parrotfish over 2 lbs in his marked spot) unlocks **Baited My Tackle**.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** Rockstar hasn't published a fishing guide. The controls above are the in-game prompts, as described by the outlets that played the game.
- **Reported by guide sites:** the rod locations, lure prices, the 35-species count, the Pro Angler challenge, the catch-and-release rule and the Baited My Tackle requirement come from GTABase, Shacknews, Game Rant and Game Checklists, not from a Rockstar statement.
