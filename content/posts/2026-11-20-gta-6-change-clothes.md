**Short answer: use the wardrobe at your home, or the changing room in any gym or clothing store. You can switch into any clothes you already own there without buying them again. To change on the go, save outfits to your car trunk first.**

## Where to change

- **At home:** your wardrobe.
- **Away from home:** the changing room at a gym or clothing store, usually at the back of the store.

On the map, Polygon says clothing stores have a pink T-shirt icon and gyms a blue weight icon. To find a certain kind of shop, open the map, go to Style, and pick a type such as sportswear or formal to see every matching store.

## Buying new clothes

Interact with the racks in a store to browse. Clothes you buy, or unlock through missions, go to your wardrobe and stay yours. You can change a whole outfit at once or go piece by piece: tops, bottoms, footwear and accessories. Tech4Gamers says you can save up to 15 favorite combinations as outfits.

## Changing from your car

1. At a wardrobe or changing room, add outfits to your car trunk. Each character can keep **five**, so ten in total.
2. When you need one, open the car's **Loadout**, go to **Items**, and pick a saved outfit.

Polygon says these trunk outfits are available from any car you own. If you're stuck without your car, you can have it delivered with the Scooter Bros app, but that can cost a few hundred dollars depending on distance and damage.

## Why it matters

Police in GTA 6 remember what you're wearing. Changing into a different outfit helps you slip past them, which is why it's worth having an outfit in the trunk before a job. More on that in [how to lose the cops](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-wanted-level-lose-cops).

## Official or not?

- **In the game:** wardrobes, changing rooms, the five-outfits-per-character trunk limit and the Loadout menu are how the game works, as described by Polygon and Tech4Gamers. Rockstar has not published a guide for this.
- **From guides only:** the 15 saved-outfit limit comes from Tech4Gamers, and the delivery cost estimate from Polygon.
