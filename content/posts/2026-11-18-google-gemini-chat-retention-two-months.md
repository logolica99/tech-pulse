Google is changing how the Gemini app handles conversations. Chat history and AI training, which were both tied to a single "Keep Activity" switch, are becoming separate settings, so people can keep their chat history while opting out of having those chats used to train Google's generative AI models.

## The trade-off

The same update lengthens retention for chats that are not saved to history. Google says that if the new History setting is off, future chats will be kept for around two months, and uses them to respond to users, process feedback and protect Google, its users and the public. TechCrunch notes that Google's Gemini Apps Privacy Hub previously listed a 72-hour retention period for these chats, and that temporary chats will also be kept for about two months.

Google frames the change as a safety measure as AI systems become more capable. According to TechCrunch, the updated Privacy Hub also cites potential system failures as a reason, and says temporary conversations are not used for model training. Google did not answer TechCrunch's questions about what prompted the change.

For comparison, TechCrunch says OpenAI may keep ChatGPT temporary chats for up to 30 days, and Anthropic keeps Claude incognito chats for 30 days by default.

## Rollout and defaults

The changes roll out globally in the Gemini app over the coming weeks. Users who had previously turned Keep Activity off will have both new settings off by default; those who had it on keep chat history enabled and can choose separately whether their chats are used for training.
