Google is opening up Tasks in Gemini, the mode that turns its chatbot into an assistant that works in the background on multi-step jobs, to everyone in six countries at no cost. The company says the free rollout reaches hundreds of millions of people in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India, and runs on its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

## What Tasks does

Instead of just answering questions, Tasks carries out work and notifies you when it is done, such as scheduling a daily briefing from your email and calendar, planning a trip itinerary, or automating repetitive research. Google says it can phone businesses to book appointments, sign into websites using Google Password Manager when you allow it, and complete purchases through Google Pay. Users switch it on with a toggle from Chat to Tasks, according to TechCrunch.

In Google's announcement, Gemini VP Josh Woodward describes Gemini calling two car dealerships at once to book a service slot, and analysing past utility bills to find why an electric bill spiked. Google says Gemini discloses that it is an AI when it calls, and businesses can opt out.

## Payments and history

For shopping, Google says Gemini uses the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) with tokenized cards from Mastercard and Visa, with American Express coming soon, and lets users set a hard spending cap. Receipts go to Google Wallet, which Google says is expanding to iOS.

TechCrunch notes the feature began in May as a tester-only 24/7 agent called Gemini Spark, then reached U.S. Google AI Ultra subscribers before becoming free.
