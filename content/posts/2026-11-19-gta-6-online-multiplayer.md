**Short answer: not at launch.** GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game. PC Gamer describes it as launching "as a singleplayer-only console game," and Sony's store page lists it as a one-player game. Rockstar has not announced a GTA Online for GTA 6 or a date for one.

## What Rockstar and Take-Two have said

PCGamesN reports that Rockstar has stated the GTA 6 it is selling "is a single-player experience." Copies come with a month of Rockstar's GTA+ subscription, which includes bonuses for the existing GTA Online.

On the existing game, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said in August that the company expects "to support GTA Online going forward," according to PCGamesN. So the GTA 5 version of GTA Online keeps running for now.

## When could online come?

No date exists. Reuters reports that some industry analysts expect the online mode, along with a PC version, in 2027, and that investors see the timing and size of the next GTA Online as a key question for Take-Two. In August, Bloomberg's Jason Schreier said in a video that Rockstar was not planning to talk about the new GTA Online any time soon, PCGamesN reports. He did not cite a source.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** GTA 6 launched without online play, and Take-Two says it will keep supporting the current GTA Online.
- **Not confirmed:** any GTA 6 online mode, its features or its timing. The 2027 estimate is analyst opinion.
