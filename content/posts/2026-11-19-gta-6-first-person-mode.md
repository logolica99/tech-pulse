**Short answer: no. GTA 6 has no full first-person mode.** You play in third person. There are a few short first-person views, and players found a glitch that forces first person, but Rockstar has not added a proper mode.

## What Rockstar said

In August, VICE reported that Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told YouTuber Davy Jones the game would not have a full first-person mode at launch. Nelson said the team chose to focus on third-person animation. VICE says first-person views while aiming certain weapons were still planned.

For comparison, GTA 5 also launched without first person. Its first-person mode came a year later, with the 2014 PS4 and Xbox One versions.

## What first-person views you do get

- **A quick close look:** on foot, hold the left trigger (L2/LT) and click the stick. Engadget says to click the right stick; GamesRadar says to click both sticks. Either way you can't walk around in this view.
- **Scoped guns:** GamesRadar says you can click R3 while aiming down a scope to aim in first person.
- **The phone:** Engadget says a setting in the Camera menu shows your phone in first person, even while driving.
- **No cheat:** GamesRadar says no cheat code turns on first person.

Engadget also lists the third-person camera options: height and follow speed in the Camera settings, and three camera heights plus a cinematic camera when you hold down on the D-pad in a vehicle.

## The first-person glitch

On launch day, players found a way to lock the camera in first person. PC Gamer tested it and Dexerto covered it. The rough steps:

1. On PS5, open Settings, then Controller, and set the touchpad to the camera.
2. Get in a car and open your phone with up on the D-pad.
3. Close the phone with Circle (or B), then quickly press the camera button. Repeat if it doesn't stick.

PC Gamer says the result is fully playable but rough: your shoulders clip into the view when you aim a long gun. There is no button to go back. To return to third person, save and reload that save.

This is a glitch, not a feature. Rockstar could patch it out at any time.

## Official or not?

- **From Rockstar:** no full first-person mode at launch, with the focus on third person. This was said in an interview with a YouTuber, as reported by VICE, not in a Rockstar press release.
- **From the game, as tested by outlets:** the close-up, scope and phone views come from GamesRadar and Engadget. The glitch comes from players and was tested by PC Gamer and covered by Dexerto.
- **Unknown:** Rockstar has not said whether a full first-person mode will come in a later update. Outlets that call it likely or unlikely are guessing.
