Dcubed, a German company that makes release actuators, deployable hardware and solar arrays for spacecraft, has raised €27.2 million in a Series A+ round. Warsaw Equity Group led the round, joined by the European Innovation Council Fund, the HTGF Opportunity Fund and existing investors including BayBG and Expansion.

## Off-the-shelf space hardware

Dcubed's pitch is to sell space components that are ready to order rather than custom-built for each mission, cutting the long lead times and complex supply chains common in the industry. The company plans to use the money to expand production in Germany and the United States, aiming to quadruple its manufacturing capacity, and to invest in automation, staff and supply chains.

## Cheaper power in orbit

The other priority is ARAQYS, a new family of solar arrays and power systems. Its first design, HALO, is a radial, origami-folded array that packs a large solar surface into a small launch volume. By using widely available silicon solar cells, Dcubed says it aims to cut the cost of space-based power by one to two orders of magnitude and move from kilowatt-scale arrays to ones producing tens or even hundreds of kilowatts.

The first HALO system is set to fly in 2027 on a SpaceX rideshare as part of ARAQYS-D3, a 2 kW in-orbit demonstration that will also deploy a 10-meter boom manufactured in space. Five power-beaming partners are expected to take part.
