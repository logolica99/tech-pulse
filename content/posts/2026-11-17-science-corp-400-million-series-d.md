Science Corporation has raised a $400 million Series D, the company announced on November 17. Khosla Ventures, the Ackman Oxman Institute, Tenet Healthcare, 885 Capital, Y Combinator and PLC Neurotech are among the investors. Science says it has now raised more than $900 million since it was founded in 2021. TechCrunch, citing a person familiar with the deal, reports that the round values the company at $3.5 billion.

## What PRIMA does

Science was founded by Max Hodak, a former president of Neuralink. Its first product is PRIMA, an implantable chip paired with wearable glasses. The company describes it as the first CE-marked brain-computer interface that restores "form vision," meaning the ability to read letters, numbers and words, for some patients blinded by late-stage macular degeneration. PRIMA went on sale in Europe a few months ago, and TechCrunch reports it is available in Germany. Hodak also said the device is now being trialed in patients with Stargardt disease, another condition that causes blindness.

## What the money is for

Science says it will use the funding to:

- Make PRIMA available in more of Europe.
- Prepare a US launch. The company is seeking FDA approval through the Humanitarian Device Exemption pathway, which is meant for rare conditions.
- Expand research, manufacturing and operations.
- Invest more in its biohybrid program, a neural interface that combines engineered neurons with the brain's own circuits. TechCrunch reports that Science is designing first human trials with Yale neurosurgeon Murat Günel.
