**Short answer: Port Gellhorn, the Chapter 2 area of GTA 6, has three safes at Illegal Operations. The codes are 6823 (Brooks Boatworks), 6969 (La Cascara Motel) and 0300 (Kingpin Bowling). If you don't have a code, a Sticky Bomb opens the safe, but it's loud.**

## The codes

| Illegal Operation | Safe code |
| --- | --- |
| Brooks Boatworks | 6823 |
| La Cascara Motel | 6969 |
| Kingpin Bowling | 0300 |

VICE lists all three. PC Gamer, VGC and Game Rant all give 6969 for La Cascara Motel. PC Gamer says the codes are fixed, so they're the same in every game.

## Where to find them in the game

You can learn all three at once at the Brooks Boatworks Illegal Operation in Port Gellhorn:

1. Go in through the hole in the fence on the east side of the compound (VICE's route).
2. Climb the stairs of the grey building and go inside.
3. Find the map on the wall, next to the whiteboard.
4. Hold L2 on PS5 to study it.

VICE says this adds all three codes at once. After that, the right code shows on screen when you walk up to one of these safes.

## La Cascara Motel, the big one

The La Cascara safe is on the top floor of the run-down motel. You can reach it by the middle stairs or the fire escapes, and VGC warns there are gang members on most floors. PC Gamer calls it probably the most lucrative robbery in Port Gellhorn. Besides cash, it lists an LMG, a grenade launcher, a unique vehicle and drugs. In the room next to the safe there's a silenced SMG, the Scrapped VF5C. PC Gamer also suggests bringing Jason, because there's more loot than two stash bags can carry.

## No code? Use a Sticky Bomb

VICE says you can pick **Open with Explosive** at any locked safe if you have a Sticky Bomb. You'll often find them in crates at Illegal Operations, or you can buy them at Ammu-Nation and other gun shops. The blast alerts nearby enemies, and they can call the cops, so use the code if you want to stay quiet.

## Official or not?

- **Official:** Rockstar hasn't published safe codes or a list of robbery locations. The codes are in the game itself.
- **Player-found:** The codes, where to find them and the loot all come from hands-on guides by VICE, PC Gamer, VGC and Game Rant, and those guides agree on the codes. This post only covers Chapter 2; codes for other areas aren't listed here.
