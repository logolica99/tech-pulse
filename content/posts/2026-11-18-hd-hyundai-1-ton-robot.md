HD Hyundai Robotics has begun selling its own 1-ton-class industrial robot, the HDT1000-33, aiming at heavy-duty automation jobs that smaller arms cannot handle. The Korea Herald reports the company marked the first sale at an event on Wednesday at Hankook R&D's headquarters in Ansan, South Korea.

## What it does

The HDT1000-33 is a six-axis articulated arm with a payload of up to 1 ton. Its first job is in building engines for large construction equipment: loading heavy cylinder engine blocks into machining equipment and moving finished parts to the next production step. The company says it holds positioning accuracy within 0.1 millimeter even after thousands of repeated moves.

HD Hyundai Robotics started developing the model in February 2024, citing rising demand for robots that can handle bigger, heavier components as factories move to modular production. Beyond engines, it plans to target shipbuilding equipment, large auto parts, and EV battery packs.

CEO Kim Wan-soo said large robots and physical AI will be the company's two main growth drivers, and that automating heavy lifting should reduce workplace safety risks.
