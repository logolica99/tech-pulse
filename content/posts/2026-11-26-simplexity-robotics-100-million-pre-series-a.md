Simplexity Robotics, a 16-month-old company based in Suzhou, China, has raised $100 million in a pre-Series A round that values it at $1.6 billion, TechTarget reports. Ant Group led the round, joining existing investors including Tencent Holdings, HSG and Lanchi Ventures.

## A bigger bet in a cooling market

The round comes as money for Chinese robotics startups is slowing. Citing Bloomberg, TechTarget says robotics fundraising fell to about $1.6 billion in October, down 67% from its June peak. China dominates the hardware side of the market: according to Smart Analytics figures cited in the report, it accounted for 97% of humanoid robot shipments in the first three quarters of 2026. Officials have acknowledged the sector is overheated and warned of a bubble.

Analysts quoted by TechTarget read the large valuation as a sign of more selective investing. SAG founder Linda Sui said investors are moving from technology demos toward companies with strong physical AI capabilities, real commercial uses and scalable business models. Policy analyst Nathanael Cheng said China's planning agency has described the slowdown as consolidation rather than a retreat, and that he expects both continued investment and startup failures.
