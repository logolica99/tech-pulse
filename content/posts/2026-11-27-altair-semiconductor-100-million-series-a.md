Altair Semiconductor, which makes wireless modem chips for connected devices, has raised $100 million in a Series A round led by Maverick Silicon, SiliconANGLE reports. Pitango, which reportedly invested $50 million when Altair spun out of Sony earlier this year, also took part, and Sony is said to have kept a stake.

## The chip

Altair's flagship ALT1550 modem is built for devices like wearables and industrial sensors that need to send data over carrier networks without a phone's power draw. It supports:

- **5G NR eRedCap**, a lower-power 5G mode that can reach up to 233 megabits per second.
- **LTE-M** (up to about 1 Mbps) and **Cat 1bis**, both of which run on more widely available 4G networks.

The chip pairs a radio transceiver with a baseband processor, onboard memory and power management.

## Why it matters

As more AI features move into small connected devices, from smart glasses to sensors, they need efficient ways to reach the cloud. Altair is betting that low-power 5G modems will be a big part of that market.
