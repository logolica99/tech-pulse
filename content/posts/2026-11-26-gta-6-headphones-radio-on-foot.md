**Short answer: Buy a pair of headphones or earbuds at an electronics shop. One of the first you can reach is Zeke's Gadgets in Hamlet. Equip them from the item wheel, then hold left on the D-pad and press X (PS5) or A (Xbox) to turn the radio on. After that you can hear the stations and podcasts almost anywhere.**

GTA 6 doesn't tell you this. Normally the radio only plays in vehicles, so once you step out the music stops.

## Step by step

1. **Find a shop.** Shacknews says electronics shops sell headphones, and Zeke's Gadgets in Hamlet is one of the first you'll find. PC Gamer suggests opening the map directory and setting a route to Zeke's Gadgets.
2. **Buy a pair.** Shacknews says they cost $119 at Zeke's and come in six colors. PC Gamer puts the price at around $150, so expect a little over $100 depending on the model.
3. **Equip them.** PC Gamer says you put them on from the item wheel.
4. **Turn the radio on.** Shacknews says to hold left on the D-pad, as you would in a car, and press X on PS5 or A on Xbox. Press it again to switch the radio off.

Then the music and podcasts keep playing while you explore Leonida on foot.

## Related tips

PC Gamer also points out a couple of radio shortcuts in vehicles: double-tap left on the D-pad to turn the radio on or off. For the music itself, see [What songs are on the GTA 6 soundtrack?](/posts/2026-11-19-gta-6-soundtrack-album).

## Official or not?

- **Not from Rockstar:** Rockstar hasn't published a guide to this. The shop, the price and the controls come from Shacknews and PC Gamer playing the launch version on PS5. The two give different prices ($119 and around $150), so check the price in the shop.
- Default controls are listed. If you've remapped buttons, the input may be different.
