China's Shenzhou-24 lifted off on a Long March 2F from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center at 8:18 a.m. Beijing time on November 25 (4:18 p.m. PST on November 24). It docked with the front port of the Tianhe core module about three and a half hours later. China's crewed space agency says this is its first crewed mission with a foreign astronaut on board.

## The crew

- **Tang Hongbo**, commander and pilot
- **Wu Yi**, flight engineer
- **Muhammad Zeeshan Ali**, a Pakistani payload specialist and Pakistan's first orbital astronaut, now the first foreigner to board Tiangong

The Shenzhou-23 crew opened the hatch to welcome them.

## What happens next

Ali is on a short stay. He'll come home on Shenzhou-23 with two of that mission's crew. The other two Shenzhou-24 astronauts will stay about six months with Shenzhou-23's Zhu Yangzhu, who has already been up for six months and is staying on as part of a year-long-stay experiment.

More than 90 experiments and technology tests are planned, including several joint projects with foreign partners. Pakistan's space agency, Suparco, says its research aboard includes lightweight graphene-based composites for spacecraft radiation shielding, and "self-healing" carbon-fibre composites that repair their own micro-cracks.
