**Short answer: hold R2 (RT on Xbox) to build speed, then pull the left stick back to take off. Turn the nose with Square and Circle (X and B on Xbox), hold L2 (LT) to slow down, and click the left stick to raise or lower the landing gear. Helicopters climb with R2/RT, drop with L2/LT, and hover when you let go of everything.**

## Plane controls

These are the default buttons on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, according to the guides listed below:

| Action | PS5 | Xbox |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Throttle up | Hold R2 | Hold RT |
| Slow down | Hold L2 | Hold LT |
| Pitch and roll | Left stick | Left stick |
| Rudder (yaw) left / right | Hold Square / Circle | Hold X / B |
| Landing gear | L3 | Left stick click |
| Pick a weapon (armed aircraft) | Hold L1 | Hold LB |
| Fire | R1 | RB |
| Get out | Triangle | Y |

If you played GTA 5, this is the change that will trip you up. The rudder is no longer on the bumpers. Output Lag found no way to put it back on L1/R1 at launch.

## Taking off and landing

1. Get in. Helicopters need a few seconds to spin up before they lift off.
2. Line the nose up with the runway or open water, using the rudder.
3. Hold R2/RT. Once you're moving, gently pull the left stick back.
4. Raise the gear with a left-stick click once you're in the air.

To land, line up early and fix small errors with the rudder. Let go of the throttle, hold L2/LT to lose speed, and come in shallow. On a runway, drop the gear first and keep the nose up until the wheels touch.

## Helicopters and parachutes

Hold R2/RT to climb and L2/LT to descend. Square/Circle (X/B) turn it on the spot. If you let go of the controls, the helicopter hovers by itself. GTABase says you get a parachute automatically whenever you board an aircraft. Open it with X (A on Xbox).

## Settings worth knowing

- **Pushing up makes you dive.** Output Lag says aircraft controls are inverted out of the box. If you want up to climb, turn off Aircraft Movement under Settings > Controller > Inverted Controls.
- **Reduced Vehicle Damage** in the Accessibility settings lets planes survive crashes that would normally destroy them. That helps while you're learning.

## Where to find an aircraft

Output Lag reports that airfields and helipads marked on the map usually have a plane or helicopter, and taking one doesn't give you a wanted level. The airstrips in Mariana County are an easy early pick. Stealing from Vice City International Airport or the Mile Zero Air Station military base does bring the police, and at the base that means a high wanted level. Your first flight comes during the story, in a seaplane.

## Official or not?

Rockstar hasn't published a flying guide for GTA VI. The button layout comes from the game's own on-screen prompts as written down by GTABase and Output Lag, and both guides list the same controls. The tips on settings and where aircraft spawn come from Output Lag's own testing and player reports, not from Rockstar.
