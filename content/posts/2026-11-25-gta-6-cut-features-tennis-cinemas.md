**Short answer: Rockstar worked on tennis for GTA 6 but dropped it because it wasn't happy with how it turned out. Cinema and theatre visits, crawling, furnishing safehouses and crafting were also considered and left out. Darts and bowling are missing for the same quality reasons.**

## What Rockstar said

In an interview with the German site PC Games, published November 25, Rockstar North's Rob Nelson and design producer Scott Mitchell went through features that were planned or discussed but did not make the finished game:

- **Tennis.** It was planned as a returning activity from GTA 5, but Rockstar wasn't satisfied with the result, so it was cut. Mini golf did make it in.
- **Cinemas and theatres.** Visits like the shows in Red Dead Redemption 2 were dropped because Rockstar felt they weren't interactive enough.
- **Crawling.** TheGamer reports that Rockstar considered it "at length." The game ships with crouching for stealth, but not crawling.
- **Furnishing safehouses and crafting.** Both were considered. PC Games says some of these ideas didn't fit the game's story or gameplay.

## What about darts and bowling?

Nelson covered those in an earlier interview with VGC on launch day, November 19. He said darts was "one we just didn't get to," that even Rockstar doesn't have unlimited time or money, and that "it's better not to do it than to do it and have it not be good enough." Bowling, which was in GTA 4, isn't in GTA 6 either. Nelson said activities now have to feel like an extension of the game's mechanics, and pointed to pool, weightlifting and basketball as examples that met that bar.

## Will any of it be added later?

Nothing has been announced. Rockstar hasn't said any of these features will come in a patch, an update or a future online mode, so for now they're cut, not coming soon.

## Official or not?

- **Official (developer interviews):** The cut features come from Rockstar North's Rob Nelson and Scott Mitchell speaking to PC Games, and the darts and bowling comments from Nelson speaking to VGC. These are interviews, not a Rockstar Newswire post. TheGamer and Dexerto report the PC Games interview in English.
- **Not announced:** There is no word from Rockstar about adding tennis, cinemas, crawling, furnishing or crafting later. Any claim that they're coming back is speculation.
