OpenAI has launched ChatGPT for Accounting, a package aimed at bookkeepers, accountants and finance teams. Dataconomy reports it went live on November 18. At its center is a free Accounting plugin that gives ChatGPT guidance for routine work such as processing invoices, reconciling bank transactions, building month-end reports and gathering audit support.

## How it works

The plugin connects ChatGPT to the accounting apps and files a team already uses. OpenAI lists partner plugins for Intuit QuickBooks, Xero, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle QuickStart, BILL, FreshBooks, Rillet and Campfire. For software without a plugin, OpenAI says ChatGPT can fall back on computer use to work inside the app. Users can also save templates, instructions and review checks as reusable skills and run them again on each month's records.

Typical tasks in OpenAI's help article include matching a bank statement against the ledger and listing duplicates or missing items separately, drafting invoice and receipt entries with suggested account codes, and filling an existing Excel report template while pointing to the workpapers behind each figure.

## Humans stay in the loop

OpenAI frames the tool as a first-pass assistant: ChatGPT prepares drafts and flags unclear details, and a person reviews the records, calculations and coding before any entry or report is approved. In Business workspaces members can install the plugin themselves, while Enterprise admins must approve it first. OpenAI says Business and Enterprise data is not used to train its models by default.

## Partners move too

Xero announced on November 19 that it is expanding its OpenAI integration so accountants can run month-end close reviews, client portfolio reviews and quarterly meeting prep inside ChatGPT for Accounting, using live Xero data. Xero says the feature is currently available in the US. Dataconomy notes the launch follows OpenAI's earlier ChatGPT for Sales package.
