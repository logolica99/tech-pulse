**Short answer: version 01.002.001.** GTA 6 has had two updates, both released before the November 19 launch. The newest, 01.002.001, started rolling out on November 16. Rockstar has not published patch notes for either update, so nobody outside Rockstar knows exactly what they changed.

## The two updates so far

| Update | Release | PS5 size | Xbox Series X\|S size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01.002.000 | November 13 | about 1.07GB | about 2.18GB |
| 01.002.001 | November 16 | about 602MB | about 1.6GB |

The sizes come from Red Bull and VICE, based on player reports and dataminers. Red Bull notes they are not confirmed by Rockstar.

Both updates rolled out in waves rather than to everyone at once. VICE says 01.002.001 reached PS5 owners in three waves 12 hours apart, citing a rollout schedule shared by dataminers, and was expected to reach all PS5 players by 2 AM PT on November 17. Red Bull says the first update, 01.002.000, was marked as mandatory on PS5, so you need it before you can play.

## Are there patch notes?

No. VICE and Red Bull both report that Rockstar has not published patch notes for 01.002.000 or 01.002.001. Red Bull says it is also unknown whether more launch patches are coming. Any list of "fixes" you see for these versions is a guess, not Rockstar's word.

## How to make sure you are up to date

- **PS5:** highlight GTA 6 on the home screen, press the Options button, and choose **Check for Update**, as VICE describes. If it says you are up to date but you are still on 01.002.000, VICE suggests checking again after the next rollout wave.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Red Bull recommends turning on automatic updates and checking your download queue if the patch has not shown up yet.
- **Storage:** leave some room beyond the game itself. Red Bull points out that patches take extra space on top of the roughly 212GB install.

## Official or not?

- **Official (Rockstar):** no patch notes have been published for either update.
- **Reported:** the version numbers, release dates, download sizes and rollout waves come from VICE, Red Bull, player reports and dataminers, not from Rockstar.
- **Unknown:** what either update changed, and when the next one will arrive.
